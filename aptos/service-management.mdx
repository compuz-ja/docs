Service management on a CompuZign platform rests on one property of the architecture: every configuration is declared in code.

## Change

Change flows through a GitOps pipeline. A change is a commit, a review and a sync, which makes it declarative, auditable and reversible. There is no console-only change to reconstruct afterwards.

That has a direct operational consequence. Because the repository is the source of truth, any component, or an entire site, can be rebuilt from it.

## Incident

Incidents are handled by the NOC and SOC with full-stack observability across all seven platforms. The value of running the disciplines together shows up here: there is no question of whose problem an incident is, because one partner operates the whole environment.

## Problem

Operational learning feeds back into the architecture. That is the fifth ATLAS discipline closing the loop on the first, and it is why the framework is a cycle rather than a sequence.

## Backup and disaster recovery

Managed and rehearsed continuously. Recovery is a procedure that has been performed, against the second site, under the same automation that runs production.

<Note>
  A disaster recovery plan that has never been executed is a document. The distinction this service draws is between rehearsed and documented.
</Note>
