APTOS is how a CompuZign platform is run and cared for after it is built. Go-live is the start of it, not the end.

## The operating envelope

Monitoring and observability across all seven platforms. Automation and orchestration that take human error out of routine work. NOC and SOC coverage with eyes on glass around the clock. Backup and disaster recovery that are managed and rehearsed continuously rather than tested once a year.

Continuity is operational as much as architectural. Failover procedures are rehearsed as part of the service, which is the difference between a procedure and a hope.
