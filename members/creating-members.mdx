Member profiles are the starting point for every loan in iLoan. Before you can open a loan application, the borrower must have a member record in your workspace. This page walks you through creating a new member, searching for existing members, editing profile details, and managing a member's status over time.

<Tip>
  Always search for a member before creating a new one. Duplicate records can cause confusion when linking loan applications and tracking KYC history. Use the search bar on the Members list to check whether a member already exists.
</Tip>

## Who can create members

You need the **members.create** capability to add new member profiles. This capability is granted to Credit Officers and all roles above. If you cannot see the **New Member** button, ask your Administrator to check your role assignment.

## Creating a new member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Member form">
    Go to **Members** in the main navigation, then click **New Member** in the top-right corner of the Members list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal details">
    Fill in the member's core identifying information:

    - **First name** and **last name** (required)
    - **Middle name** (optional)
    - **Date of birth** (required for KYC verification)
    - **Email address**
    - **Phone number**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter identity details">
    Fill in the member's government-issued identity numbers. These fields are **encrypted at rest** and are never exported in plain text:

    - **TRN** (Tax Registration Number) — used as the primary identifier for KYC verification
    - **Driver's licence number** — used as an alternative KYC identifier
    - **Passport number** — used as an additional identity reference
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter address details">
    Fill in the member's mailing address:

    - Address line 1 and address line 2
    - City
    - Parish
    - Postal code
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bank account details (optional)">
    If you have the member's bank account information, enter it now. This field is encrypted at rest. You can also add or update it later by editing the member's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the member">
    Click **Save**. iLoan creates the member record and opens the member's profile page. The member's KYC status will be **UNVERIFIED** until you run a verification check.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Identity fields — TRN, driver's licence number, passport number, and bank account number — are encrypted at rest as a compliance requirement. They cannot be exported or copied in plain text from iLoan. To view a stored value, use the **Reveal** function on the member's profile, which requires the appropriate capability and is recorded in the audit log.
</Warning>

## Searching for members

The Members list includes a search bar at the top of the page. As you type, iLoan searches member names using type-ahead matching — results update in real time. You can also filter by KYC status or member status using the filter controls alongside the search bar.

Click any result row to open that member's profile.

---

## Editing a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's profile">
    Find the member using search, then click their name to open their profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Click the **Edit** button in the top-right area of the profile header. You need the **members.edit** capability to see this button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the fields">
    Change any fields as needed — personal details, contact information, address, identity numbers, or bank account details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. The changes are applied immediately and recorded in the audit log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Member status

A member's status reflects the current state of their account with your credit union.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="ACTIVE — default status">
    New members are created with **ACTIVE** status. An active member is in good standing and can be linked to loan applications (once KYC is also VERIFIED).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="INACTIVE — account closed">
    Set a member to **INACTIVE** when their account has been formally closed. Their profile and all associated records — loans, documents, KYC history — are preserved for audit purposes. An inactive member cannot be linked to new loan applications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DECEASED — terminal status">
    The **DECEASED** status is a terminal state that signals the member has passed away. All records are fully preserved. This status cannot be reversed. Contact your Administrator before applying this status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Suspending a member">
    Suspending a member is an administrative action (requires the **members.suspend** capability) used when an account needs to be placed under review — for example, due to suspected fraud or a compliance hold. A suspended member's profile remains visible to staff but cannot be used for new loan origination until reactivated. The suspension reason is recorded in the member's status history.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Soft deletion

Deleting a member requires the **members.delete** capability, which is typically restricted to Administrators. When a member is deleted, they are hidden from the standard Members list and search results. Their record is not permanently erased — it is preserved in full for regulatory audit purposes and can be restored by an Administrator if needed.

If you need to remove a member from active use without deleting them, set their status to **INACTIVE** instead.
