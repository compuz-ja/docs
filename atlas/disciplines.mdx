Five disciplines govern every CompuZign engagement. They are not phases to complete once. They run as a continuous cycle: architecture informs technology selection, automation carries designs into production, and services feed operational learning back into the architecture.

## Architecture

Every engagement starts from a proven reference architecture, tailored to the institution rather than invented from a blank page. High availability, disaster recovery and security are designed in from the first diagram.

What this produces: the institution's own version of the reference design, sized and sequenced.

## Technology

CompuZign curates a technology stack and pre-validates it as a single platform, so integration risk is retired before the project begins rather than discovered during it.

What this produces: a bill of materials that has already been run together.

## Lifecycle

CompuZign owns assessment, design, build, migration, go-live, optimization and technology refresh, with a governance gate at every phase transition.

What this produces: a plan whose phases end in a decision rather than in a date.

## Automation

Delivery and operations are automated by default. GitOps pipelines, infrastructure as code, automated failover and orchestrated backup and recovery. Because every configuration is declared in code, any component, or an entire site, can be rebuilt from the repository.

What this produces: change that is declarative, auditable and reversible.

## Services

The platform is run as a service through the CompuZign NOC and SOC, under ITSM-aligned service management. Go-live is the beginning of the relationship, not the end of a project.

What this produces: an operating envelope, not a handover document.

<Note>
  The distinction between a delivery framework and a project plan is the cycle. A project plan finishes. These five keep running, and the last one feeds the first.
</Note>
