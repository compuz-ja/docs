An ATLAS engagement runs through six phases. Each one closes with a governance gate, which is a decision rather than a date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assess">
    Current-state discovery and a readiness baseline. What exists, what it costs to run, and what has to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Architect">
    The reference design tailored to the institution, sized, priced and sequenced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    Both sites stood up: infrastructure, cloud, data and security.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Migrate">
    Core banking data migration with a rehearsed cutover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operate">
    Managed services assume the environment and run it around the clock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optimize">
    Continuous improvement, analytics and capacity planning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Because the architecture, the automation and the runbooks already exist, engagements phase over quarters rather than years.

## The riskiest step

Migration and cutover. It is rehearsed repeatedly against the second site before the production event, under the same automation that will run the platform afterwards. The rehearsal and the live event are not two different procedures.
