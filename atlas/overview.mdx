ATLAS is the framework CompuZign delivers under. It is not a product, and there is nothing to buy, subscribe to or log into.

The name states the five disciplines that govern every engagement: **A**rchitecture, **T**echnology, **L**ifecycle, **A**utomation, **S**ervices. Together they form the CompuZign Enterprise Delivery Methodology.

## Why a framework at all

Core banking modernization fails at the seams. A typical programme selects a core vendor, an infrastructure vendor, a security vendor and an integrator, then asks a programme office to hold the boundaries between them together. Responsibility fragments across contracts. Architectural decisions get made locally and conflict globally. When something breaks at two in the morning, working out whose problem it is takes longer than the problem.

ATLAS exists so the seams are not there. The disciplines are designed together, delivered together and operated together, under one accountable partner.
