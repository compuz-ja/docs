ARGOS publishes sixty-six reports across nine domains. Every one is a governed row in the warehouse, which is why what a user may run, and the capability it required, is a matter of record.

## By domain

| Domain | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Regulatory** | DCFS and BOJ returns, FID threshold reporting, TAJ, FATCA and CRS |
| **Financial close** | Combined trial balance, statements, general ledger, budget variance |
| **IFRS 9 and credit risk** | ECL staging, provisioning, delinquency, vintage and roll-rate |
| **AML and CFT** | Screening outputs, alert disposition, CTR candidates, STR support |
| **Management and board** | Board pack, ALCO, KPI trends, branch scorecards, liquidity |
| **Operational** | Teller and branch operations, cash, reconciliation, exceptions |
| **Member analytics** | Segmentation, penetration, attrition, campaign lists, member 360 |
| **Channels and cards** | Digital adoption, card and ATM activity, disputes, switch volumes |
| **Governance** | Lineage, data quality, reconciliation certificates, requirements |
