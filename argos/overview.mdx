CompuZign ARGOS is the enterprise data warehouse for credit unions. It consolidates every core banking module into a single dimensional warehouse and publishes sixty-six governed reports from it.

Regulatory returns, IFRS 9 provisioning, AML surveillance, financial close and board reporting. All reconciled to the general ledger before anyone sees them.

## Isolation

Each institution's data is isolated by row-level security in the database, not by the application. The account the platform connects as holds neither `SUPERUSER` nor `BYPASSRLS`, and the application refuses to start if it does. A session with no institution context sees no rows at all.

Each institution's encrypted fields are protected by a separate key.

## The four editions

ARGOS is sold in four editions, and a report belongs to exactly one of them. The edition is the depth of the question a report answers, not the subject it covers.

| Edition | Tier | The question | Reports |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comply** | 1 | What happened? | 40 |
| **Understand** | 2 | Why did it happen? | 18 |
| **Anticipate** | 3 | What happens next? | 4 |
| **Decide** | 4 | What should we do? | 4 |
