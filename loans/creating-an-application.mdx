Creating a loan application in iLoan is a multi-step process. You work through a series of form sections at your own pace — saving progress along the way — and submit when everything is complete. Once submitted, the application is automatically routed to the Adjudication queue.

## Before you start

Make sure the following conditions are met before opening a new application:

- **Member KYC status is VERIFIED.** iLoan blocks submission for any member whose identity verification is not complete. If KYC is still pending, complete that process first.
- **You have the Credit Officer role.** You need permission to create and submit loan applications. Contact your system administrator if you're unsure about your role.

## Steps to create and submit an application

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go to Loans → New Loan Application">
    From the main navigation, select **Loans**, then click **New Loan Application** in the top-right corner of the loans list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for and select the member">
    Type the member's name, account number, or TRN into the member search field. Select the correct member from the results. The member's basic details will pre-fill the Applicant Information section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the loan type">
    Choose one of the four loan types: **Unsecured**, **Cash-Secured**, **Auto**, or **Mortgage**. The loan type determines which form sections appear. See [Loan Types](/loans/loan-types) for a full breakdown.

    <Note>
      The loan type is locked once the application is submitted. If you select the wrong type, you will need to abandon the draft and start a new application.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the loan product">
    Choose the loan product that applies to this application. Products are configured by your credit union and determine the eligible amount range, term limits, and applicable rate tables.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Applicant Information">
    Confirm or complete the applicant's personal details — name, date of birth, contact information, and address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Employment">
    Enter the applicant's employment details: employment type (employed, self-employed, retired, etc.), employer name, occupation, length of employment, and gross monthly income. iLoan uses this information to calculate risk and residual income.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Statement of Affairs">
    List the applicant's existing financial obligations — monthly debt payments, living expenses, and other outgoings. iLoan uses these figures to calculate the Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR) and residual income.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in Loan Purpose">
    Enter the loan amount requested, the purpose of the loan, and the requested term in months.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the collateral section (if applicable)">
    Depending on the loan type you selected, an additional section will appear:

    - **Cash-Secured:** Enter the savings account details and balance to be pledged as security.
    - **Auto:** Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, VIN, estimated valuation, and insurance details.
    - **Mortgage:** Enter the property address, estimated valuation, title reference, and insurance details.

    This section does not appear for Unsecured loans.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add guarantors (optional)">
    If the application requires or benefits from a guarantor, click **Add Guarantor** and fill in the guarantor's personal, employment, and income details. You can add more than one guarantor.

    <Tip>
      You can add or remove guarantors at any time while the application is in DRAFT status.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the auto-calculated figures">
    Before submitting, review the figures iLoan calculates automatically on the Review screen:

    - **Risk Band** (R1–R5) — overall risk score based on employment, income, debt, and loan type
    - **TDSR** — share of gross income going to all debt repayments
    - **LTV** — loan amount as a proportion of collateral value (secured loans only)
    - **Monthly payment** — estimated repayment amount
    - **Residual income** — take-home income remaining after all obligations

    These are estimates displayed during capture. The authoritative figures are confirmed at Adjudication.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Submit">
    When you're satisfied that all sections are complete and the figures look correct, click **Submit**. The application status moves from DRAFT to SUBMITTED, and is automatically placed in the Adjudication queue.
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the member's KYC status is not VERIFIED, the Submit button will be disabled. Complete KYC verification before attempting to submit.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Your loan is assigned a unique Loan ID (e.g. MDVL-UNS-00001) when you first save the draft. This ID appears in the application header and in all subsequent notifications.
</Note>

## Saving a draft

You do not need to complete all sections in a single session. Click **Save Draft** at any point to preserve your progress. The application remains in DRAFT status, and you can return to it from the Loans list at any time.

iLoan also auto-saves your progress as you move between form steps, so you won't lose work if you navigate away unexpectedly.

---

## Abandoning a draft

If you've started an application but decide not to proceed, use the **Abandon Draft** option from the application's action menu. The status changes to **DRAFT\_ABANDONED**, the record is preserved in the audit trail, and the application is removed from your active drafts.

An abandoned draft cannot be reopened or resubmitted. If you need to proceed with the same member, create a new application.

## Auto-calculated fields

When you submit an application, iLoan automatically calculates the following figures:

| Field | How it's calculated |
|---|---|
| **Risk Band (R1–R5)** | Scoring across five factors: employment type and stability, TDSR, LTV (if secured), loan type, and credit history. R1 is the lowest risk; R5 is the highest. |
| **TDSR** | (Total monthly debt payments) ÷ (Gross monthly income). Includes the proposed new loan repayment. |
| **LTV** | (Requested loan amount) ÷ (Collateral value). Applies to Cash-Secured, Auto, and Mortgage loans only. |
| **Monthly payment** | Calculated using the applicable rate from the loan product's rate table and the requested term. |
| **Residual income** | Gross monthly income minus all monthly obligations (existing debts + new loan payment + living expenses). |
| **Share requirement** | Mandatory shares purchase amount, calculated per your credit union's product configuration. |
| **Creditor life insurance premium** | Estimated premium based on the loan amount and term. |

These figures are calculated at the point of submission and stored as part of the risk assessment record. The Adjudicator reviews them when making a decision.
