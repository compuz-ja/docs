iLoan supports four loan types, each with specific form sections, collateral requirements, and rate calculation rules. Choosing the right loan type at the start of the application determines which sections the Credit Officer must complete, how iLoan calculates the risk assessment, and which review steps the loan goes through after approval.

---

## Unsecured

An Unsecured loan is a personal or consumer loan with no collateral backing. Eligibility is assessed entirely on the applicant's income, employment stability, credit history, and — where applicable — the strength of any guarantors.

**Form sections required:**
- Applicant Information
- Employment
- Statement of Affairs
- Loan Purpose
- Guarantors *(optional)*

**Risk assessment factors:**
- Employment type and stability
- Length of employment
- TDSR (Total Debt Service Ratio)
- Credit history

**No additional collateral section is required.**

**Typical use cases:** personal expenses, home improvements, education costs, medical expenses, debt consolidation.

---

## Cash-Secured

A Cash-Secured loan is backed by the applicant's savings or deposit account held at the credit union. Because the collateral is liquid and already under your credit union's control, these loans typically carry the lowest risk rating.

**Form sections required:**
- Applicant Information
- Employment
- Statement of Affairs
- Loan Purpose
- **Cash Collateral** *(savings account number, account balance to be pledged)*
- Guarantors *(optional)*

**Collateral rules:**
- Up to **95%** of the pledged savings balance is eligible as security.
- The pledged funds are held (not accessible to the member) for the life of the loan.
- LTV is calculated against the savings balance: (loan amount) ÷ (pledged savings balance).

**Risk assessment:** Because the collateral is liquid, Cash-Secured loans typically receive a Risk Band of **R1 or R2** — the lowest risk tiers — subject to the applicant's employment and income profile.

**Typical use cases:** members who want a lower interest rate by pledging savings they are not currently using.

---

## Auto

An Auto loan uses the financed vehicle as collateral. iLoan factors in the age of the vehicle in addition to the standard employment and income criteria when calculating risk, since older vehicles carry a higher depreciation risk.

**Form sections required:**
- Applicant Information
- Employment
- Statement of Affairs
- Loan Purpose
- **Vehicle Details** *(make, model, year, VIN, estimated valuation, insurance details)*
- Guarantors *(optional)*

**Collateral rules:**
- LTV is calculated against the vehicle valuation: (loan amount) ÷ (vehicle valuation).
- Vehicle insurance must be in place and assigned to the credit union before disbursement.
- The Securities review includes confirmation of vehicle title and insurance documentation.

**Risk assessment factors:**
- Employment type and stability
- TDSR
- LTV against vehicle valuation
- Vehicle age (older vehicles attract a higher risk factor)
- Credit history

**Typical use cases:** vehicle purchase loans, including new and used vehicles.

---

## Mortgage

A Mortgage loan is secured against real property. It carries the most extensive documentation and review requirements, including property valuation, title verification, and mortgage registration.

**Form sections required:**
- Applicant Information
- Employment
- Statement of Affairs
- Loan Purpose
- **Property Details** *(property address, estimated valuation, title reference, insurance details)*
- Guarantors *(optional)*

**Collateral rules:**
- LTV is calculated against the property valuation: (loan amount) ÷ (property valuation).
- Title documents must be reviewed and a mortgage registered against the property before disbursement.
- Property insurance assigned to the credit union is required.
- The Securities review covers valuation reports, title search, and mortgage registration paperwork.

**Risk assessment factors:**
- Employment type and stability
- TDSR
- LTV against property valuation
- Credit history

**Typical use cases:** home purchase, home equity release, land purchase, construction loans.

## Choosing the right loan type

| If the member has... | Recommended type |
|---|---|
| No collateral to offer | **Unsecured** |
| Savings they can pledge for a better rate | **Cash-Secured** |
| A vehicle to use as security | **Auto** |
| Property to use as security | **Mortgage** |

When in doubt, discuss the options with the member before opening the application. Selecting the wrong type means abandoning the draft and starting again.

<Note>
  The loan type is locked once the application is submitted. If the wrong type was selected, the application must be abandoned and a new one created with the correct loan type.
</Note>
