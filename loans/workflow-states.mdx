Every loan application in iLoan moves through a defined workflow. Each status tells you where the application is, who is responsible for it, and what happens next. Understanding these states helps you track applications across your team, respond quickly when action is needed, and know at a glance whether a loan is waiting on you or on another department.

## All workflow states

| State | Who is responsible | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **DRAFT** | Credit Officer | The application is being filled in. The officer can save progress and return at any time. No one else can see or act on a draft. |
| **SUBMITTED** | System | The officer submitted the application. iLoan automatically validates the form, calculates the risk assessment, and routes the application to the Adjudication queue. |
| **ADJUDICATION** | Adjudicator | The adjudicator reviews the application, the risk score, and supporting information, then makes a decision: Approve, Decline, or Request Information. |
| **INFO\_REQUESTED** | Credit Officer | The adjudicator needs more information before deciding. The officer opens the application, makes the required updates, and resubmits it for review. The risk assessment is recalculated on resubmission. |
| **APPROVED** | System | The adjudicator approved the application. For secured loans (Auto, Mortgage, Cash-Secured), the application is automatically routed to the Securities queue. For Unsecured loans, it routes directly to Disbursement. |
| **SECURITIES** | Securities Reviewer | The reviewer checks collateral documents — valuation reports, insurance certificates, title documents, or savings account details — and clears them for disbursement. |
| **SECURITIES\_ISSUE** | Credit Officer | The securities reviewer flagged an issue with the collateral documentation. The officer must upload the missing or corrected documents before the review can proceed. |
| **DISBURSEMENT** | Disbursement Officer | All collateral has been cleared. The disbursement officer schedules the payees and executes the release of funds. |
| **DISBURSED** | System | Funds have been released. Depending on your credit union's configuration, the loan may return to Securities for a final post-disbursement check before being filed. |
| **FILED** | — | **Terminal.** The loan record is locked. The audit trail is complete. No further edits or transitions are possible. |
| **DECLINED** | — | **Terminal.** The adjudicator declined the application. The record is sealed and preserved in the audit trail. |
| **DRAFT\_ABANDONED** | — | **Terminal.** The Credit Officer chose not to proceed with the draft and selected Abandon Draft. |
| **CANCELLED** | — | **Terminal.** A staff member with the appropriate permissions cancelled the application after submission. |

<Tip>
  If you're not sure what to do next on a loan, check the status badge — it tells you which department holds the ball.
</Tip>

## Section locking

Once a loan application moves past DRAFT status, all form sections become **read-only**. You can no longer edit the applicant information, employment details, statement of affairs, or collateral data while the loan is under review.

The only exceptions are states where iLoan explicitly unlocks specific sections for a targeted action:

- **INFO\_REQUESTED:** the officer can update the sections that the adjudicator identified as needing clarification, then resubmit.
- **SECURITIES\_ISSUE:** the officer can upload additional or corrected collateral documents.

All other sections remain locked throughout the loan's lifetime after submission. This protects the integrity of the record the adjudicator reviewed and ensures the audit trail reflects what was actually decided.

## Status badge colours

iLoan displays a coloured status badge on every loan record in the list view and on the application header. The colours provide a quick visual cue at a glance:

| Colour | States |
|---|---|
| **Grey** | DRAFT |
| **Blue** | SUBMITTED, ADJUDICATION, INFO\_REQUESTED |
| **Green** | APPROVED, SECURITIES, DISBURSEMENT |
| **Amber** | SECURITIES\_ISSUE |
| **Teal** | DISBURSED, FILED |
| **Red** | DECLINED, CANCELLED, DRAFT\_ABANDONED |

<Note>
  Loans are never deleted. Even DECLINED or CANCELLED applications remain in the system for the audit record. Staff with the appropriate read permissions can always locate and review the full history of any application.
</Note>
