iLoan uses role-based access control. Every user is assigned one or more roles within your credit union, and those roles determine which pages, queues, and actions are available to you. Roles are configured per credit union — the same person could hold a Credit Officer role in one workspace and an Adjudicator role in another.

<Note>
  Roles are assigned by your Tenant Administrator. If you need access to a feature that is not available to you, contact your administrator to discuss a role change.
</Note>

## Built-in roles

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit Officer" icon="file-pen">
    Captures new loan applications and manages the member relationship at intake. Can search, create, and edit member records, submit KYC information, add or remove guarantors, and submit completed applications for adjudication. Sees only their own loans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adjudicator" icon="scale-balanced">
    Reviews submitted loan applications and makes credit decisions. Can approve, decline, or request additional information. Has read access to all loans across all branches in the adjudication queue, can claim unassigned loans from the team queue, and can transition loans through workflow stages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Securities Reviewer" icon="shield-check">
    Reviews and resolves collateral documentation attached to a loan. Can raise security requests and review and fulfil them, ensuring all pledged assets are properly documented before disbursement proceeds. Can claim unassigned securities work from the team queue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Disbursement Officer" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Executes approved loans by scheduling and releasing funds. Manages the payee directory used to route payments. Can claim unassigned disbursement work from the team queue, and is responsible for confirming that disbursement instructions are complete and accurate before funds leave the credit union.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit Manager" icon="chart-line">
    Oversees lending operations across all branches. Can view all loans regardless of branch, assign or reassign loans to officers, manage the loan product catalogue, publish updated rate tables, and access analytics and reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branch Manager" icon="building-columns">
    Manages lending activity within their branch. Can view all loans in their branch, assign loans to officers within that branch, and view branch-level reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tenant Administrator" icon="gear">
    Full access to the workspace. Manages users, invitations, roles, branding, loan products, rate tables, and all tenant configuration. The only role that can invite new staff, suspend members, and change role assignments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How roles are assigned

Roles are chosen at the point of invitation. When a Tenant Administrator sends an invitation, they select the appropriate role before the email is sent. The new team member joins with that role active from their very first sign-in.

If someone's responsibilities change — for example, a Credit Officer is promoted to Credit Manager — an administrator can update the role at any time from **Admin → Members**. The change takes effect on the user's next page load.

A user can hold multiple roles simultaneously if your credit union requires it. For example, a senior officer might hold both Credit Officer and Branch Manager roles during a transition period. Contact your Tenant Administrator if you need a combined access arrangement.

## Capability stacking

Some actions in iLoan require more than one capability to line up before they succeed. This is by design — it enforces separation of duties and prevents a single role misconfiguration from opening up sensitive actions unexpectedly.

<Accordion title="Example: submitting a loan application">
  Submitting a loan for adjudication requires two things to be true simultaneously:

  1. **The acting user holds `loans.submit`** — granted to Credit Officers and Tenant Administrators.
  2. **The member's KYC has been submitted** — the `members.kyc.submit` capability must have been exercised on that member record before submission is allowed.

  If either condition is missing, the **Submit** button remains unavailable and iLoan shows a clear message about what still needs to be completed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Example: transitioning a loan through a workflow stage">
  Moving a loan from one stage to the next (for example, from *Adjudication* to *Securities Review*) requires:

  1. **`workflows.transition`** — the global workflow engine gate, held by Adjudicators.
  2. **`loans.transition`** — the loan-domain gate, ensuring the transition request is for a loan workflow and not another workflow type.

  Both must be present. This layered approach means that gaining access to one capability alone is never enough to perform a sensitive action.
</Accordion>

These stacking rules are enforced automatically — you do not need to think about them day to day. They are documented here so you understand why an action may be unavailable even when your role looks correct.
