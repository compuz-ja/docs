iLoan is a loan origination platform built specifically for credit unions. It gives your team a single, structured workspace to take a loan from first enquiry all the way through to disbursement and regulatory filing — with every action recorded automatically and every department seeing exactly what they need to see. Whether you are a Credit Officer capturing a new application, an Adjudicator reviewing risk, a Securities Reviewer confirming collateral, or a Credit Manager watching the pipeline, iLoan keeps the process moving and the record straight.

## Departments covered

iLoan is designed around the way credit union loan departments actually work. The following roles are built into the platform:

- **Credit Officer** — captures loan applications, manages member records, and submits applications for adjudication
- **Adjudicator** — reviews submitted applications, assesses risk scores and rates, and records approval, decline, or information-request decisions
- **Securities Reviewer** — confirms that collateral documentation is in order before a loan proceeds to disbursement
- **Disbursement Officer** — executes approved and secured loans and records disbursement instructions
- **Credit Manager** — monitors the full in-flight pipeline across all statuses and branches; read-only oversight view
- **Branch Manager** — views and tracks loans belonging to their assigned branch
- **Tenant Administrator** — manages users, role assignments, invitations, and tenant-level configuration

Each role has a tailored dashboard and can only perform the actions their role permits.

## How it works

iLoan moves a loan through four departments in sequence. At each step the appropriate team acts, and the loan advances automatically to the next stage.

1. **Capture** — A Credit Officer creates a member record, completes KYC verification, then fills out a loan application including employment details, income and expenses, and collateral information where applicable. The platform auto-calculates the applicant's TDSR, risk band, and indicative interest rate.
2. **Adjudication** — The application enters the Adjudication queue. An Adjudicator reviews the risk assessment, may request additional information from the Credit Officer, and ultimately records an approval or decline decision.
3. **Securities** — Approved applications move to the Securities queue. A Securities Reviewer confirms that all collateral is perfected (title transferred, property registered, or cash hypothecated). Any documentation gaps raise a securities issue, which must be resolved before the loan can proceed.
4. **Disbursement & Filing** — Once securities are clear, a Disbursement Officer schedules and executes the funds release. After disbursement is confirmed, the loan is filed and archived in a terminal state.

<Note>
  iLoan is a **multi-tenant** platform. Your credit union is a separate, fully isolated tenant — your members, loans, staff accounts, rate tables, and audit records are never visible to or shared with any other credit union on the platform. Your tenant has its own URL path (for example, `https://app.example.com/your-slug`), and every piece of data is scoped exclusively to your organisation.
</Note>
