## What replicates how

**FLL and MIA are the metro cluster pair.** They replicate synchronously, which is what delivers a Recovery Point Objective of zero: a transaction is acknowledged only once both sites hold it durably.

**Every other site pair replicates asynchronously.** That includes KIN to either Florida site. Asynchronous replication has a non-zero recovery point by definition, and any statement about zero data loss applies to the metro pair.

Configuration is replicated to an AWS S3 bucket over a site-to-site VPN, off the private backbone, so a rebuild does not depend on the backbone being up.

<Note>
  In-country residency for Jamaican institutions means primary processing at KIN. Which data leaves the island, and under what arrangement, is set per institution and is a contractual matter rather than a platform default.
</Note>
