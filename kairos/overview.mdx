KAIROS is the foundation every other CompuZign platform runs on. Enterprise compute, all-flash storage, an extended SAN fabric, virtualization and a cloud-native runtime, delivered as a subscription rather than as a capital project.

An institution gets enterprise-grade infrastructure economics without an enterprise-scale purchase.

## What it covers

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Compute and storage" icon="server">
    Enterprise x86 compute hosting virtualized and containerized workloads, on all-flash primary storage with local immutable snapshots.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fabric" icon="network">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Cloud runtime" icon="box">

  <Card title="Data services" icon="database">
    CloudNativePG running PostgreSQL as a Kubernetes-native three-instance cluster, with synchronous replication between the metro pair.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Resilience before applications

Every component is duplicated across sites, so the failure of a server, an array, a switch or a whole site is an operational event rather than a business event. That is engineered before a single application is deployed, not retrofitted afterwards.
