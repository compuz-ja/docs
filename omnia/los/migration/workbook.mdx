The migration workbook is the spreadsheet you fill in to import a book of loans at once. This page is the column reference: what each sheet holds, which columns are required, and how to write values so every loan reads cleanly.

A loan can have several guarantors, collateral items and disbursement payees, which one flat sheet cannot hold. So the workbook is **five linked sheets**.

| Sheet | Holds | One row is |
|---|---|---|
| `loans` | The loan, its decision values and its historical dates | One loan |
| `guarantors` | Guarantors standing for a loan | One guarantor on one loan |
| `collateral` | Security recorded against a loan | One collateral item |
| `payees` | Disbursement payees | One payee line |
| `fees` | Disbursement fee lines | One fee line |

Only `loans` is required. Add the other sheets only for loans that have guarantors, collateral, split payees or itemised fees.

<Tip>
  The upload dialog has two starter templates. **Download the loan template** gives a single sheet, one row per loan, which is all most books need. The **advanced template** gives all five sheets. Start from a template so the headers line up.
</Tip>

## How the sheets link

Every loan needs an `external_ref`: a reference that stays the same for that loan. Use the legacy paper loan number when you have one, or any stable key like `ROW-1`.

Every row in `guarantors`, `collateral`, `payees` and `fees` carries the `external_ref` of the loan it belongs to. That is the only link. A row whose `external_ref` matches no loan is flagged for review, never silently attached or dropped. The same `external_ref` on two loan rows errors both.

Headers ignore case, and spaces or hyphens count as underscores, so `External Ref` and `external_ref` are the same column. Unrecognised columns are ignored.

## Writing money

You choose **one scale** for the whole workbook when you upload, and every money column follows it.

| Scale | Write it as | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Dollars** (the usual choice) | A plain amount. A leading currency symbol, thousands commas and spaces are fine. | `$1,250,000.00` |
| **Cents** | A whole number of cents, no decimal point. | `125000000` |

Both examples mean one million two hundred and fifty thousand. In dollars, at most two decimal places are allowed: three or more is rejected as a likely scale mistake. Negative and empty amounts are always rejected. The dry-run shows every amount back in plain form so you can confirm the scale before you commit.

## Writing dates

Write dates day, month, year: `DD/MM/YYYY` or `DD-MM-YYYY`. `YYYY-MM-DD` is also accepted. An impossible date, like month 13, is rejected.

When both day and month are 12 or less, a date could be read either way: `03/04/2023` could be 3 April or 4 March. It is read as day-month (3 April) and the loan is marked **Needs review** so you confirm it. The dry-run shows every date back, like `14 Mar 2023`.

## Currency

One currency per workspace. Leave `currency` blank. A row naming a different currency is rejected rather than landing in the wrong one, and must be handled manually.

## Naming the borrower

Put the borrower's account number (the CIF) in `member_account_number`. It is matched to a member during review.

Without the account number, a unique full name works instead. A name that matches more than one member marks the loan **Needs review**, and you pick the right borrower from a list. Import your members first through [Bulk import](/omnia/los/members/bulk-import) so every loan has someone to match.

## Naming the officers

Officer columns, like `created_by_officer` and `adjudicated_by_officer`, take a full name, an email address or a user ID. A name is easiest and is matched to your staff automatically.

An officer who has left is still credited. Name them in the workbook, and during review they are recorded as a historical actor, with no login. See [Loan migration](/omnia/los/migration/overview#former-staff-attributed-without-a-login).

## Sheet: `loans`

Required columns:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `external_ref` | The loan's link key. |
| `member_account_number` | The borrower's account number (CIF). |
| `loan_type` | `UNSECURED`, `CASH_SECURED`, `AUTO` or `MORTGAGE`. |
| `target_status` | The stage the loan had reached. See [Stage values](#stage-values). |
| `requested_amount` | The principal. |
| `term_months` | The term in months. |
| `purpose` | Free text. |
| `origination_date` | When the loan was originated. Sets the reporting period. |
| `created_by_officer` | The Credit Officer who captured it. |

Optional columns:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `currency` | Leave blank. See [Currency](#currency). |
| `loan_product_ref` | Your product code, matched to a loan product. |
| `loan_type_def_ref` | Your loan type code. |
| `submitted_date` | The paper submission date. |
| `decided_date` | The approval decision date. |
| `scheduled_date` | When disbursement was scheduled. |
| `executed_date` | When funds were released. |
| `first_payment_date` | The first scheduled payment date. |
| `recommended_by_officer` | The officer who recommended it. |
| `adjudicated_by_officer` | The approver who decided it. |
| `approved_by_manager` | The manager who approved it. |
| `scheduled_by_officer` | The disbursement officer who scheduled it. |
| `executed_by_officer` | The disbursement officer who released it. |
| `risk_band` | `R1` to `R5`, stored as written. |
| `risk_score` | The historical risk score, as written. |
| `rate_bps` | The rate in basis points, for example `1450` for 14.50%. |
| `monthly_payment` | The actual monthly payment. |
| `adjudication_decision` | `APPROVED`, `DECLINED` or `INFO_REQUESTED`. |
| `adjudication_rationale` | The recorded reason for the decision. |
| `gross_amount` | The disbursement gross. |
| `total_fees` | Total fees. |
| `total_gct` | Total tax on fees (sales tax, for example GCT). |
| `total_deducted` | Total deducted from the gross. |
| `net_to_member` | The net amount paid out. The payee rows must add up to this. |
| `co_applicant_account_number` | A joint borrower's account number. |
| `refinanced_from_ref` | The `external_ref` of the loan this one refinances. |

## Stage values

Set `target_status` to the stage each loan had really reached:

`DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `ADJUDICATION`, `INFO_REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `DECLINED`, `SECURITIES`, `SECURITIES_ISSUE`, `DISBURSEMENT`, `DISBURSED`, `FILED`, `DRAFT_ABANDONED`, `CANCELLED`.

`ADJUDICATION` is the app's **Credit approval** stage. A later stage expects the dates and people that go with it: a `DISBURSED` loan wants its disbursement dates and officers, and payee rows that add up to `net_to_member`.

## Sheet: `guarantors`

One row per guarantor. Required: `external_ref`, `first_name`, `last_name` and `relationship` (to the borrower). Optional: `middle_name`, `date_of_birth`, `email`, `phone`, `address_line1`, `city`, `parish` and `account_number` (the guarantor's own member account, if any).

## Sheet: `collateral`

One row per item. Required: `external_ref`, `collateral_type`, `valued_amount`, `valuation_date` and `valuation_source` (who valued it). Optional: `details`, free text like a VIN or title number.

`collateral_type` is `VEHICLE`, `CASH_SHARES`, `CASH_HYPOTHECATION`, `CASH_EXTERNAL` (funds at another institution), `PROPERTY` or `OTHER`. Use `OTHER` for real security that fits none of the others. It is recorded without affecting any figure.

## Sheet: `payees`

One row per payee. Required: `external_ref`, `sequence_no` (order within the disbursement), `payee_name`, `purpose`, `amount` and `method`. Optional: `payee_type` (defaults to `MEMBER`), `bank_name` and `account_type`. Payee amounts must add up to the loan's `net_to_member`.

| Column | Allowed values |
|---|---|
| `method` | `ACH`, `CHECK`, `INTERNAL_TRANSFER`, `WIRE`, `RTGS`, `MANAGERS_CHEQUE` |
| `payee_type` | `MEMBER`, `THIRD_PARTY`, `GOVERNMENT`, `INSURER`, `ATTORNEY`, `LENDER_PAYOFF`, `OTHER` |

## Sheet: `fees`

One row per fee. Required: `external_ref`, `sequence_no`, `code` (like `PROCESSING` or `LEGAL`), `label`, `base_amount`, `amount`, `treatment` and `remit_to`. Optional: `taxable` (`true` or `false`, also `yes` or `no`, `1` or `0`), `gct_rate_bps` (tax rate in basis points), `gct_amount` (tax amount), `source` and `note`.

| Column | Allowed values |
|---|---|
| `treatment` | `DEDUCT` (deducted from the loan), `PAID_SEPARATELY`, `CAPITALIZE` (added to the loan) |
| `remit_to` | `CU` (your credit union), `TAJ` (the tax authority), `GOVERNMENT`, `INSURER`, `ATTORNEY`, `LENDER`, `OTHER` |
| `source` | `SCHEDULE`, `MANUAL` |

## What each loan comes back as

- **Ready**: no errors and nothing to confirm. Safe to commit.
- **Needs review**: no errors, but something to confirm, like an ambiguous date or a borrower to match.
- **Errored**: a cell problem on the loan or one of its linked rows.

An errored loan never fails the batch. Errored rows go into a downloadable report with the reason on each. Fix them and upload them as a new batch.

## Worked example

One disbursed vehicle loan, linked across sheets by `external_ref` `LEGACY-000123`, in the dollars scale with day-month-year dates. All names and figures are illustrative.

**`loans`**

| external_ref | member_account_number | loan_type | target_status | requested_amount | term_months | purpose | origination_date | executed_date | created_by_officer | adjudicated_by_officer | rate_bps | net_to_member |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEGACY-000123 | ACC-000123 | AUTO | DISBURSED | $1,250,000.00 | 48 | Motor vehicle purchase | 14/03/2021 | 28/03/2021 | J. Officer | A. Adjudicator | 1450 | $1,200,000.00 |

**`guarantors`**

| external_ref | first_name | last_name | relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEGACY-000123 | Gina | Guarantor | Sister |

**`collateral`**

| external_ref | collateral_type | valued_amount | valuation_date | valuation_source | details |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEGACY-000123 | VEHICLE | $2,000,000.00 | 13/03/2021 | Dealer appraisal | VIN ABC123 |

**`payees`**

| external_ref | sequence_no | payee_name | purpose | amount | method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEGACY-000123 | 1 | Dealer Ltd | Vehicle | $1,200,000.00 | CHECK |

This loan comes back **Ready**, with the guarantor, collateral and payee linked under it. The dry-run shows `1,250,000.00` and `14 Mar 2021` for you to confirm.

## Saving the file

Save each sheet as CSV and upload one CSV per sheet. Or, for the full workbook, upload one text file with each sheet introduced by a marker line like `===SHEET:loans===`, running to the next marker. A workbook holds up to 2,000 loans and 5 MB. Split a larger book into separate batches.

When the file is ready, go to [Loan migration](/omnia/los/migration/overview) to upload, review and commit it.
