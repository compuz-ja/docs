Legal documents are the forms your credit union puts in front of a member and their guarantors to make a loan enforceable, and the loan cannot move on until the ones that matter are signed and on file.

They are your credit union's own forms. Your Administrator or Credit Manager registers each form once as a template, and from then on the system fills it in for every loan. See [Document templates](/omnia/los/legal-documents/templates).

<Note>
  These legal documents are separate from the **loan agreement**, the contract the system generates automatically after approval. See [Loan agreements](/omnia/los/loans/loan-agreements).
</Note>

## The forms a loan needs

The system works out which documents a loan needs from its type, its guarantors, and its net disbursement. You never pick from a list. The **Legal documents & contracts** card on the loan shows one row per form, with whose copy it is.

| Form | When a loan needs it | Whose copy | Must be signed before |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal Credit Consent** | Always. | The borrower, plus one per guarantor. | The loan leaves Securities. |
| **Guarantee & Indemnity** | One per guarantor. | Each guarantor. | The loan leaves Securities. |
| **NSIPP Security Agreement** (security over the vehicle) | Auto loans. | The borrower. | The loan leaves Securities. |
| **Mortgage Deed** | Mortgage loans. | The borrower. | The loan leaves Securities. |
| **Insurance Assignment** | Mortgage loans. | The borrower. | The loan leaves Securities. |
| **Declaration of Purpose** | The net to member is below the declaration threshold. | The borrower. | Funds are released. |
| **Loan Disbursement Sheet** | Always. | The loan, signed by staff. | Funds are released. |

A loan with two guarantors shows three consent forms, two guarantees, and the disbursement sheet, plus whatever its loan type adds. Remove a guarantor and their rows drop off.

The **declaration threshold** is $1,000,000 in the standard setup. A net of exactly that amount needs no Declaration. The net comes from the scheduled disbursement, so the Declaration row appears only once the Disbursement team has scheduled the payment.

### Optional documents per loan type

An Administrator or Credit Manager can offer extra forms for a loan type, under **Required documents** on the loan type's edit page, each set to hold the loan at Securities or at Disbursement. An optional form shows on the loan with a **Require for this loan** checkbox and holds nothing up until someone ticks it. Once ticked, the loan waits at that stage until the form is signed. Ticking the box needs the **Prepare documents** permission. See [Loan types](/omnia/los/admin/loan-types#documents-attached-to-a-loan-type).

### Additional forms

Forms registered as an **Additional form** sit on their own **Additional forms** card. They fill from the loan with one click and never hold a loan up.

## Prepare, then execute

Every legal document goes through two steps. **Prepare** produces the PDF from the loan's figures. **Execute** files the signed copy, either an uploaded wet-signed scan or an electronic signature.

<Frame caption="Each row names the form, whose copy it is, and where it stands. The buttons on the right change with the status.">
  <img src="/images/omnia/los/documents/legal-documents-panel.png" alt="The Legal documents and contracts card on a loan, listing consent forms, a guarantee and the disbursement sheet, some prepared and some executed" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the document">
    Click **Prepare** on the form's row. The system fills your template with the loan's data and the row moves to **Prepared, awaiting signature**.

    On the Declaration of Purpose the button reads **Itemize & prepare**. List each purpose and its amount. **Prepare Declaration** stays disabled until the amounts add up to the gross loan exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it for signature">
    Use **View**, **Print**, or **Download** to put the PDF in front of the signers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the signed copy">
    For a wet signature, click **Upload signed**, fill in the signing details the form asks for, drop the scan (PDF, PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 10 MB), and click **Mark executed**. For an electronic signature, click **Sign electronically**. See [Signing](/omnia/los/legal-documents/signing). Either way the row moves to **Executed** and **View** becomes **View signed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Signing details each form records

The upload refuses to mark a document executed without the details your signing rules need.

| Form | Recorded with the signed scan |
|---|---|
| Personal Credit Consent, Guarantee & Indemnity | **Witness name**, required. |
| NSIPP Security Agreement | **Justice of the Peace (JP) name**, required. JP registration, witness name, and a registration reference, optional. |
| Mortgage Deed, Insurance Assignment | **JP name**, required. JP registration and witness name, optional. |
| Loan Disbursement Sheet | **Checked by**, required. See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet). |

### Row statuses

- **Required**: not prepared yet.
- **Optional** or **Required for this loan**: an optional form from the loan type, unticked or ticked.
- **Prepared, awaiting signature**: the PDF exists and is waiting for a signature.
- **Executed**: the signed copy is on file.
- **Template not configured**: your credit union has no active template for this form, so there is nothing to prepare and it holds nothing up.
- **Signed copy voided, prepare again**: a supervisor voided the signed copy. Prepare it again.

## The paperwork gates the workflow

Two moves wait for the right documents. The check runs when the move is attempted, so a stale page cannot get around it.

| Move | Must be executed first |
|---|---|
| **Securities to Disbursement** | Every consent and guarantee, plus the security documents for the loan type: the NSIPP Security Agreement on an auto loan, the Mortgage Deed and Insurance Assignment on a mortgage. Plus any optional form ticked to hold at Securities. |
| **Disbursement to Disbursed** | The Loan Disbursement Sheet, the Declaration of Purpose when required, and any optional form ticked to hold at Disbursement. |

When a move is refused, the message names the first outstanding document and whose copy it is, says how many others are outstanding, and reminds you that prepared is not executed. It also says who can execute it: a Credit Officer, or the Securities or Disbursement team.

<Note>
  **The hand-off to Disbursement is automatic.** When the last document the Securities stage is waiting on is executed, and no security request is open, the loan moves to **Disbursement** in the same step and the Disbursement team is told. See [Securities](/omnia/los/workflows/securities#how-the-loan-reaches-disbursement).
</Note>

A form only holds a loan up when your credit union has an active template for it. The **Personal Credit Consent** and the **Loan Disbursement Sheet** always do: every workspace starts with a template for each, and the last active one of either cannot be archived.

## Correcting a document

An unsigned document with an error is **re-prepared**. A signed one is **voided** by a supervisor and prepared again. See [Correcting documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/correcting-documents).

Documents also tidy themselves up when the parties change. If a guarantor is removed after their documents were prepared, and the loan is later sent back to draft or resubmitted, those documents are marked superseded. They stay on file but no longer count.

Preparing the same loan from the same template always produces an identical document, so an auditor can reproduce exactly what a member signed.

## Who can do what

| Action | Permission | Built-in roles |
|---|---|---|
| **Prepare** or **Re-prepare** | **Prepare documents** | Credit Officer, Securities, Disbursement, Administrator |
| **Upload signed** or **Sign electronically** | **Record signed documents** | Credit Officer, Securities, Disbursement, Administrator |
| **View**, **Print**, **Download** | **View documents** | Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement, Credit Manager, Branch Manager, Administrator |
| **Void** a signed document | **Void signed documents** | Adjudicator, Credit Manager, Administrator |

The person who executed a document can never void it. Every prepare, signature, supersession, and void is recorded in the audit trail with who did it and when, and a void or supersession keeps its reason. See [Roles and permissions](/omnia/los/access/roles-and-permissions).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no Declaration of Purpose row on my loan?">
    Either the net to member is at or above the declaration threshold, or the disbursement is not scheduled yet. The row appears once Disbursement schedules the payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The loan is approved but still sits in Securities. Why?">
    A document the Securities stage needs is not executed yet. On an unsecured loan, check the borrower's Personal Credit Consent first. The loan moves to Disbursement by itself as soon as the last one is executed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can an Adjudicator execute a document?">
    No. The Adjudicator, Credit Manager, and Branch Manager can view documents but not prepare or execute them. That is the job of the stage teams.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
