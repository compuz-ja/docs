The Loan Disbursement Sheet is the internal posting record for a loan's money, and the loan cannot be marked disbursed until a signed copy is on file.

Every loan needs one. The member never signs it. It carries three staff sign-off lines, **Prepared by**, **Disbursed by**, and **Checked by**, and the checker can never be the officer who prepared it.

## What the sheet shows

**The heading.** Your logo prints at the top, with your credit union's legal name above the title. The logo must be a PNG or JPEG. Any other format, or no logo, gives a text heading instead. With no legal name set, the workspace name is used. See [Workspace setup](/omnia/los/admin/workspace-setup#updating-branding).

**The loan details.**

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Borrower** | The member's full name. |
| **Member account** | The member's Core Banking System account number, masked to its last five digits, for example `****48213`. |
| **Loan reference** | The loan ID. |
| **Holding account** | The masked member account followed by your credit union's holding-account suffix. |
| **Loan suffix** | The suffix for the loan type. |
| **Posting reference** | The disbursement's posting reference. |
| **Gross amount** | The gross loan amount. |
| **Date** | The date the sheet was prepared. |

**The General Ledger posting.** A double-entry table debits the loan account with the gross amount and credits the holding account with the net to member, with one row per General Ledger account your fees post to, and a tax payable row when tax applies. The Debit and Credit columns are totalled and always balance. Each fee's General Ledger account is set on the [Fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule).

**The sign-off.** The **Prepared by**, **Disbursed by**, and **Checked by** lines, each with its own **Date**.

**The footer.** Every page carries your workspace's document short name followed by **HORIZON: Loan Origination System**, for example `Meridian HORIZON: Loan Origination System`.

**The prepared-by stamp.** Under the footer, every page says who prepared the sheet and when, in local time, for example `Prepared in HORIZON by Jane Brown on 26 Oct 2026 at 2:15 PM`. The name and time are fixed when the sheet is prepared, so a reprint never changes them.

## The member account stays masked

The full account number never reaches the sheet. Only the last five digits are printed, and the full number is not kept in the PDF, in the record of what was printed, or in the audit trail.

A member with no Core Banking System account number on file prints `** NOT ON FILE - OBTAIN FROM CORE BANKING **` in both account fields, so the gap cannot be missed. Add the number to the member's record and re-prepare the sheet.

No other form can print the member's core banking account.

## Preparing the sheet

Prepare the sheet after the disbursement is scheduled, because its figures and General Ledger rows come from the scheduled disbursement. A sheet prepared earlier shows the requested amount as both gross and net.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the loan">
    The sheet is on the loan's **Legal documents & contracts** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Prepare">
    The row moves to **Prepared, awaiting signature**. You need the **Prepare documents** permission, which the Credit Officer, Securities, Disbursement, and Administrator roles hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print it for signature">
    Use **Print** or **Download** and collect the three signatures.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the figures change before the sheet is signed, click **Re-prepare**. See [Correcting documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/correcting-documents).

## Recording the signed sheet

There are two ways to record the checker's signature.

**On screen.** A second officer who did not prepare the sheet clicks **Sign electronically** and signs the **Checked by** line. See [Signing](/omnia/los/legal-documents/signing#an-officer-signs-the-disbursement-sheet-as-checker).

**On paper.** Any officer with the **Record signed documents** permission can upload the wet-signed scan, including the officer who prepared it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Upload signed">
    On the sheet's row, click **Upload signed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Checked by">
    Pick the officer whose signature is on the **Checked by** line. The list holds current officers whose role can check a sheet, and leaves out the preparer. If you can check it yourself, you are already selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop the scan and mark it executed">
    Drop the scan and click **Mark executed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The checker rules

The checker is checked again when you click **Mark executed**, whatever the page sent.

- A checker must be named. An upload without one is refused.
- The checker cannot be the officer who prepared the sheet. The refusal reads "The checker must be different from the person who prepared or disbursed (maker ≠ checker)."
- The checker must be an active staff member with the **Record signed documents**, **Approve loans**, or **Approve disbursements** permission. Among the built-in roles that is the Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement, Credit Manager, and Administrator. Anyone else is refused with "The officer named as checker cannot check this sheet."

The audit trail records who signed as checker and whether that was the person who uploaded the scan.

This separation of duties on the document sits alongside the separate second approval on the money itself, described in [Disbursement controls](/omnia/los/disbursement/controls).

## The sheet holds the release

**Mark disbursed** is refused while the Loan Disbursement Sheet is unsigned, even from a stale page. A supervisor can still void a signed sheet before the loan is disbursed. After that it is permanent.

## Previewing the template

An Administrator or Credit Manager can preview the sheet from **Document Templates**. The preview uses sample data and a made-up account number, never a real member, and shows your branding and a sample prepared-by stamp.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does the member account show only five digits?">
    The sheet is printed and handled on paper. Five digits are enough to post against the right account without the full number travelling with the sheet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I prepared the sheet. Can I upload the signed copy?">
    Yes. Name the officer who signed as checker. You cannot name yourself, and you cannot sign electronically as checker.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my logo missing from the sheet?">
    The sheet prints PNG and JPEG logos only. Upload your logo in one of those formats under **Settings → Branding**, then re-prepare any unsigned sheet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the footer name?">
    Yes. Set the **Short name** under **Settings → Branding**, up to 20 characters. Sheets already prepared keep the name they were printed with.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
