A legal document is signed on paper and uploaded as a scan, or signed electronically on screen, and either way it lands as **Executed** on the loan.

Electronic signing is always optional. You can print, collect a wet signature, and upload the scan instead. See [Legal documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/overview) for the prepare-then-execute steps.

## Who signs what

Electronic signing covers two signers: the **borrower**, on their own acknowledgement forms, and a **staff officer**, as checker on the Loan Disbursement Sheet. Guarantors and witnessed deeds always sign on paper.

| Document | Borrower e-sign | Officer e-sign | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal Credit Consent** (borrower's copy) | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Personal Credit Consent** (a guarantor's copy) | No | No | Yes |
| **Declaration of Purpose** | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Guarantee & Indemnity** | No | No | Yes, with a witness |
| **NSIPP Security Agreement** | No | No | Yes, with a Justice of the Peace |
| **Mortgage Deed**, **Insurance Assignment** | No | No | Yes, with a Justice of the Peace |
| **Loan Disbursement Sheet** | Never signed by the member | Yes, as checker | Yes, naming the checker |
| **Draft loan agreement** | Yes, to accept the proposed terms | No | Not applicable |

Asking for an electronic signature on anything else is refused with "This document must be printed and wet-signed; it cannot be signed electronically." On a guarantor's copy it is refused with "Electronic signing is available for the borrower only in this version."

## The borrower signs in the branch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the document">
    On the **Legal documents & contracts** card, click **Prepare** on the borrower's consent, or **Itemize & prepare** on the Declaration of Purpose. The row moves to **Prepared, awaiting signature**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign electronically">
    A signature panel opens on the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The member signs">
    The member can **draw** a signature on the pad, **type** their name and pick a style, or **upload** a photo of their signature. A preview shows exactly what will go on the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and finish">
    The member ticks the box to confirm they have read the document, agree to be bound by it, and consent to signing electronically. Click **Sign electronically**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The document moves straight to **Executed**, with the signature on the member's own signature line. If it was the last document the Securities stage was waiting on, the loan moves to Disbursement in the same step.

## The borrower accepts the terms before approval

You can generate a **draft loan agreement** while the loan is still being worked. It carries a **DRAFT, PROPOSED TERMS, NOT YET APPROVED** watermark and is priced the way approval will price it. The member reads it and e-signs to accept the terms on offer.

Accepting the draft does not approve the loan or make a binding contract. After approval the system generates the real [loan agreement](/omnia/los/loans/loan-agreements) from the approved figures. Generating the draft again replaces the earlier one, so the member always accepts the version they were shown.

A product priced by its security needs that security captured first. Without it you see "Add the security details (the cash account or deposit pledged) before generating the draft agreement", or, for a product priced by vehicle age, a prompt to check the vehicle's year of manufacture.

### Signing the draft remotely

When the member is not in the branch, click **Email signing link** on the draft loan agreement card. The member opens a single-use link, enters a one-time code sent to the same email address, reviews the draft, and signs on their own device.

- The member's identity check must be **Verified**.
- The member needs an email address on file. No phone number is needed.
- The link expires after **72 hours**. The code expires after **10 minutes** and allows five attempts.

<Note>
  Remote signing covers the draft loan agreement only. Consent forms and the Declaration of Purpose are signed in the branch or on paper. The link, code, and receipt emails go out only when signing emails are turned on for your workspace. If members are not receiving them, ask your LOS support team.
</Note>

## An officer signs the Disbursement Sheet as checker

The Loan Disbursement Sheet has three sign-off lines: **Prepared by**, **Disbursed by**, and **Checked by**. A second officer can sign the **Checked by** line on screen.

The officer who prepared the sheet cannot. For them **Sign electronically** is hidden, and the row explains that a different officer must check it. Any other officer with the **Record signed documents** permission sees the button, signs, and ticks a statement that they reviewed the sheet and it is correct. No one-time code is needed, because the officer is already signed in.

If the sheet is wet-signed, anyone with **Record signed documents**, including the preparer, can upload the scan, naming the **Checked by** officer whose signature is on the page. See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet#recording-the-signed-sheet).

## Signing on paper

Click **Upload signed**, enter the signing details the form needs, drop the scan, and click **Mark executed**.

| Form | Required on upload |
|---|---|
| Personal Credit Consent, Guarantee & Indemnity | Witness name |
| NSIPP Security Agreement, Mortgage Deed, Insurance Assignment | Justice of the Peace name |
| Loan Disbursement Sheet | Checked by |

The scan can be a PDF, PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 10 MB. Each scan is checked to be the file type it claims and virus-scanned before it is stored.

## What gets kept

- The signature goes into a new signed PDF. The prepared PDF is never changed. Both are kept for seven years in storage that cannot be altered or deleted.
- A tamper-evident **signed receipt** records the exact statement the signer agreed to, who signed, how (in the branch or remotely), and when.
- The audit trail records that a signature happened and how, never the member's name or signature image.
- When signing emails are on, the member receives a confirmation email in your credit union's branding.

## Who can do what

| Action | Permission | Built-in roles |
|---|---|---|
| Record an electronic or wet signature | **Record signed documents** | Credit Officer, Securities, Disbursement, Administrator |
| Offer **Sign electronically** to a member, or send a remote signing link | **Request e-signatures** | Credit Officer, Securities, Disbursement, Administrator |
| See whether a member has signed | Every loan role | Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement, Credit Manager, Branch Manager, Administrator |
