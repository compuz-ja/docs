Four controls can sit around the moment funds are released: a second approver, AML and sanctions screening, a personal limit per officer, and automatic release of small disbursements. Each is off until your credit union turns it on, so a workspace that sets nothing keeps the simple schedule-then-release path.

You see the controls on the **Controls & settlement** card of the disbursement page.

## Second approver (maker-checker)

A disbursement whose net to member is at or above your credit union's dual-control threshold needs a second person to approve it before it can be released.

When you schedule one, the card's **Approval** badge shows **Pending** and reads "Awaiting a maker-checker approval before the funds can be released." Until someone approves it, **Mark disbursed** is refused.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the disbursement">
    The approver opens the loan from the Disbursement queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a rationale">
    On the **Controls & settlement** card, type the reason for the decision. It is required and goes on the audit trail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject">
    Click **Approve release** or **Reject**. An approved disbursement can then be released. A rejected one cannot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The approver needs the **Approve disbursements** permission, which only the **Credit Manager** role holds. The Disbursement team makes the disbursement and a Credit Manager checks it.

The approver must be a different person from the officer who scheduled it. If you scheduled it, the card tells you a second officer must approve, and the system refuses your approval even if you hold the permission. Maker is never checker.

Below the threshold, the badge reads **Not required** and no second approval is needed.

The signed paperwork has its own, separate check: the **Checked by** signer on the Loan Disbursement Sheet cannot be the officer who prepared it. See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet#the-checker-rules).

## AML and sanctions screening

Screening checks every payee against sanctions and politically exposed person lists before money moves.

Click **Screen payees** on the card while the disbursement is still scheduled. The **AML screening** badge shows the result for the disbursement as a whole:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not screened** | No screen has run yet. |
| **Clear** | No payee matched. Release can go ahead. |
| **Review** | A payee possibly matched. A person must decide. |
| **Blocked** | A payee matched. Release is refused. |

The worst payee decides the result: one blocked payee blocks the whole disbursement. If you change payees or want a fresh check, click **Re-screen payees**. Every screen is kept.

When your credit union requires screening, **Mark disbursed** is refused unless the badge reads **Clear**. When it does not, screening is available but optional. Screening needs the **Screen disbursements** permission, which the **Disbursement** role holds.

## Per-officer limits

Your credit union can give any officer a personal ceiling on a single disbursement. When you try to release a net above your ceiling, **Mark disbursed** is refused and an officer with a higher ceiling, or none, must release it.

An administrator sees each officer's ceiling on **Administration → Users & Roles**, in the hover card on the user's row, as **Disb. ceiling** or **No personal limit**. It is a ceiling on one release, not an approval limit.

## Automatic release

Your credit union can choose to release small disbursements the moment they are scheduled. It is off by default.

When it is on, clicking **Schedule disbursement** also releases the funds, with no separate **Mark disbursed** step, but only when all of these hold:

- The net to member is at or below the automatic-release threshold.
- No second approval is needed.
- The officer is within their personal limit.
- Your credit union does not require AML screening. Screening runs after scheduling, so a workspace that requires it always releases by hand.

Anything that fails a condition waits for **Mark disbursed** as usual. An automatic release is recorded in the audit trail like any other.

## Setting the controls

The dual-control threshold, the screening requirement, officer ceilings, and automatic release are set for your credit union by your LOS support team. There is no screen to change them in the app yet. The **Configure disbursement controls** permission, held by the **Credit Manager**, is reserved for that screen when it arrives.
