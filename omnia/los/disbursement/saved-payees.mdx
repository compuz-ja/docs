**Saved Payees** is your credit union's directory of payees you pay over and over, so nobody re-types an attorney's or a dealer's bank details on every loan. It stores the payee's name, type, default payment method, and bank routing. It never stores an account number.

## Where to find it

Open **Saved Payees** under **Administration** in the sidebar. You need the **Manage saved payees** permission, which the **Disbursement** and **Credit Manager** roles hold.

## Add a payee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New payee">
    On the **Saved Payees** page, click **New payee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter the payee's name, pick a **Type**, and optionally a **Default method**. For a bank payment, pick the **Bank** to fill its name and SWIFT/BIC, then add the bank code, branch transit code, routing number, and **Account type** as needed. For cheques, add a mailing address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save payee**. The payee now appears in the **Pick saved payee** list on every disbursement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also save a payee while you schedule a disbursement: fill in the payee row and click **Save to directory**. That needs only the **Disburse loans** permission.

## Payee types

| Type | Who it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | The member themselves. |
| **Loan payoff** | Another lender whose loan to the member is being paid off. |
| **Attorney** | An attorney, for example for title transfer. |
| **Insurer** | An insurance company. |
| **Government** | A government office, for example for registration fees. |
| **Third party** | Anyone else paid on the member's behalf, such as a car dealer. New payees start as this type. |
| **Other** | Anything that fits none of the above. |

## Edit, deactivate, and restore

- **Edit** a payee to change any of its details.
- **Deactivate** hides a payee from the picker but keeps its record. You confirm before it happens.
- **Restore** brings a deactivated payee back.

Search the directory by name and filter it with **All**, **Active**, or **Inactive**. Use **Inactive** to find a payee to restore.

No two active payees can share a name, ignoring capitals, so the picker is never ambiguous. A new payee or a rename that would clash with an active payee is refused.

Every create, edit, deactivation, and restore is recorded in the audit trail.

## The account number is never saved

Picking a saved payee on a disbursement fills in the routing details only. You still type the account number for that payment. It is encrypted when saved and stays with that one disbursement.

<Warning>
A saved payee never holds an account number. Check and enter it on every disbursement.
</Warning>

## Paying off a loan at another lender

A **Loan payoff** payee settles a loan the member holds at another institution. Choose **Loan payoff** as the payee type and enter the other lender's loan reference in the field that appears.

A payoff is one slice of the net to member, like any other payee. It is paid to the other lender by its payment method, and the member receives what remains. The payees together must still add up to the net to member exactly.
