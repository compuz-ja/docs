Fee, tax, and deduction lines take the approved loan amount down to the **Net to member**, the figure your payees must add up to exactly.

## From gross to net

The disbursement screen works it out in this order:

1. Start from the **Gross approved** amount, the loan amount on the latest approval decision.
2. Work out each fee line: a percentage of the gross, a rate per thousand of the gross, a fixed amount, or a statutory duty formula (stamp duty, for example).
3. Add sales tax to every line marked taxable.
4. Subtract every line, tax included, that is **Deducted from the loan** and not waived.
5. What is left is the **Net to member**.

The panel shows the running total as **gross less deducted**, and the net updates as you change any line. If the deductions ever exceed the gross, the screen says so and **Schedule disbursement** is refused until you reduce or waive a fee.

## How each line is treated

Every fee line carries a **Treatment**. Only the first one changes the cash the member receives.

| Treatment | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Deducted from the loan** | Taken from the loan proceeds, so the member receives less cash. |
| **Charged to the member's account** | Collected from the member's account outside the loan. The proceeds are unchanged. |
| **Added to the loan** | Added to the principal, so the member repays it over the term. |

Each line also records who the money is owed to: **CU income**, **Tax authority**, **Government**, **Insurer**, **Attorney**, **Other lender**, or **Other**. And each line posts to a General Ledger account, which starts on the fee's default and can be changed per line. Your Administrator or Credit Manager sets up the accounts on the [Fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule).

## Tax on fees

Sales tax on fees (for example GCT) applies only to lines marked taxable. Your credit union marks which fees are taxable on the fee schedule. For example, a processing fee might carry tax like a legal or valuation fee, while an insurance premium carries none.

The workspace tax rate is set for your credit union, for example 15%. A Disbursement officer can change the rate for one disbursement in the tax rate box at the top of the fees panel, for a special or zero-rated case. The change applies to that disbursement only and leaves the workspace rate alone.

## Where the starting figures come from

Each fee line opens on the most specific figure available:

1. **The amount agreed at capture.** While the loan is a draft, the Credit Officer can replace a fee's rate or amount for that loan, for example a 3% processing fee instead of the 2% on the schedule. They click **Edit amount** on the fee card, choose **% of loan** or a fixed amount, and can give a reason. The line shows **Edited** with the schedule figure beside it, and **Reset to schedule** puts it back. The officer also picks each fee's treatment and can add fees of their own. This needs the **Edit loans** permission. See [Fees and insurance at capture](/omnia/los/loans/fees-and-insurance-at-capture).
2. **The product's own fee.** An amount or waiver set on the [product sheet](/omnia/los/admin/loan-products#setting-fees-per-product).
3. **The fee schedule.** Your workspace default from the [Fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule).

If no fee schedule exists for the loan type, the panel says so and the full gross goes to the payees.

### The creditor-life premium line

When creditor-life cover applies and the member pays the premium **annually** or **in full**, the screen adds its own line: **Creditor life insurance (annual premium, first year)** or **Creditor life insurance (single premium, paid in full)**. It is owed to the insurer, carries no tax, and is deducted from the proceeds by default. Its amount comes from the cover set at capture and cannot be edited here.

## What a Disbursement officer can change

On top of the starting figures, you can:

- **Edit** a line's rate or amount for this loan. A formula line shows **Auto**. Override it with a manual amount, and **Use formula** puts the calculation back.
- **Waive** a line. Give a reason of at least 3 characters. A waived line is struck through and no longer reduces the net.
- **Add fee or deduction** for a charge that is not on the schedule.

A disbursement holds at most 20 fee lines.

When you schedule, the exact lines, tax, and net are frozen on the disbursement. Later changes to the fee schedule never alter a disbursement already scheduled. Every edit, waiver, and added line is recorded in the audit trail with who made it, and a waiver keeps its reason.

## Who can change fees

| Task | Permission | Built-in roles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edit a fee for one loan at capture | **Edit loans** | Credit Officer, Administrator |
| Edit, waive, or add lines at disbursement | **Disburse loans** | Disbursement |
| Change the fee schedule itself | **Manage fees** | Administrator, Credit Manager |
