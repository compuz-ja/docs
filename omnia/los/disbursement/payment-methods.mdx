Each payee on a disbursement is paid by one of six payment methods, and the method decides which bank details the form asks for.

## The six methods

The payment method list on each payee row offers these, in this order.

| Method | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **ACH** | Batch transfer to another bank, settled later in the day. For smaller amounts. |
| **RTGS** | Large-value transfer to another bank, settled immediately and irrevocably. |
| **Internal transfer** | A move into the member's own share or savings account at your credit union. |
| **Cheque** | A printed cheque made out to the payee. |
| **Manager's cheque** | A banker's draft drawn on your credit union. |
| **Wire / SWIFT** | An international wire, chiefly for foreign-currency beneficiaries. |

## Large transfers go by RTGS

An ACH payee can carry at most $1,000,000. Anything above that must go by **RTGS**. This follows the central bank's large-value payment rule in the standard setup.

The check runs when you click **Schedule disbursement**. Exactly $1,000,000 on ACH is accepted. A cent more is refused, and the message tells you to switch that payee to RTGS.

The ceiling applies to each payee, not to the whole disbursement. A $1,500,000 loan split across two $750,000 ACH payees schedules without complaint.

## Bank details by method

The form shows only the fields the chosen method needs.

| Method | Fields shown |
| --- | --- |
| **ACH** and **RTGS** | Bank picker, **Bank code**, **Branch / transit**, routing number, **Account type** |
| **Wire / SWIFT** | Bank picker, **SWIFT / BIC**, **Bank code**, routing number (the ABA number for a US bank) |
| **Internal transfer** | **Account type**: the member's share or savings account |
| **Cheque** and **Manager's cheque** | **Payee mailing address**, optional |

**Account type** is one of **Chequing**, **Savings**, or **Share**. A SWIFT/BIC is 8 or 11 characters.

Bank transfers and wires also take the payee's bank account number, and an internal transfer takes the member's account number. A cheque row offers an optional line for cheque payee details instead. You type the account number on each disbursement. It is encrypted as soon as it is saved and is never stored on a [saved payee](/omnia/los/disbursement/saved-payees).

## Picking a bank

For ACH, RTGS, and wires, the **Pick a bank** list fills in the bank's name and SWIFT/BIC for you. It also fills the bank code once your credit union has loaded the national bank and branch code listing from its clearing house. Until then, type the bank code and branch transit code yourself.

Branches open and close, so the code listing is refreshed from the clearing house rather than kept by hand. If a bank or code is missing or out of date, ask your administrator to reload it.

<Note>
Some banks receive foreign-currency wires through a correspondent bank rather than directly. Confirm the correspondent path with the receiving bank before you send a first wire to it.
</Note>
