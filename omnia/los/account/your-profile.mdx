Your profile holds the settings that belong to you as a person: your avatar, account details, password, and multi-factor authentication. They are the same in every workspace you work in.

The profile has its own left sidebar with three pages: **Settings**, **Authentication**, and **Notifications**. Notifications has its own guide, [Notification preferences](/omnia/los/account/notification-preferences).

## Opening your profile

Click your avatar in the top-right corner. Select **Profile** to open **Settings**, or **Authentication** to go straight to sign-in security. The same links sit under **Manage Account** at the bottom of the left menu.

The top bar also shows your current role next to your avatar. Your Administrator sets your role; you cannot change it here.

To get back to work, click the back arrow at the top of the profile sidebar. With one workspace, it returns you to that dashboard. With more than one, it opens the workspace picker.

## Settings

### Avatar

Your avatar appears next to your name in the top bar and the user menu. Click the avatar circle or **Upload**, pick an image, crop it, and save.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Accepted formats" icon="user">
    PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 2 MB. Square images look best.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replace or remove" icon="settings">
    Use **Replace** for a new image, or **Remove** to go back to your initials.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Large images are scaled down when you save, so a big photo still uploads. Every image is checked for the right file type and for viruses. A file that fails is rejected and your current avatar stays.

### Account details

The page header reads **Profile** and shows your email and a **Member since** date. Below it:

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Email** | The address you sign in with, with a badge showing whether it is verified. |
| **Display name** | How your name appears in the audit trail and in emails. |
| **Language** | The application language. English only. |
| **Timezone** | Used to show dates across the app. Set to UTC. |

<Note>
  These fields are read-only. To correct your email or display name, ask your Administrator or LOS support team.
</Note>

The **Delete your account** action is shown but disabled.

## Authentication

### Two-factor authentication

Shows whether multi-factor authentication is on, with a button to set it up or start over. See [Set up multi-factor authentication](/omnia/los/access/mfa).

### Password

Enter your current password, then the new one. The new password needs at least 12 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number, and a symbol. A strength meter checks each rule as you type. **Update password** stays greyed out until your current password is filled in and the new one meets every rule.

**Sign out other devices** is ticked by default. Leave it ticked to end every other session when you change your password. That is the safe choice if you think someone else has used your account.

## Switching workspace

**Switch workspace** appears in the avatar menu only when you have access to more than one workspace. It opens the workspace picker. Any role can switch between the workspaces it belongs to.

<Tip>
  Your avatar, password, and MFA follow you into every workspace. Switching changes where you are working, not who you are signed in as.
</Tip>
