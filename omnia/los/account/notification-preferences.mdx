The **Notifications** page in your profile lets you turn off optional email alerts. Your choices belong to your account and apply in every workspace you work in.

## Opening notification preferences

Click your avatar in the top-right corner and choose **Profile**, or open **Manage Account** then **Profile** at the bottom of the left menu. In the profile sidebar, select **Notifications**.

## What you can turn off

The page has one preference.

| Preference | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **New invitations sent by other admins** | When another administrator invites someone to a workspace you belong to, you get an email so you can revoke it if it was a mistake. Turn this off to stop those emails. |

It is **on by default**.

The toggle saves the moment you flip it. A short message confirms "Enabled invitation alerts" or "Disabled invitation alerts". If the save fails, the toggle snaps back and an error appears, so a toggle that snaps back means nothing changed.

## Who gets the invitation alert

A preference can only stop an email you would otherwise get. Whether you get it at all depends on your role.

- The alert goes to people who can see the invitation list, which means people with **Manage users and invitations**.
- The person who sent the invitation does not get their own alert.
- Suspended and revoked users get nothing.

## What this page does not control

Loan emails, bell alerts, and the weekday morning summary have no switch here. They follow your role and the loans you own:

- **Hand-off emails** go to whoever owns the loan's current step, or to the whole team when nobody does.
- **Bell alerts** mirror your hand-off emails. Mark them read to clear the count.
- **The weekday morning summary** only arrives on days when something is waiting for you.

To get fewer loan emails, claim or get assigned the loans you work. Once a loan has an owner, its stage emails stop going to the whole team.

For which loan events send email and who receives each, see [Email notifications for loan workflow events](/omnia/los/workflows/notifications). For the bell and the morning summary, see [Alerts, hand-off emails and the morning summary](/omnia/los/navigation/alerts-and-email).
