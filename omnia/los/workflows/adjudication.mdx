**Credit Approvals** is the queue where submitted loan applications are reviewed and decided. An approver claims a submitted application, reviews it, and records one of three decisions: approve, decline, or request more information. The queue was called **Adjudication** in earlier releases.

## What you need

You need the **Approve loans** permission, which the Adjudicator role holds. Your credit union's [approval authority](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes) may also limit what you can approve on your own, by type of security and amount.

Staff with **View reports** but not **Approve loans**, like the Credit Manager and Branch Manager by default, can open the queue as a read-only oversight view.

## The queue

Open **Credit Approvals** under **Review Queue** in the sidebar.

- The **Active** tab shows applications that are **Submitted**, in **Approval**, or in **Info requested**. **Completed** shows decided files: **Approved**, **Declined** and **Cancelled**.
- **All**, **My queue** and **Unassigned** filter by owner, with counts.
- Search, branch and loan-type filters narrow the list.

Hover over or focus a row to see a quick preview: product, amount, term, stage, assigned officer and who captured it.

## Process an application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the application">
    Click a row. The page header shows the loan number, the member and the member number (CIF).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim it">
    Click **Claim for adjudication**. The status moves from **Submitted** to **Approval** and the file is yours. The claim is refused when you cannot approve this loan on your own, and the message names the roles that can. See [Loan assignment](/omnia/los/loans/loan-assignment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the file">
    Read every card before you decide (described below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record your decision">
    In the **Record decision** card, pick **Approve**, **Request more information** or **Decline**, write a rationale of at least 20 characters, and click **Record decision**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you review

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Application summary** | Loan ID, type, branch, member, CIF, phone, email, amount requested, term, submitted time. |
| **Affordability snapshot** | Monthly gross income, monthly expenses, net monthly income, an expense ratio (monthly expenses ÷ gross), and counts of guarantors, employment records and documents. |
| **Risk assessment** | Band (R1 to R5), score, policy flags, and the **How the score was reached** breakdown. Under it, **Credit report / Risk Registry** shows the captured credit rating and the risk registry result. |
| **Collateral** | The security on file, with loan-to-value (LTV) and any security documents. Hidden for unsecured loans. |
| **KYC status**, **identity**, **documents** | The member's verification state, masked identifiers you can reveal (each reveal is recorded), and the document library. |
| **Credit officer recommendation** | The officer's note and the **Recommended approval level**. This is advice; the approval route decides who approves. |
| **Decision history** | Every earlier decision with its date, who made it (with their role), the rationale and any conditions. |
| **Approval route** | Who must approve this loan, why, votes so far, waivers. See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes). |
| **Signed documents still outstanding** | Documents that will stop the file after approval, shown while you decide. |

The affordability snapshot's expense ratio is a quick read on gross income. The TDSR that counts is in the risk breakdown and on the loan's **Income & expenses** section; it uses counted income and includes this loan's payment. See [Affordability and TDSR](/omnia/los/loans/affordability-and-tdsr).

## Decision outcomes

Every decision needs a rationale of at least 20 characters, which is kept in the audit trail.

<Frame caption="The decision panel. The approval route note above the options tells you whether your approval completes the decision or records a committee vote.">
  <img src="/images/omnia/los/approval/decision-panel.png" alt="Record decision card with Approve, Request more information and Decline options, a rationale field and the Route Securities review to picker" />
</Frame>

### Approve

"Move the loan forward through the workflow. The Securities team picks it up next."

- Write the rationale.
- Optionally pick someone in **Route Securities review to (optional)**. Leave it blank to drop the file in the Securities queue.
- On the **In-House Committee** route, your approval is a vote; the loan is approved when the quorum completes.
- On the **Credit Committee** route, enter the meeting date, an optional minute number and the proof of approval.
- Click **Record decision**.

If you cannot approve on this route, the panel says so: "Your role cannot approve on this route. You can still request more information or decline."

A successful approval generates the Loan Agreement PDF and moves the loan to **Securities**. An unsecured loan has no security requests and moves on to **Disbursement** once the documents your credit union requires before disbursement are signed.

### Request more information

"Send the file back to the Credit Officer with a list of items to address. Reversible."

- Write the rationale.
- List at least one **condition**, one per line. The officer sees them.
- Click **Record decision**.

The application moves to **Info requested**, then back to **Draft** for the officer to edit and resubmit. Any committee votes already cast on the file are cleared.

### Decline

"Close this application. Terminal."

- Write the rationale.
- Click **Record decision** and confirm **Yes, decline** in the dialog.

The loan moves to **Declined** and leaves all active queues. The Credit Officer is notified. The file cannot be reopened; a member who wants to try again needs a fresh application.

## Refusals at approval

The approval is refused, and the loan stays in **Approval**, when any of these apply:

- You captured the application, or you overrode its rate, and your credit union has switched on that rule.
- You lack authority for the product and amount. The message names who can approve it.
- The amount is above the product maximum and no valid exception applies.
- A waiver is pending, or a required waiver is missing.
- The committee quorum is incomplete, or the committee decision is not recorded.
- The risk band is above the product's ceiling and the override rules are not met (below).

Each rule is explained on [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes).

## Risk band override

When the risk band is above the product's ceiling, an **Override required** panel appears on the Approve path: "Risk band BAND exceeds the policy ceiling for TYPE loans." Approving then needs all three:

- the **Override rates** permission, which the Credit Manager and Administrator hold by default;
- your credit union allowing risk-band overrides (your administrator turns this on);
- an override rationale of at least 20 characters.

Without the permission the panel adds: "Your role doesn't include the override capability. Contact your Credit Manager to approve, or decline this application." If any of the three is missing, the approval is refused, the attempt is recorded in the audit trail, and the status does not change.

## Rate override

A manual rate override is set on the draft, not on this page. It needs the **Override rates** permission and a reason of 3 to 280 characters, and works only while the loan is a **Draft**. The rate shows as **Rate override active** with who set it and why, and approval prices the loan at that rate. Where your credit union has switched on the rate-overrider rule, the person who set the override cannot approve the loan.

## The Info requested cycle

When the officer resubmits, the risk assessment runs again on the updated application, the file returns as **Submitted**, and any eligible approver can claim it again. The decision history shows what was asked and what was decided each time.

## Credit Approvals dashboard

**Dashboards**, then **Credit Approvals**, shows **Pending decision** (files that are Submitted, in Approval or in Info requested), **Approved this week** and **Declined this week** (last 7 days), **Avg time to decision** (submission to decision, last 7 days) and a **Decisions per day** sparkline over 14 days. It needs **Approve loans** or **View reports**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not claim this loan?">
    You cannot approve it on your own under your credit union's approval authority. The message names who can. The loan may also already be owned by someone else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I approved but the status did not change. Why?">
    The loan is on the In-House Committee route. Your approval was recorded as a vote. It is approved when the quorum is complete.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the interest rate here?">
    No. A rate override is set on the draft by someone with the **Override rates** permission. You can request more information and ask for it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a declined loan be reopened?">
    No. Declining is terminal. The member needs a new application.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the risk registry result adverse but the band is low?">
    The risk registry result does not change the band. It is shown to you as a separate signal. Weigh it in your decision.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the recommended approval level decide who approves?">
    No. It is the officer's advice. Your credit union's approval authority and routing rules decide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did Adjudication go?">
    It is the same queue, renamed **Credit Approvals**. Bookmarks to the old page still work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
