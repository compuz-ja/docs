The **Securities** stage is where an approved loan's collateral is checked and its security paperwork is signed before any money moves.

Two things must be clear before a loan leaves Securities: every **security request** is resolved, and every document the Securities stage requires is signed. The moment both are true, the loan moves to **Disbursement** by itself and the Disbursement team is notified. Nobody presses a hand-off button.

## What you need

The **Securities** role holds **Review securities**, which lets you approve, reject, and waive security requests and send a loan back. An Adjudicator can raise and waive security requests too.

A Credit Officer can open the Securities page only to answer requests: submitting a requested document needs **Edit loans**, and uploading the document itself also needs **Upload member documents**.

## How a loan arrives

Approval moves a loan to **Securities** straight away.

| Loan type | What happens on arrival |
|---|---|
| Motor vehicle, real estate, cash secured | A first security request is raised automatically, listing the documents the approval conditions asked for. With no conditions on the approval, the defaults are: **Cash collateral confirmation** for cash secured; **Vehicle valuation**, **Insurance binder**, and **Title transfer document** for motor vehicle; **Property valuation**, **Title search**, **Insurance binder**, and **Survey diagram** for real estate. |
| Unsecured | No security request is raised, because there is no collateral. The LOS tries to route the loan on to Disbursement immediately. |

An unsecured loan only skips ahead if its required documents are already signed. Every loan needs the borrower's credit consent, and every guarantor's consent and guarantee, signed before it leaves Securities. If any of those is still outstanding, the unsecured loan waits in **Securities** and moves on the moment the last one is signed. See [Legal documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/overview#the-paperwork-gates-the-workflow).

## Reviewing a loan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Securities queue">
    Open **Securities** under **Review Queue** in the sidebar. The count beside it is the number of loans waiting. A **Pending / Completed** toggle switches between active and finished requests, and the **All / My queue / Unassigned** tabs filter to your files or unclaimed work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the application">
    Click a row. The Securities page shows the application, the risk assessment, the collateral on file, the decision history, the member's KYC status, identity and documents, and the security requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the collateral">
    The **Collateral on file** card shows the collateral type, valued amount, valuation date, valuation source, and loan-to-value ratio. Cross-check it against what the loan type needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the requests and the documents">
    Resolve, reject, or waive each security request, and make sure the required documents on the loan's **Legal documents & contracts** card get signed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Raising a security request

A **security request** asks the Credit Officer for a specific document. A loan can carry several open requests at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request form">
    On the Securities page, find the **Request additional documents** card and click **Request additional documents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say exactly what is needed">
    List each document, for example a vehicle title, an insurance binder, or a survey diagram. The Credit Officer reads your words to find or obtain it. Click **Create request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The request lands as Pending">
    The request is created as **Pending** and the Credit Officer is notified by email. The loan stays in **Securities**.

    If every document you ask for is already on the member's file, the request skips straight to **Submitted** with an **Auto-detected** pill, and you can review it immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The officer submits">
    The Credit Officer uploads the document to the member's file and marks the request submitted. You are notified to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve, reject, or waive">
    - **Approve.** The document meets the requirement. The request moves to **Resolved**.
    - **Reject.** The document is wrong or incomplete. Enter a reason of at least 10 characters. The officer is notified and a fresh request is needed.
    - **Waive.** The document is not needed on this file. See [Waiving a security request](#waiving-a-security-request).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you resolve the last open request, a **Route Disbursement to** picker lets you hand the file to a named person on the Disbursement team. The confirmation tells you where the loan landed: **Securities cleared, sent to Disbursement** when it moved, or **Security request resolved** when a document is still outstanding.

## Waiving a security request

Waive a request when the document is not actually needed: you hold waiver authority, an exception policy applies, or the document is already on the member's record. Open the request, choose **Waive**, and enter a justification of at least 20 characters.

The request becomes **Resolved** with a **Waived** pill, and the audit trail records it as a waiver with your reason, so compliance can find every waiver. The Credit Officer is notified. A waived request counts as completed.

## Security request states

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Raised. The Credit Officer must supply the documents. |
| **Submitted** | The officer has supplied them. Waiting for your review. |
| **Resolved** | Approved or waived. Cleared. |
| **Rejected** | Turned down. A fresh request is needed. |

Only **Pending** and **Submitted** requests hold a loan. A rejected request does not.

## How the loan reaches Disbursement

The LOS checks whether the loan can move to Disbursement whenever one of these happens:

- the last open security request is resolved or waived;
- a required document is signed, by upload or e-signature;
- the loan is approved (the attempt described for unsecured loans above).

If no request is open and every required document is signed, the loan moves to **Disbursement** and the Disbursement team is emailed. If a document is still outstanding, the loan stays in **Securities**, the reason is recorded in the audit trail, and no "ready for disbursement" email goes out, because the file is not ready.

A green **Ready for disbursement** banner appears on a loan whose requests are all resolved. If nobody has picked the file up, click **Notify Disbursement team** to send the email again. When a document is still outstanding, the button shows that reason instead of sending.

<Tip>
  The loan is stuck in Securities with every request resolved? Look at the **Legal documents & contracts** card on the loan. A prepared document is not a signed one. The loan moves the moment the last signed copy is recorded.
</Tip>

## Sending a loan back

Three ways out of Securities that are not forward:

| Action | Where | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revert to Credit approval** | Page header | Securities | Returns the loan to **Approval** with prior work intact. Needs a reason of at least 20 characters. The approvers are notified. |
| **Send back to draft** | Page header | Securities | Returns the loan to the Credit Officer as a **Draft** to rebuild and resubmit. Needs a reason of at least 10 characters. |
| **Send back to Adjudication** | Card on the page | Adjudicator | Raises a **Securities issue** for a structural problem with the collateral package, such as a valuation mismatch or ineligible collateral. Needs a reason of at least 10 characters. |

A **Securities issue** is owned by the Adjudicator, who can restructure the collateral plan, request information, or decline. Open security requests on their own never change the loan's status.

### When every request was rejected

If you reject the last open request and none of the loan's requests is pending, submitted, or resolved, the LOS sends the loan back to **Approval** automatically and emails the approvers and the Credit Officer. The confirmation reads **Rejected, sent back for a fresh approval**. An older loan stranded this way shows a recovery banner with a one-click action that does the same.

## Collateral and security holds

The **Collateral & security holds** panel is where the perfecting officer records or adjusts collateral and places a lien on the member's own pledged account.

- **Record collateral** or **update a valuation** needs **Manage collateral**. The cash types are **Cash, shares**, **Cash, hypothecation**, **Cash, held at another institution**, and **Fixed / time deposit**, alongside vehicle and property.
- **Place a hold** on a member's shares, savings, or deposit, so the funds cannot be withdrawn while the loan is outstanding, or **Release** it, needs **Place and release holds**.

Securities, Disbursement, the Credit Manager, and the Administrator hold both permissions. The same panel appears on the Disbursement page.

## The post-disbursement re-check

Some credit unions check security after funds have gone out, for example that vehicle insurance was activated or a lien was filed. An Adjudicator can send a **Disbursed** loan back to Securities for this, but only when your credit union has turned the re-check on; it is off by default and your system administrator sets it. The loan reappears in the Securities queue while its status stays **Disbursed**, and once the new request is resolved the file returns through Disbursement to be filed.

## Securities dashboard

**Dashboards**, then **Securities**, shows **Pending reviews**, **Outstanding requests**, **Resolved this week**, **Reviews completed**, and **Auto-skipped**, with a **Requests resolved per day** sparkline. It needs **Review securities** or **View reports**.
