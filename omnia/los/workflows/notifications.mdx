The LOS sends email automatically at major loan lifecycle events, to the person responsible for the file. If a loan is assigned to a specific staff member, only that person is notified. If the loan has no owner yet, the email goes to everyone who works that stage: every Adjudicator, the whole Securities team, or the whole Disbursement team.

Every email sent to a staff member also lands as an alert in the bell in the top bar. The bell, the weekday morning summary email and Teams channel posts are covered in [Alerts, hand-off emails and the morning summary](/omnia/los/navigation/alerts-and-email). This page is the full routing reference for the event emails.

## How routing works

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Assigned loan" icon="user-check">
    Only the assigned staff member receives the notification. Once you claim a file or are assigned one, you are the single point of contact for every stage event on that loan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unassigned loan" icon="users">
    The email goes to everyone who works the stage: every Adjudicator, the whole Securities team, or the whole Disbursement team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Claiming a loan" icon="hand">
    Claiming a loan for yourself does **not** trigger an email. Only an assignment by someone else sends an "assigned to you" notification.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who is emailed for each event

| Event | Recipients |
|---|---|
| Loan submitted, routes to Credit Approvals | Assigned Adjudicator, or all Adjudicators if unassigned |
| Loan assigned to a staff member | The newly assigned staff member |
| Recommendation routed for sign-off | The approvers for the approval level the Credit Officer recommended. A Branch Manager recommendation goes only to the managers of the loan's branch. |
| Loan approved | Credit Officer who captured the application |
| Loan declined | Credit Officer, plus the member if member emails are switched on |
| Information requested | Credit Officer |
| Loan returned to Draft (full rebuild) | Credit Officer |
| Loan sent back to a previous stage | The team that now has it, plus the Credit Officer |
| Security request created | Credit Officer |
| Security request documents submitted by officer | The assigned Securities owner, or the Securities team if unassigned |
| Security request resolved or rejected | Credit Officer |
| Ready for Disbursement | The assigned Disbursement owner, or the Disbursement team if unassigned, plus the Credit Officer |
| Disbursement scheduled | The assigned Disbursement owner, or the Disbursement team if unassigned |
| Funds disbursed | Credit Officer, plus the member if member emails are switched on |
| Loan filed | Credit Officer (see [Loan filed](#loan-filed)) |
| Loan cancelled | The owner and the Credit Officer when the file has an owner. Without an owner, a loan cancelled while **Submitted** goes to the Adjudicators, and one cancelled in **Disbursement** goes to the Credit Officer, the Adjudicators and the Securities team, so every team that touched the file knows it closed |

## Confidential loans

A confidential loan narrows every team email. It goes only to staff who work the stage **and** hold the **View confidential loans** permission. The assigned owner and the Credit Officer who captured the loan are named individuals and are still notified. Staff who cannot see the loan on screen are not told about it by email, and the loan is never posted to a Teams channel.

Whether a loan is confidential is fixed when the loan is captured, so its notifications follow the rules that applied when the loan was taken, even if the member's record changes later.

## The link in every email

Every staff email carries a **View loan & current stage** button that opens the loan in your workspace, where the [next-step banner](/omnia/los/navigation/the-loan-page#the-next-step-banner) shows who holds it and what is stopping it.

## Member notifications

Emails to members are off by default. They are switched on by your system administrator, not from a screen in the app. When they are on, members receive emails for two events only:

- **Loan declined.** The email tells them the application was unsuccessful and includes the rationale.
- **Funds disbursed.** A confirmation that funds have been released.

Members never receive internal workflow notifications such as Information requested or Ready for Disbursement.

<Note>
Your credit union's name and branding appear in every email, to staff and members alike. The sender name is your credit union's configured sender name, and your primary brand colour accents the loan link button. An email reflects the branding in force when the event happened.
</Note>

## Assignment notifications

When someone uses the **Assignment** panel to route a loan to a specific person, that person receives an "a loan needs your attention" email straight away, as long as the loan is actionable.

Who can direct a loan to a person:

- Staff with the **Assign loans** permission (Credit Managers, Branch Managers and Administrators by default) can assign any loan.
- A Credit Officer can route their own draft to an approver when submitting it.
- A stage worker can hand a loan to a colleague in the same stage, for example one Adjudicator to another.

One exception: assigning a loan that is still a **Draft** does **not** send the email. The approver gets the notice later, when the loan is submitted.

Claiming a loan yourself never sends an email. It is recorded in the audit trail.

## Scoped versus broadcast

Once a loan has an owner, stage emails go only to that person instead of the whole team.

| Event | Unassigned | Assigned |
|---|---|---|
| Loan routed to Credit Approvals | Every Adjudicator in the credit union | The one assigned Adjudicator |
| Security request documents submitted | The whole Securities team | The one assigned owner |
| Ready for Disbursement or scheduled | The whole Disbursement team | The one assigned owner |

Assigning loans promptly, either through the Assignment panel or by claiming, keeps your team's inboxes manageable and makes it clear who is accountable for each file.

## Loan filed

By default, only the Credit Officer is emailed when a loan is filed. Senior staff track filed volume from their dashboards rather than from their inbox. Your system administrator can widen this so Credit Managers and the Branch Managers of the loan's branch are also emailed. Branch Managers at other branches are never included.

<Tip>
If you are not getting notifications you expect, check three things. First, confirm with your Administrator that your role works that stage. Second, check whether the loan is assigned to someone else; if it is, only that person receives stage emails. Third, check whether the loan is confidential and you lack **View confidential loans**. After any loan action, the confirmation on screen names every colleague who was emailed, so you can see the routing for yourself.
</Tip>
