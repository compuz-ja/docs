The **Disbursement** queue holds approved loans that have cleared Securities and are ready for their funds to be released.

As a Disbursement officer you check the fees, enter who gets paid, schedule the payment, and release it. Nothing is scheduled or released until you act, and a loan becomes **Disbursed** only when you confirm the release.

A loan arrives here by itself. When its last security request is resolved and its last Securities document is signed, it moves to **Disbursement** and your team is emailed. See [Securities](/omnia/los/workflows/securities#how-the-loan-reaches-disbursement).

## What you need

You need the **Disburse loans** permission, which the **Disbursement** role holds. If you can see the queue but cannot act on a loan, ask your Administrator to check your role.

## How a disbursement works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Disbursement queue">
    Open **Disbursement** under **Review Queue** in the sidebar. The count beside it is the number of loans waiting. The **Pending** tab lists loans ready to schedule or release. The **Completed** tab lists disbursed and filed loans. **All**, **My queue**, and **Unassigned** narrow the list to your files or unclaimed work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the loan">
    Click a row. The page shows the approved amount, the fees already filled in, and the net to member.

    The fees start from what the Credit Officer agreed with the member at capture: any fee they edited, the treatment they chose, and any fee they added. Untouched fees come from the product, or else the fee schedule. A creditor-life premium paid annually or in full appears as its own line. See [Fees and taxes](/omnia/los/disbursement/fees-and-gct).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the fees and the net to member">
    Review the **Gross approved** amount, each fee and tax line, and the **Net to member**. Edit, waive, or add lines if needed. The net is the amount you must pay out in full across your payees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payees">
    For each payee, give the type, name, payment method, bank and account details, amount, and purpose. Use **Pick saved payee** to fill a regular payee's details, and **Add another payee** to split the money. The payee amounts must add up to the net to member exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the disbursement">
    Choose a **Scheduled date** of today or later and click **Schedule disbursement**. The loan stays in **Disbursement**, and the Disbursement team, or the loan's assignee, is told it is scheduled.

    Scheduling is refused if the loan already has a scheduled disbursement, or if the deductions would leave the net below zero. To schedule again, send the loan back to Securities first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release the funds">
    Enter the **Bank reference number** from the issuing bank and click **Mark disbursed**. The loan moves to **Disbursed** and the Credit Officer is told. The member is told too when member emails are turned on for your workspace.

    Scheduling and release are separate, so one officer can schedule and another release.

    **Mark disbursed** is refused until the signed **Loan Disbursement Sheet** is on file, plus the **Declaration of Purpose** when the net is below the declaration threshold. The message names the first missing document. Both are on the loan's **Legal documents & contracts** card. See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet).

    It is also refused while a second approval is pending, while required screening is not clear, or when the net is above your personal limit. See [Controls](/omnia/los/disbursement/controls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="File the loan">
    With the loan **Disbursed**, click **File loan (terminal)** on the **File loan** card. The loan closes at **Filed**. Filing is always a manual step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Paying more than one party

Many loans pay several parties. An auto loan might split between the car dealer and the member. A mortgage might send part to an attorney for transfer costs. A disbursement holds up to 20 payees.

<Warning>
The payee amounts must equal the net to member exactly. Any shortfall or excess blocks scheduling. The **Payees total vs net** panel shows the current total, the required net, and the difference.
</Warning>

## Pre-execution checklist

Three conditions must hold before you can schedule or release, on top of the signed documents. The page shows them as the **Pre-execution checklist** and keeps **Mark disbursed** disabled until all three pass:

- The member's identity check is **Verified**.
- No security request on the loan is still open.
- A loan agreement has been generated.

The checks run again when you click, so a stale page cannot get around them. If something slips back after scheduling, for example a security request reopens, the release is blocked until it is fixed. If the agreement is missing, click **Generate agreement** on the **Loan agreement** card. See [Loan agreements](/omnia/los/loans/loan-agreements).

## Sending a loan back or cancelling it

Until the funds are released, the page header offers two ways out. Each needs a reason of at least 20 characters.

- **Revert to Securities** sends the loan back one stage with its work intact, for example to replace a signed Securities document. The Securities team is told.
- **Cancel** closes the application for good. The member must apply again.

## What else the page shows

The page also recaps the whole file so you can check it without leaving:

- **Risk assessment**: the risk band, score, and the factor breakdown the Adjudicator saw.
- **Collateral summary**: the latest collateral record with its value and valuation source.
- **Decision history**: every adjudication decision, with its rationale and who made it.
- **Identity check status**: the member's verification state, with a link to their documents.
- **Collateral & security holds**: record collateral, or place or release a hold on a pledged account. See [Securities](/omnia/los/workflows/securities#collateral-and-security-holds).
- **Loan agreement**: view or download it. If it failed to generate, the reason shows here.

## After the release

The bank reference you entered is kept on the disbursement. Record the payment's progress, or reverse it, on the **Controls & settlement** card. See [Settlement](/omnia/los/disbursement/settlement).

### Post-disbursement security check

Some credit unions check security again after funds go out, for example to confirm the vehicle insurance started or a lien was registered. If yours does, a staff member with the **Approve loans** permission opens a post-disbursement security request. The file goes back to the Securities queue while the loan stays **Disbursed**, and returns to Disbursement for filing once the request is resolved. **File loan** never starts this check. Your LOS support team turns the check on for your credit union.

<Note>
**Filed** is final. A filed loan is locked against further changes and its audit trail is complete. New lending for the member needs a new application.
</Note>

## Disbursement dashboard

Open **Dashboards → Disbursement** for four tiles: **Awaiting disbursement** (the same count as the **Pending** tab), **Disbursed this week**, **Total disbursed** (net of fees, last 7 days), and **Avg approval to fund** (last 7 days), plus a **Disbursements per day** chart over the last 14 days. You need **Disburse loans** or **View reports**.
