The Members section is the directory of everyone who borrows from your credit union. Every loan application belongs to a member, and a member's identity must be **Verified** before a loan can be submitted for them. You search, create, update and manage members from the Members list, and jump from a profile to the member's loans and documents.

## What a member record contains

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Salutation, first, middle and last name | Used for search and display everywhere |
| Date of birth | Required |
| Gender | Male, Female or Other. Optional |
| Email address, phone number | Primary contact details. Optional |
| Address | Street, city, parish or region, postal code, residential status, years at address, and the previous address. A separate mailing address defaults to the residential address |
| Marital status, dependents, education | Part of the applicant profile used in loan assessment |
| Tax ID (TRN in Jamaica) | Encrypted |
| Driver's licence, passport, bank account numbers | Encrypted |
| Government ID type, number and issuer | The general identity document fields. The number is encrypted |
| Mother's maiden name | Encrypted |
| Internal notes | Free text, encrypted |
| Next of kin | Name, relationship, contact details and address |
| Employment status | Used in loan assessment |
| Branch | The branch the member belongs to. Fixed once the member is created |
| Member CIF number | The member's identifier in your Core Banking System. Unique in your workspace |
| Credit Union Member Account Number | The member's account number in your Core Banking System. Up to 16 digits, encrypted, shown as the last four digits |
| Member since | The date the member joined the credit union, from Core Banking. Not the date the record was created here |
| Classification | Optional. Your credit union's staff classifications. A confidential classification makes the member's new loans confidential |

Branch, Member CIF number, Credit Union Member Account Number, Member since, first name, last name and date of birth are always required. Your credit union can require more fields, and the form refuses to save until they are filled; see [Creating members](/omnia/los/members/creating-members#required-fields). A record saved before a field became required shows an amber **This member record is incomplete** banner. It does not block loans.

## Sensitive details

The tax ID, driver's licence, passport, bank account, Core Banking account number, government ID number, mother's maiden name and notes are encrypted with your credit union's own key. On the edit form, staff with the **Edit members** permission can click **Reveal** on the tax ID, driver's licence, passport, bank account, Core Banking account number and notes. Loan reviewers with the **View member personal details** permission can reveal the tax ID, driver's licence and passport, read-only, from the loan pages. Every reveal is recorded in the audit trail. The government ID number and mother's maiden name never reveal; they show as **On file (encrypted)**.

## Additional contacts

A member can have extra contacts beyond the primary email, phone and address: an alternate phone, an alternate email or an alternate address. One contact of each type can be primary. From the profile you add a contact, remove one, or promote one to primary, which demotes the current primary of that type. Managing contacts needs the **Edit members** permission.

## Member status and identity status

Every member carries two separate statuses.

**Member status** says whether the account is open for new loans.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The default. The member can apply for loans. |
| **Inactive** | Suspended. No new loans until the member is reactivated. |
| **Deceased** | Can only arrive through a data migration. There is no in-app control to set it, but it stays available as a filter on the Members list. |

**Suspend** sets a member to **Inactive** and **Reactivate** sets them back to **Active**. Deleting a member is separate: it hides the record from the list and search, keeps the reason and date, and can be undone with **Restore**.

**Identity status** tracks the know-your-customer (KYC) check.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Unverified** | No check has run yet. |
| **In progress** | A check was submitted and is waiting for a result. |
| **Verified** | Identity confirmed. Loans can be submitted. |
| **Failed** | The check was rejected. Correct the details and resubmit. |
| **Manual review** | The automated check could not decide. A reviewer approves or rejects it. |

<Note>
  A member can be **Active** and **Unverified** at the same time. You can create and edit the profile at any time, but you cannot submit a loan until identity status is **Verified**.
</Note>

## Members and loan applications

A member can have many loan applications over time. The profile shows the total count and the three most recent, each linked. Every loan page shows its member, so you can move between the two in either direction.

## Finding the Members section

Open **Members** in the sidebar under **Workspace**. Search, open a profile, or create a new member. Rest the pointer on a row to peek at the member's key details without opening the profile.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a member be Active but not able to borrow?">
    Yes. An Active member whose identity is **Unverified** or **Failed** cannot have a loan drafted at all. One whose check is **In progress** or in **Manual review** can have a draft, but it cannot be submitted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do the CIF and account number come from?">
    Your Core Banking System. Both, and the Member since date, are copied from the member's record there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can see a member's tax ID?">
    Staff with **Edit members** on the edit form, and loan reviewers with **View member personal details** on the loan pages. Every reveal is recorded in the audit trail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a member show an incomplete banner?">
    The record was saved before your credit union required one of its fields. Loans are not blocked. Complete it on the next edit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a member ever deleted?">
    No. Deleting hides the record from the list and search. An Administrator can restore it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
