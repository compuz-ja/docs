A member profile is the customer record a loan application links to. No member record, no loan application. The profile starts from identifiers your Core Banking System already holds, so the two systems agree on who the member is.

<Tip>
  Search before you create. Duplicate records cause confusion when linking loans and tracking identity checks.
</Tip>

## Who can create members

You need the **Create members** permission, which the Credit Officer and the Administrator hold. If you cannot see **New member**, ask your Administrator to check your role.

## Create a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New member form">
    Go to **Members** and click **New member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the Core Banking identifiers">
    The first card, **Core Banking identifiers**, holds values set when the member enrolled. Take all of them from your Core Banking System: for a new member, after you have opened them there; for an existing member, from their current record.

    - **Branch** (required): the branch the member belongs to. Fixed once the member is created.
    - **Member CIF number** (required): the member's Customer Information File number in Core Banking. Copy it exactly. One CIF per person, unique in your workspace. If the member does not exist in Core Banking yet, create them there first.
    - **Credit Union Member Account Number** (required): digits only, up to 16. Stored encrypted and shown as the last four digits. The full number prints on the Loan Disbursement Sheet.
    - **Member since** (required): the date the member joined, as recorded in Core Banking. For a newly enrolled member, that is today. It cannot be in the future, and the form never fills it in for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal details">
    **First name** and **Last name** (required), middle name, **Date of birth** (required), gender, employment, email and phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter identity details">
    The **Sensitive identifiers** card holds the tax ID (TRN in Jamaica), driver's licence, passport and an external bank account. They are optional unless your credit union requires them, and they are encrypted. The **Identity details** card holds salutation, marital status, dependents, education, government ID type, issuer and number, and mother's maiden name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter address, residence and next of kin">
    Mailing address, residential status, years at the address, previous address, and the next of kin's name, relationship, contact details, address and years known.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create member">
    Every required field is checked, the member is created and the profile opens. Identity status starts as **Unverified**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Required fields

Branch, CIF, Core Banking account number, Member since, first name, last name and date of birth are always required. Your credit union can require more fields: contact details, address lines, residence history, identity numbers, next of kin. Ask your system administrator to change the list. A field can also be conditional, for example:

- a mailing address only when it differs from the residential address;
- an ID number only for a chosen ID type;
- a previous address only when the member has lived at the current one for fewer than a set number of years;
- a field only when another field is filled in.

The form marks required fields and checks them before saving. If any are blank, the save is refused with **Complete these fields before saving:** and the list. The same rules apply at [loan submission](/omnia/los/loans/creating-an-application#the-required-fields-policy).

### Records saved before a field became required

These records are not blocked. The profile shows an amber banner, **This member record is incomplete (N fields missing)**, lists the fields, and says loans are not blocked but the fields must be filled the next time the record is edited. **Complete the record** opens the edit form.

Older members may also lack the Core Banking account number or Member since. The profile header prompts you to add the account number, and Member since reads **Not yet taken from Core Banking**. Add both on **Edit**.

## The member profile

The header shows the member's name, CIF and the Core Banking account number masked to its last four digits. The quick facts strip shows **Member since**, branch, date of birth, gender and employment.

### Revealing an encrypted value

On **Edit**, encrypted fields show whether a value is on file and offer **Reveal** for the tax ID, driver's licence, passport, external bank account, Core Banking account number and notes. Reveal needs **Edit members**. The audit trail records who revealed which field, never the value. Loan reviewers with **View member personal details** can reveal the tax ID, driver's licence and passport read-only from the loan pages. The government ID number and mother's maiden name never reveal.

### Peek without opening

In the Members list, rest the pointer on a row, or tab to it, to see a preview of the member's key details. It opens after a short pause and closes on Escape.

## Search for members

The search bar matches names as you type. Filter by identity status, member status or created date. **Export** downloads the filtered list as a CSV. Click a row to open the profile.

## Edit a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile and click Edit">
    You need **Edit members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    Leave an encrypted field blank to keep its stored value. Type a new value to replace it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save changes">
    Required fields are checked again. The change applies immediately and is recorded in the audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Member status

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Active, the default">
    New members are **Active**. Drafting a loan is blocked only while identity status is **Unverified** or **Failed**. Submitting needs **Verified**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inactive, suspended">
    Suspending sets **Inactive**. The record and its history stay, but the member cannot be linked to new loans until reactivated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deceased">
    Has no in-app control and is not set by bulk import. It arrives only through a data migration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Suspending and reactivating">
    Both need **Suspend members**, which the Administrator holds. A suspension needs a reason of at least three characters, stored on the record and in the audit trail. Reactivating clears it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Deleting a member

Deleting needs **Delete members**, which the Administrator holds. A deleted member disappears from the list and search but is kept in full, and an Administrator can restore it. To take a member out of use without deleting, suspend them.

## Classification and linked login

The **Identity and access** card on the profile sets the member's **classification** (which can make their loans confidential) and links a staff member's own login. It needs **Manage member identity links**. See [Staff and confidential loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find the Credit Union Member Account Number?">
    In your Core Banking System, on the member's record. Open the member there first if they are new.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why will the form not let me save?">
    A required field is blank. The error lists every one. Your credit union may require more than the minimum.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set Member since to today for an existing member?">
    No. Use the joining date from Core Banking. Member history and reporting depend on it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does an older member show an incomplete banner?">
    Their record was saved before a field became required. Loans are not blocked. Fill the fields next time you edit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see the full Core Banking account number?">
    Yes, with **Edit members**, by clicking **Reveal** on the edit form. Every reveal is recorded in the audit trail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change a member's branch?">
    No. The branch is fixed once the member is created.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
