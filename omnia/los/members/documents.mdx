The Documents page holds every file attached to a member: identity documents, proof of address, income evidence, collateral paperwork and anything else that supports a loan. Every file is virus-scanned before it is stored, and uploads, deletions and restores are recorded in the audit trail. The page also lists outstanding Securities document requests across the member's loans, so you can see exactly what is still needed.

<Tip>
  Upload the member's identity documents before starting KYC verification. With the tax ID card (TRN card in Jamaica) or driver's licence already on file, the reviewer has everything they need.
</Tip>

## Accepted files

| Detail | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Formats** | PDF, JPEG, PNG, HEIC and WebP |
| **File size** | 10 MB per file |
| **Batch** | Up to 25 files per upload |
| **Content check** | Each file's actual content is checked, not just its name |

<Warning>
  **Unsupported file type** means the file's content is not one of the accepted formats, whatever its name says. A file disguised with a misleading name, for example a program renamed to `.pdf`, is rejected.
</Warning>

## Document types

Pick the right **Document type** when uploading so documents are easy to find and the loan workflow can match them to requests.

| Document type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Primary ID** | Tax ID card, national ID, passport, driver's licence. The tax ID card and driver's licence show on their own panels on the KYC page; any other ID shows in its **Other identification** card. |
| **Secondary ID** | Voter's ID, work ID, school ID, expired passport |
| **Proof of address** | Utility bill, tenancy agreement, government letter |
| **Proof of income** | Payslip, tax return, employer letter, audited financials |
| **Employment letter** | Letter of employment, contract of employment |
| **Bank statement** | Statements, cash collateral confirmation, hold confirmation letter |
| **Collateral valuation** | Vehicle or property valuation |
| **Insurance certificate** | Insurance binder, comprehensive insurance rider |
| **Other** | Title search, survey diagram, bill of sale, signed assignment letter, anything else |

## Upload documents

You need the **Upload member documents** permission.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's Documents page">
    Open the member's profile and click **Documents** in the header. With upload permission, an **Upload documents** card sits on the page next to any outstanding Securities document requests. Staff who can only view see the library but not the pending requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the document type and label">
    Pick a **Document type** and enter a **Label** (required, up to 120 characters). Suggested labels appear as chips under the field: pick one where it fits, because Securities requests look for those names. **Subtype** is optional free text, for example `utility-bill`. Every file in the batch shares the type, label and subtype.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the files">
    Drag files onto the card or click **Choose files to upload**. A multi-page document, like both sides of a licence or every page of a passport, goes in one batch under one label. (The identity panels on the KYC page add "(page 1)", "(page 2)" to each file instead.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upload">
    The button reads **Upload N files** for a batch. Each file is checked and scanned, then appears in the library as it finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  An upload is also refused when a virus is found, or when you hit a limit: 60 successful uploads per hour per person, 100 active documents per member, and your workspace's total storage quota. The error says which, for example **Upload rate limit reached**.
</Warning>

## View documents

The library lists each document's name, type, upload date, uploader and scan status. Each row has a **preview** icon, which opens the file in a new browser tab, and a **download** icon. Viewing needs **View member documents**, which the Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement and Administrator hold.

| Scan status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending scan** | Stored and waiting for its scan result |
| **Clean** | Passed the virus scan |
| **Infected** | The scan flagged the file |
| **Expired** | Past its retention period |
| **Archived** | Moved to archive storage |

## Delete and restore documents

Deleting needs **Delete member documents**. Click the **trash icon** on the row, confirm, and give a reason of at least three characters. The document is hidden from the list but kept in full. Staff with the same permission can turn on **show deleted** to see removed documents and restore one, until its file is purged at the end of its retention period.

## Documents kept on the loan instead

Some files belong to one loan, not the member. They are uploaded on the loan page, follow that loan through every stage, and do not appear in the member's library.

| Document | Where it is uploaded | Who can upload | Who can view |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guarantor ID, income and employment documents | **Guarantors** section, each guarantor's **Documents** block | Staff with **Manage guarantor documents**: Credit Officer, Securities, Administrator | Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement, Credit Manager, Administrator |
| Security documents (hypothecation, insurance assignment, other) | **Collateral** section | Staff with **Upload security documents**: Credit Officer, Securities, Administrator | Staff with **View security documents**: the same reviewer roles |
| Waiver supporting file | **Approval route** card, with the waiver request | Anyone who can request a waiver | Approvers on the loan |
| Committee proof of approval | **Record decision**, on the committee route | The committee recorder | Approvers on the loan |

Guarantor and security documents have the same 10 MB limit, content check and virus scan. A waiver file or committee proof is a PDF, PNG or JPEG.

When a draft is [changed to a different kind of loan](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type), guarantor documents stay on the abandoned draft. Upload them again on the new one.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is the guarantor's ID not in the member's documents?">
    Guarantor documents belong to the loan, not the borrower. Open the loan and look in the Guarantors section.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upload a Word document?">
    No. The upload card accepts PDF, JPEG, PNG, HEIC and WebP. Save or scan the file as a PDF first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a deleted document gone for good?">
    No. It is hidden and kept, and staff with **Delete member documents** can restore it until its file is purged at the end of its retention period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my upload rejected as an unsupported file type?">
    The content is checked, not the name. A file whose content does not match an accepted format is rejected even if its extension looks right.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many files can I upload at once?">
    Up to 25 in one batch, each up to 10 MB.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
