KYC (know your customer) verification confirms a member is who they say they are before your credit union lends to them. You verify a member's tax ID or driver's licence against your national identity service, or record an in-person check at the branch. Every attempt, passed or not, stays on the member's profile permanently.

<Note>
  If your credit union has no automated identity service connected yet, in-person verification is your day-to-day flow. Ask your LOS support team whether an automated service is live for your workspace.
</Note>

<Warning>
  A loan cannot be submitted unless the member's identity status is exactly **Verified**. A member who is **In progress** or in **Manual review** can have a draft, but submission is refused with a message naming the member and their state, for example "Identity verification for Jane Demo is waiting on manual review." Looking at the documents is not verifying: the status on the member's record must read **Verified**.
</Warning>

## Identity statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Unverified** | No check has run. The default for new members. |
| **In progress** | A check was submitted. The result comes back within seconds. |
| **Verified** | Identity confirmed. The member can be linked to a loan. |
| **Failed** | The details did not pass. Check the tax ID or driver's licence number for errors and resubmit. |
| **Manual review** | The automated check could not decide. A reviewer decides from the KYC Review queue. No loans can be submitted until then. |

<Note>
  KYC history is permanent. Every attempt adds a record showing the date, time, result and the staff member who submitted it. History cannot be edited or deleted.
</Note>

## Submit a member for verification

You need the **Submit identity checks** permission, which the Credit Officer and the Administrator hold.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member's profile">
    Search the Members list and click the member's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the KYC page">
    Click **KYC** in the profile header. The page, headed "KYC for" and the member's name, shows the current status and the full history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the identifier you are verifying">
    Two panels sit side by side: one for the member's tax ID (TRN in Jamaica) and one for their **Driver's licence**. Each shows whether a number is on file and lists the identity documents already uploaded for it.

    If a number is missing, use **Add number** on the panel to set it without leaving the page. The tax ID must match the format the field shows, and a driver's licence number is 4 to 12 letters or digits.

    Below them, the **Other identification** card holds every other identity document: passport, national ID, voter's ID and the like. A passport number cannot be added here. Set it on the member's **Edit** page first.

    <Tip>
      Each **Upload** control accepts several files at once, so you can attach both sides of a licence or every page of a passport in one go.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for verification">
    In **Verification type**, choose the tax ID or **Driver's licence**, then click **Submit for verification**. An identifier with no number on file is disabled. The status moves to **In progress**.

    The automated check covers the tax ID and driver's licence only. A passport is confirmed by in-person verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    Within seconds the page shows **Verified**, **Failed** (see retrying, below) or **Manual review** (see manual review, below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify from the New Application page

You do not have to leave loan capture. On **New Application**, when the member you pick is not verified and you have **Submit identity checks**, a **KYC verification** card appears under the member. Capture the documents and run the check there. When the status turns **Verified**, carry on creating the draft.

## Record an in-person verification

Staff with **Submit identity checks** can record an in-person check instead of the automated one. Use it when the member presents identification at the branch and you confirm it against the person. You must provide all three:

- **At least one identifier you checked**: tax ID, **Driver's licence** or **Passport**. The number must already be on file.
- **At least one supporting document** from the member's file. It must be uploaded and have passed its virus scan. An infected, expired or archived document cannot be used.
- **An attestation note** of 10 to 500 characters saying what you confirmed, for example "Customer in branch, IDs match photo, DOB matches."

On submission the status moves to **Verified**. The audit trail records who verified, the documents used and the start of the note.

## Retry a failed verification

**Failed** means the details did not match the identity service's records. Fix the cause before resubmitting.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the identity details">
    Open the profile and click **Edit**. Check the tax ID and driver's licence number for typos, transposed digits or missing characters. Only these two are sent for checking, so a typo elsewhere cannot cause a failure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct and save">
    Update the field and save the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resubmit">
    Return to the KYC page and click **Submit for verification** again. Each submission adds a new record. Earlier attempts stay in the history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manual review

When the automated check cannot decide, for example on an ambiguous match, the status moves to **Manual review** and a task appears in the **KYC Review** queue, under **Review Queue** in the sidebar.

A reviewer with **Review identity checks** (the Credit Manager and the Administrator by default) claims the task, which moves it from the open queue to them, then decides:

- **Approve**: the status moves to **Verified**.
- **Reject**: the status moves to **Failed**. Correct the details and resubmit.
- **Escalate to senior reviewer**: closes the task as escalated and records the chosen senior reviewer and a reason in the audit trail. The member stays in **Manual review**.

During review the profile stays fully visible and editable, and you can keep uploading documents to support the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I checked the member's ID. Why can I still not submit the loan?">
    Checking documents is not verification. Record an in-person verification or run the check on the KYC page so the status becomes **Verified**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a passport be verified automatically?">
    No. Record an in-person verification with the passport number on file and the passport scan attached.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I draft a loan while KYC is in manual review?">
    Yes. Drafting is blocked only for **Unverified** and **Failed**. Submission waits for **Verified**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who resolves a manual review?">
    Staff with **Review identity checks**: the Credit Manager or the Administrator by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a verification be deleted?">
    No. Every attempt stays in the member's KYC history.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
