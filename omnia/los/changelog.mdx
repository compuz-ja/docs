This is the running history of what changed in the Loan Origination System, by date with the newest release at the top. Each entry describes what staff notice in day-to-day work. Open an entry to see the detail.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="26 October 2026 · Correct a legal document: re-prepare before signing, void after">
    A clear answer to "what happens when a document has an error?". How you fix it depends on whether it has been signed.

    - **Re-prepare an unsigned document.** A document at **Prepared, awaiting signature** has a **Re-prepare** button. It builds a fresh copy from the loan's current details and marks the old one superseded. On the Declaration of Purpose it reopens the purposes editor first. A signed copy is never re-prepared over.
    - **Void a signed document.** A signed document has a **Void** button for supervisors: the Adjudicator, the Credit Manager, and the Administrator. A reason of at least 10 characters is required and recorded. The person who recorded the signed document, or signed as checker on a disbursement sheet, cannot void it. The slot then reads **signed copy voided, prepare again**.
    - **A void never undoes a stage already passed.** A document needed to leave Securities can be voided up to and including Securities; once the loan is in Disbursement, send it back to Securities first. Every other document can be voided until the loan is disbursed. Nothing on a closed loan can be voided.
    - Nothing is deleted. Voided and superseded copies stay on file for the audit trail.

    See [Correcting documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/correcting-documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="26 October 2026 · A branded Loan Disbursement Sheet with a masked member account">
    The Loan Disbursement Sheet now looks like your credit union's own document and no longer carries the member's full account number.

    - **Your branding.** The sheet prints your workspace logo with your credit union's legal name above the title.
    - **Masked accounts.** The member's account prints as its last five digits only, and the holding account is masked the same way. The full number is not stored with the document at all.
    - **A footer on every page** carries your credit union's short name and the product name. Set the short name under **Administration**, then **Settings**, then **Branding**.
    - **A prepared-by stamp** under the footer says who prepared the sheet and when.
    - **Cleaner sign-off.** Prepared by, Disbursed by, and Checked by each have a signature line and their own date line.

    See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="26 October 2026 · See what is waiting for you, and what is stopping each loan">
    Two changes that answer "who has this loan, what is holding it, and who gets it next" without opening every queue.

    **Knowing when work arrives**

    - Every queue in the menu, Credit Approvals, Securities, Disbursement, KYC Review, and **Returned to Me**, shows a live count. A count turns amber when the oldest loan in it has waited more than a day.
    - A **Waiting for you** list on your dashboard and on **My Work** names the loans that are your next action, oldest first, with how long each has waited.
    - A **notification bell** in the top bar lists your latest 20 alerts, with mark read and mark all read.
    - A **morning email** each weekday lists up to 10 loans waiting for you. Credit Managers also get a team summary.
    - An Administrator can switch on **Teams posts** per queue, so a short card (loan ID, stage, amount, never member details) lands in a Microsoft Teams channel when a loan arrives.

    **Guidance on the loan page**

    - A **next-step banner** says which stage the loan is in, who can act on it now, and who gets it next.
    - **What's stopping this loan** lists every unmet requirement, from identity verification and required fields to unsigned documents.
    - After an action, the confirmation names who was notified.
    - A **timeline** shows when the loan entered each stage, who moved it, and how long it spent there.
    - A pinned **action bar** offers the one next action you can take, and sections not relevant to your role fold away, remembering your choice.

    See [Finding your work](/omnia/los/navigation/finding-your-work), [Alerts and email](/omnia/los/navigation/alerts-and-email), and [The loan page](/omnia/los/navigation/the-loan-page).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="26 October 2026 · Income counted by its applicable share in the TDSR">
    Each type of income can now count toward affordability at the share your credit union's policy sets, for example salary in full and allowances at a reduced share.

    - Each income line on the capture screen shows the share that counts, and total income, money left over, and the TDSR use the counted income, with the gross shown beneath when it differs.
    - The TDSR on the capture screen is the same figure recorded at submission, to the cent.

    See [Affordability and TDSR](/omnia/los/loans/affordability-and-tdsr).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="22 to 23 October 2026 · Fees per loan, changing loan type, creditor-life payment, and the Securities hand-off">
    **Capture**

    - **Edit a fee for one loan.** On a draft, the Credit Officer can replace a scheduled fee's rate or amount, with an optional reason. The line shows **Edited** beside the schedule default, and **Reset to schedule** undoes it. The disbursement screen starts from the edited figure. See [Fees and insurance at capture](/omnia/los/loans/fees-and-insurance-at-capture).
    - **Change loan type or product on a draft.** Within the same kind of loan the draft changes in place and keeps its loan number. Changing to a different kind creates a new draft with a new loan number, carries the shared details across, and abandons the old draft. See [Changing loan type](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type).
    - **Choose how the creditor-life premium is paid.** Set the number of lives covered and whether the member pays **annually** or **in full**. Annually, the first year's premium is collected at disbursement and a monthly share of it counts in the TDSR. In full, the whole premium is collected at disbursement and does not count in the TDSR. Neither is added to the monthly instalment. The premium can be priced from your insurer's rate card, and the loan agreement prints it as its own line.
    - **One TDSR.** The capture screen and submission compute the TDSR the same way, so the two never disagree.

    **Securities and disbursement**

    - **Loans no longer stall in Securities.** Signing the last document Securities is waiting on, by upload or e-signature, moves the loan to Disbursement straight away.
    - **The preparer can record a wet-signed Disbursement Sheet.** The upload asks who signed as **Checked by**, leaves the preparer out of the list, and confirms the named officer is current staff who can check a sheet.
    - The Disbursement dashboard's **Awaiting disbursement** tile now matches the queue's **Pending** count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="16 October 2026 · A new name on screen, and consistent pricing">
    - The app header and browser tabs show a new product name. An Administrator can set the short label after it to your own product name under **Administration**, then **Settings**, then **Branding**.
    - **One rule for which rate table prices a product.** The estimate at capture, the draft agreement, and approval now use the same table, so officers no longer see a rate that approval then refuses.
    - A cash-secured draft loan agreement now generates. If the security is not captured yet, the message says to add it.
    - The progress panel on a draft now applies your required fields, so it agrees with what submission checks.
    - Approval authority is checked when a loan is claimed or assigned for approval, not only at the decision.
    - One email address that the mail service rejects no longer holds up other emails.
    - A next-model-year vehicle is priced as new.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="13 to 14 October 2026 · Approval authority, committees, waivers, and required fields">
    Approval now follows your credit union's delegation of authority.

    - **Approval authority.** Each approving role has its own limit by type of security. An approver above their limit cannot approve.
    - **Three approval routes.** A loan is approved by one approver within their limit, by an **In-House Committee** whose members each record a vote until the quorum is met, or as a **Credit Committee** decision recorded with the meeting date, an optional minute number, and the proof of approval.
    - **Maker-checker on approval.** When switched on, the person who captured a loan, or who changed its rate, cannot approve it.
    - **Waivers.** Request a waiver with a justification and an optional file. Every waiver approver decides it, any rejection rejects it, and the requester never decides their own. A pending waiver holds the approval.
    - **Product-limit exceptions.** An amount within a set margin above a product's maximum can be kept with a reason and goes to the named exception approver. Beyond the margin it is refused.
    - **Staff borrowing limits.** A staff loan above the limit your credit union sets for the borrower's classification needs an approved waiver first.
    - **Required fields.** Your credit union names the member, loan, guarantor, and security fields that must be filled. A member cannot be saved, and a loan cannot be submitted, with one blank. Existing incomplete members are flagged, not blocked. The collateral panels gained fields such as vehicle colour, forced sale value, valuator, title reference, and peril insurance.

    See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes) and [Confidential and staff loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="9 to 10 October 2026 · Clearer refusals and smoother hand-offs">
    - **Approved loans always reach Securities.** The hand-off to Securities now always completes, and the loan waits there for its documents.
    - **Refusals say why.** When a loan cannot move, the message names the reason and what to do: which member's identity verification is outstanding, how many security requests are open, or which document for which party is unsigned.
    - **Submit tells you about identity verification.** A draft no longer says **Ready to submit** while the member's verification is in progress or waiting on review.
    - **The approval screen lists outstanding documents** and who can sign them, before the decision.
    - **Cash-secured advance limits follow your credit policy**, with a separate limit when any of the security is held at another institution. A mixed pledge takes the stricter figure.
    - A cash pledge records the date its balance was confirmed, not a valuation date.
    - Pressing approve twice now says the decision was already recorded, rather than suggesting it failed.
    - Loan agreements open in the browser again, and attachment links in support emails work for 72 hours.

    - A confidential loan appears only to the roles your policy names, the borrower, and the person it is assigned to. Nobody else sees it in the loans list, the queues, reports, or notification emails.
    - Linking a member to their staff login, and setting the classification, needs its own permission, **Manage member identity links**, separate from editing a member.

    See [Confidential and staff loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).

A loan pledging several cash accounts is priced at its highest tier. See [Rate tables](/omnia/los/admin/rate-tables#pricing-a-loan-with-several-cash-accounts).

    - **Group creditor-life pricing.** Creditor-life cover can be priced from your insurer's rate card by the number of lives covered.

  <Accordion title="20 July 2026 · A revised loan agreement">
    The generated loan agreement was revised across all four templates (cash secured, unsecured, motor vehicle, and real estate). How it is generated, downloaded, and required before disbursement is unchanged. What changed is what the member reads and signs.

    - **The full monthly payment, itemised:** principal and interest, compulsory savings, and the creditor-life premium where it applies, with the total the member pays each month. The total matches the affordability figure the officer sees at capture.
    - **The security, stated plainly.** A secured agreement leads with the type of security and its details, before the clause that grants it.
    - **When the first payment is due.** Agreements state that the first payment is due thirty days after disbursement, since the agreement is drawn up before disbursement. The cash-secured agreement keeps its effective date.
    - **The signature page** lists the member, any guarantor, the Credit Officer, and the Branch Manager, each with a date. The Adjudicator is no longer a signatory. Each page carries the member's initials, and the agreement closes with an acknowledgement by the member and each guarantor that they have read and understood it.
    - **Credit-bureau consent and a change-in-circumstances clause** are included.
    - **Your legal name.** The name used for the credit union in the opening sentence and on the signature lines is configurable, so it can be your registered name even where the letterhead shows a trading name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="20 July 2026 · Affordability before and after, consolidation fees, escrow shares, and credit ratings">
    - **Affordability before and after the loan.** When a loan pays off or consolidates an existing debt, set what that debt's payment becomes afterwards: zero if paid in full, or a smaller figure for a partial consolidation, with a one-tap **paid off** option. The TDSR and the money left over then reflect the member's position after the loan, and the capture screen shows the before and after side by side.
    - **Top-up and consolidation fees on the new money only.** A loan marked as a top-up or consolidation charges the processing fee on the new money the member receives, not on the balance being refinanced. Other fees are unchanged.
    - **Escrow shares.** A product can require the member to hold a number of months of the loan payment in shares as security. The number of months can vary by how much the member saves, and an officer can override it on a loan.
    - **Credit rating and risk registry.** An officer can record the member's credit rating from a credit report, with the score and source; it feeds the risk assessment at submission. The officer can also record the risk registry result, clear or adverse. An adverse listing is flagged for the approver.
    - **Creditor-life cover stays as you set it.** Turning cover off saves reliably and shows the same choice at every later stage.
    - **A use-of-funds authorization.** You can add a use-of-funds form as one of your own templates, so the member signs one document authorizing the fees, the amounts held as security, and any consolidation payoff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="20 July 2026 · Credit reports and your own forms">
    **Reports**

    - A new **Reports** area under **Dashboards**, for anyone with reporting access, gives you named, period-based reports to read on screen, print, or download as a spreadsheet, such as new loans, net cash disbursed, branch monthly lending, and credit committee reports. Pick a report and a month or year; a Branch Manager sees only their own branch.
    - The figures come straight from the loans in the LOS, so a report is always current.

    **Bring your own forms**

    - An administrator can upload **any** of your own forms as a template, for example an authority to deliver a vehicle title or a salary deduction order, map its blanks to the loan's details once, and the LOS fills and generates it per loan.
    - On the loan, an **Additional forms** card generates each of these with one click. These forms never block a loan from moving forward.

    Payoff and delinquency reporting stay in your Core Banking System.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="20 July 2026 · Loan products become a full product sheet">
    A loan product now holds its whole product sheet, so what you set up in Administration matches the product sheets your credit union already writes.

    - **Audience: General or Staff.** A product can be marked **Staff** so staff-only offerings are grouped and filtered as their own set. It is an eligibility label, not a loan type, so pricing and risk are unchanged.
    - **Fees per product.** Attach specific fees from your fee schedule, set a different amount for this product only, or waive a fee while keeping it on the sheet for the record. A product with no fee settings uses the fees for its loan type. At disbursement the officer sees these fees pre-filled and can still adjust them.
    - **Minimum shares per product**, overriding the loan type's figure. Leave it blank to inherit.
    - **Required supporting documents.** List the documents a product asks for, like a job letter, recent payslips, or a valuation report, and mark each **Required** or **Recommended**. The list shows on the loan as a checklist.

    See [Loan products](/omnia/los/admin/loan-products).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="19 to 20 July 2026 · Electronic signing">
    A member no longer has to sign on paper. They can sign on screen in the branch, or from a secure link on their own device, and you can still print, wet-sign, and upload whenever a member prefers ink.

    - **Sign in the branch.** A prepared consent form carries a **Sign electronically** button next to **Upload signed**. The member signs and confirms the consent statement, and the form is recorded as signed with the signature embedded in the PDF. Deeds that need a witness or a justice of the peace still print and upload, and guarantors sign on paper.
    - **Draw, type, or upload a signature.** The member can draw it, type their name and pick a signature style, or upload a photo of their signature, and sees a preview first. The signature sits on the document's own signature line.
    - **Accept the loan terms before approval.** The officer can generate a **draft loan agreement** with a clear watermark showing the proposed amount, term, rate, and repayment. The member e-signs it to accept the terms. This does not approve the loan; the binding [loan agreement](/omnia/los/loans/loan-agreements) is generated after approval.
    - **Sign remotely.** Click **Email signing link** and the member receives a secure, single-use link that expires in 72 hours, plus a one-time code by email that expires in 10 minutes. The member must be identity-verified with an email address on file. Signing emails carry your credit union's name and branding.
    - **Officers sign the Disbursement Sheet.** A second officer can e-sign the Loan Disbursement Sheet as the checker. The officer who prepared the sheet cannot sign it off.
    - **On the record.** Every signature is embedded in the signed PDF, kept with a signed receipt of exactly what was agreed and when, and recorded in the [audit trail](/omnia/los/compliance/audit-trail) without the member's name or the signature image.
    - New permissions, **Request e-signatures** and **View documents**, govern who can facilitate signing and see its status.

    See [Member signing](/omnia/los/legal-documents/signing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="15 July 2026 · Capture, fees, collateral holds, and faster emails">
    **Capture**

    - A member can be identified by their driver's licence, alongside the other identification types.
    - The statement of affairs records every money figure to the exact cent.
    - The new loan's own monthly repayment shows in the statement of affairs and counts in the TDSR.
    - The estimated **monthly repayment** and the **interest rate** appear in the **Loan purpose and type** section, so the officer sees the cost while capturing.
    - Every section of a draft saves on its own as you go, including the officer's recommendation.

    **Fees, rates, and shares**

    - Each loan carries a plain-language explanation of how its interest rate was worked out.
    - The Credit Officer can choose how each fee is treated and add one-off fees to a specific loan.
    - Creditor-life insurance no longer applies to cash-secured loans.
    - A workspace-wide **Mandatory Shares** amount sets the flat shares a member must hold, changeable only by your administrator.
    - A separate **Minimum Shares** figure is a percentage of the loan and can differ by product.
    - Each fee can be posted to a chosen general ledger account on the disbursement sheet, and the entries stay balanced. See [Fees, deductions, and tax](/omnia/los/disbursement/fees-and-gct).

    **Securities, collateral, and documents**

    - Cash-secured loans with the cash held at another institution can carry their own supporting documents.
    - Common security request items, like a vehicle title, an insurance binder, or a valuation, are offered as **quick-add chips**. See [Securities](/omnia/los/workflows/securities).
    - Reviewers can view an applicant's identity numbers **masked**, with every reveal recorded in the audit trail.
    - Income and employment documents show on the review screens, and a Credit Manager gets a full view of the supporting documents behind a loan.
    - A new **Collateral and security holds** section lets Securities or Disbursement record or adjust collateral and place a **hold** (a lien) on the member's own shares or savings, so pledged funds cannot be withdrawn while the loan is outstanding. The hold shows on the member's profile until it is released.
    - When a loan is returned for more information, the approver's name is hidden from the Credit Officer, keeping the exchange impartial.

    **Email**

    - Password-reset and email-verification messages arrive promptly again.

    **Roles**

    - Securities, Disbursement, the Credit Manager, and the Administrator can record collateral and place or release holds. The Credit Officer, Adjudicator, and Branch Manager can view holds. See [Roles and permissions](/omnia/los/access/roles-and-permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="13 July 2026 · Import your existing loan book">
    Bring the loans you already have into the LOS, each one recorded as it was originally made and landing at its true historical stage. This is a one-time import of loans you already made: no servicing, no repayments, no balances.

    - **Two ways in.** A **bulk workbook import** takes a spreadsheet of your loans, one row per loan, with linked sheets for guarantors, collateral, and payees. **Guided single-loan entry** keys one paper loan at a time and attaches a scan of the file. See [The workbook](/omnia/los/migration/workbook).
    - **The batch console.** Every import becomes a batch under **Administration**, then **Migrations**. Upload and stage the loans, review each one and attribute its officers, check the count and total against what you expected, then commit. Amounts and dates are echoed back in plain form so a mistake is caught before anything commits. Commit takes every ready loan, or only the ones you tick, and picks up safely if interrupted. See [Loan migration](/omnia/los/migration/overview).
    - **Former staff.** An officer who has left is credited for their work in reports, with no login and no way to be assigned new work.
    - **True history in reports.** A loan disbursed years ago counts in the year it was disbursed, not the month you imported it.
    - **Signed attestation.** After a batch commits, you can produce a tamper-evident, digitally signed certificate of what came in: how many loans, their total value, whether the totals reconciled, and who uploaded and signed off. It is stored in write-once storage and downloadable from the batch.
    - **Who can run it.** The Administrator, Credit Manager, Credit Officer, and Branch Manager. Sign-off is optional, and anyone with access can commit the ready loans.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5 July 2026 · Loan legal documents and form templates">
    Your credit union's own legal forms now live in the LOS. Upload each form once, map its blanks to loan data, and the LOS prepares it for every loan, ready to sign and file.

    - **Legal documents on the loan.** Every loan carries a **Legal documents** card listing exactly the forms that loan needs, such as credit consents for the borrower and each guarantor, a guarantee per guarantor, a security agreement on vehicle loans, a declaration of purpose where your credit union requires one, and the Loan Disbursement Sheet.
    - **Prepare, then upload signed.** **Prepare** fills your template with the loan's data and produces the PDF; **Upload signed** files the signed scan back against the loan. Each row shows whether the form is required, prepared and awaiting signature, or signed.
    - **The paperwork gates the workflow.** Securities cannot hand a loan to Disbursement until its required documents are signed, and Disbursement cannot mark a loan disbursed until the Loan Disbursement Sheet is signed. See [Legal documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/overview).
    - **Maker is never checker** on the Loan Disbursement Sheet: the checker must differ from the preparer and the person disbursing.
    - **Document templates.** A new **Document Templates** page lets an Administrator or Credit Manager register a form, choose how it fills, map its fields, preview it against sample data, and activate it. Active templates cannot be edited; a new version supersedes the old one, which stays on file. A form can only print the loan data allowed for it. See [Document templates](/omnia/los/legal-documents/templates).
    - **Permissions.** Stage staff prepare and file the documents for their stage, voiding is a supervisory action, and everyone in the pipeline can view. The Administrator and Credit Manager manage templates.
  </Accordion>

    **Stage timing by officer**

    - The Analytics page gained **Stage timing by officer**: how long loans sit with each person at each stage, with averages, slowest cases, and a per-loan timeline you can click into. Branch Managers see only their branch. See [Analytics dashboards](/omnia/los/admin/analytics-dashboards).

    **Creditor-life premium**

    - The premium estimate shows per month, per year, and over the term, and the three reconcile. The manual premium field is labelled as the total premium for the term.

  <Accordion title="13 June 2026 · Service fees, insurance, and guarantor documents">
    **Fees and tax**

    - The processing fee is charged at the rate your credit union sets and is taxed like legal and valuation fees. Insurance premiums stay tax-exempt.
    - A new **Fee Schedule** page lets an Administrator or Credit Manager change any standing fee in place: its rate, whether tax applies, how it is treated, and whether it is active. See [Edit the fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule).
    - The application shows a read-only **Fees that will apply** card with total fees, tax, and the net to the member before anything is submitted.

    **Creditor-life insurance**

    - Cover is configurable on each loan: whether it applies, the sum insured, level or reducing cover, and an optional manual premium. The premium feeds the total monthly payment and the TDSR.

    **Guarantors**

    - Each guarantor has their own documents for identification, proof of income, and an employment letter, visible downstream to the same people who can see the member's documents.

    **Members and KYC**

    - A passport can back a manual identity verification, confirmed in person.
    - The KYC page gained an **Other identification** card for passports, national IDs, voter IDs, and the like.
    - Identity uploads take several files at once, with a label per page for multi-page documents.

    **Cash-secured loans**

    - A member can pledge more than one account against one loan, for example shares plus a fixed deposit.
    - The advance limit is checked against the combined pledged cash, and submission is blocked if the amount runs over.

    - Monthly expenses are captured by category, with quick-add chips and a live total income, expenses, net monthly, and TDSR.
    - Mandatory shares show as their own tile, and the rate shown reflects the applicant's risk band.
    - Real estate amount and term limits come from the loan product, which can allow terms up to 480 months.
