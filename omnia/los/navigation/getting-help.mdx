Three places answer the questions staff ask most: the **How Loans Move** page inside your workspace, the **Support** button in the top bar, and **Ask LOS Help** on this documentation site. Start with the one closest to your question.

| Your question | Go to |
|---|---|
| "What happens if Securities sends this back?" | How Loans Move |
| "Something is broken" or "I need a feature" | Support |
| "How do I do this in the system?" | Ask LOS Help, or search these docs |

## The How Loans Move page

**How Loans Move** is a map of every stage a loan passes through and every supported path between them. Open it from **Loan Workflow** in the left menu, or from the **How this works** link on any loan's [next-step banner](/omnia/los/navigation/the-loan-page#the-next-step-banner), which opens the page at that loan's stage.

Every signed-in staff member can open it. It shows the process, not loan data, so there is nothing on it a role needs to be kept from.

<Frame caption="Each stage card lists every way out of the stage. You can do this marks the moves your role is allowed to make.">
  <img src="/images/omnia/los/nav/workflow-map.png" alt="How Loans Move page showing stage cards for Draft, Submitted and Approval, each listing the paths out of the stage with labels like Automatic and You can do this" />
</Frame>

The page has three parts:

- **The main path**: Draft, Submitted, Approval, Approved, Securities, Disbursement, Disbursed, Filed. Each card says who works the stage and what happens there.
- **Branches: when a file steps aside**: Info requested and Securities issue, and how a loan gets back on the path.
- **End states: where a file comes to rest**: Filed, Declined, Cancelled and Abandoned.

Each path out of a stage is labelled:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Moves forward** or **Sends back** | The direction of the move |
| **Automatic** | The platform takes the step with no one clicking |
| **Conditional** | The step only happens under a stated condition, shown in italics underneath |
| **Fresh approval** | The prior approval no longer holds once the loan takes this path, so it must be approved again |
| **You can do this** | Your role is allowed to make this move |

At the bottom, **Signing documents** explains how loan documents are prepared, signed and recorded, and that a loan moves on by itself once every document its stage needs is on file.

The page is drawn from the workflow the platform runs, so it always matches what the system does.

## Send a support request

Click **Support** in the top bar to open **Get support**. Any signed-in staff member can send a request. It goes to the CompuZign support team, not to your own Administrator.

<Frame caption="Pick a category, give it a subject and the details, and attach screenshots if they help.">
  <img src="/images/omnia/los/nav/support-request.png" alt="Get support form with Bug, Feature request and Feedback buttons, a Subject field, a Details box and an attachments drop zone" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what it is about">
    Under **What is this about?**, pick **Bug**, **Feature request** or **Feedback**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a subject">
    A short summary, between 3 and 200 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    What happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it, or the feature you would like. Between 10 and 5,000 characters. Include the loan ID and the [version number](/omnia/los/navigation/finding-your-work#which-version-you-are-on) at the top left of the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach screenshots">
    Up to **5** files, **10 MB** each. PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC or PDF. Drag them in or click to choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send request**. A confirmation appears, and you receive an acknowledgement email with a reference number.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every attachment is checked before it is stored: the file contents must match the file type, and it must pass a virus scan. A file that fails either check is refused, with the file name in the message.

You can send **5** requests an hour. After that, wait a little before sending another.

### After you send it

The request is saved in your workspace's audit trail with its category, subject and attachment count. The support team receives it by email with secure links to your attachments, and follows up with you. Quote the reference number from your acknowledgement in any follow-up. There is no need to send the same request twice.

There is no list of your past requests inside the system in this release. The acknowledgement email is your record.

<Warning>
  Screenshots of a loan show a member's details. Crop to the part that shows the problem, and leave out anything the support team does not need to see.
</Warning>

## Ask LOS Help

Every page of this documentation site has an **Ask LOS Help** button at the bottom right. Ask it a question in plain words, like "how do I send a loan back to Securities?", and it answers from the documentation and links you to the page it used.

It searches these docs only. It cannot see your workspace, your loans or your members, so ask it how something works, not what is happening to a particular loan. For that, open the loan's [next-step banner](/omnia/los/navigation/the-loan-page#the-next-step-banner), or send a support request.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can open the How Loans Move page?">
    Every signed-in staff member in your workspace. It needs no special capability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my Administrator see my support requests?">
    No. Requests go to the CompuZign support team. They are recorded in your workspace's audit trail, which your auditors and oversight roles can read.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My screenshot was refused. Why?">
    It is over 10 MB, it is not one of the accepted types (PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC or PDF), its contents do not match its file type, or it failed the virus scan. The message names the file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Ask LOS Help tell me why my loan is stuck?">
    No. It reads the documentation, not your workspace. The loan's next-step banner lists exactly what is stopping it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I check on a request I sent?">
    Contact CompuZign support and quote the reference number from your acknowledgement email. Do not reply to the acknowledgement itself: it comes from an address nobody reads. There is no in-system list of past requests in this release.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
