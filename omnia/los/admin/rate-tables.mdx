Rate tables hold the interest rates your credit union charges, by risk band, collateral, and vehicle age.

Rate tables are **versioned**. You can publish a change now or schedule it for a future date, and every past version is kept, so you can always tell which rate applied on any day.

**Required permission:** **Manage rates**, held by the Credit Manager and the Administrator.

Open **Administration → Loan Rates**.

## The four rate sources

Each loan product uses one of four rate sources, chosen on the product.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flat rate" icon="equal">
    One fixed rate for every loan on the product, whatever the borrower's risk or collateral. You set it on the product itself, not in the rate tables.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Risk band" icon="scale">
    The rate follows the borrower's risk band, R1 (lowest risk) to R5 (highest). There are two band tables: **Risk band rates** for most products and **Real estate (mortgage) rates** for property loans, which are usually lower because property secures them.

    **Example:** R1 = 9.00%, R2 = 11.00%, R3 = 13.00%, R4 = 15.00%, R5 = 18.00%
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collateral type" icon="landmark">
    The rate follows what secures the loan: Vehicle, Shares, Cash hypothecation, Funds held externally, or Property. The three cash labels are tiers by who holds the pledged money: the member's shares, another deposit at your credit union, or funds held at another institution.

    **Example:** Shares = 7.99%, Cash hypothecation = 8.99%, Funds held externally = 9.99%
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vehicle age" icon="car">
    The rate rises with the vehicle's age. Each tier reads "Up to N years old" with a rate, where N is 0 to 100. A vehicle takes the first tier it fits; older vehicles take the last tier. Age is this year minus the model year. Next year's model counts as brand new. A model year further ahead is refused as a typing error.

    **Example:** Up to 4 years old = 9.50%, up to 9 years old = 11.50%, up to 30 years old = 14.50%
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Enter rates as percentages with up to two decimal places, such as `14.50`. Rates run from 0.00% to 100.00%.

## Pricing a loan with several cash accounts

A cash-secured loan can pledge shares, another deposit, and funds held elsewhere all at once. The whole loan is priced at the **highest** cash tier present, in this order:

1. Funds held externally
2. Cash hypothecation
3. Shares

So shares plus any other cash account prices at the other account's rate. The order the officer entered the accounts never matters. Vehicle and property loans pledge one thing and are priced on it.

<Warning>
  Publish a rate for every collateral label your products use. If a loan needs a label your table has no rate for, approval cannot price it and says so. If a whole table a product needs is missing, the platform fills it with placeholder rates so loans are not stuck, and warns your support team. The placeholders are not your rates. Publish your own as soon as you notice.
</Warning>

## Which table prices a product

The capture screen, the draft agreement, and approval all use the same rule:

1. The rate table assigned to the product, at the version in effect today.
2. If the product has none, your workspace's current rate table.

So the rate an officer sees at capture is the rate approval will set, unless the table changes in between.

Your rate table can also hold a group creditor-life insurance rate card, priced per thousand of cover by the number of lives covered. It has no editor here; your LOS support team loads it from your insurer's rate card. See [Fees and insurance at capture](/omnia/los/loans/fees-and-insurance-at-capture#how-the-premium-is-priced).

## When the rate is fixed

A loan's rate is fixed the moment it is approved, from the table in effect that day. Later table changes never touch it.

- **New approvals** use the table in effect on the approval date.
- **Loans already approved** keep their rate.
- **Reports on past loans** can always show the right rate, because every version is kept with its effective date.

## Overriding a loan's rate

Staff with **Override rates** (the Credit Manager and the Administrator) can set a different rate on one loan, for a staff loan, promotional pricing, or a retention offer. Do it from the loan itself, not this page.

- An override can be set or cleared only while the loan is a **Draft**. After that the platform refuses with "Rate overrides can only be set while the application is a draft."
- A reason is required, 3 to 280 characters.
- The rate must be between 0% and 100%.

At approval the override replaces the rate-table rate. The rate the table would have given is still recorded alongside it, and setting or clearing an override is recorded in the audit trail. When your credit union turns the rule on, the person who set the override cannot approve that loan. See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes#maker-checker-rules-on-approval).

## Publishing a rate change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Loan Rates">
    The rates in effect today are shown as percentages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the rates">
    Change the values you need. For vehicle age, use **Add tier** to add rows (at least 1, up to 12).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it applies">
    Under **When to apply**, leave **Immediately** selected, or choose **Schedule for** and pick a future date. You cannot back-date a change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish new rates**, or **Schedule rate change** for a future date, then confirm. The current rates stay in force until the new version takes effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Publishing changes only the tables you edited. Editing the risk bands, for example, leaves the real estate, vehicle-age, and collateral tables exactly as they were.

The **Version history** table at the bottom of the page lists every version, who published it and their role, when it took effect, and what changed. Versions are never deleted.

## Scheduled changes

A future-dated version shows as **Scheduled** in the version history.

<Accordion title="Can I cancel a scheduled rate change?">
  Yes. Publish a new version with the right values. The scheduled one is marked **Cancelled** and never applies. Both stay in the history.
</Accordion>
