A loan product is one lending offering your credit union makes to members, with its own code, loan type, pricing method, limits, and product sheet.

Credit Officers see only active products when they start an application, so this list decides what your team can lend.

**Required permission:** **Manage loan products**, held by the Credit Manager and the Administrator. Everyone who captures loans can see the product list, but only these roles see **New**, **Edit**, and **Archive**.

Open **Administration → Products**.

</Frame>

<Note>
  Set up your loan types first. Every product belongs to a loan type, and with no active loan types the form says "No loan types yet. Add one under Loan types first, then come back to create this product." See [Loan types](/omnia/los/admin/loan-types).
</Note>

## What a product contains

| Field | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Code** | A short, unique identifier such as `PUBLIC-PL` or `MORTGAGE-STD`. Uppercase letters, numbers, and dashes, 2 to 20 characters. Shown in the product list and the audit trail |
| **Name** | What Credit Officers see in the new-application list, up to 80 characters. Pick a name your team will recognise |
| **Loan type** | The loan type the product belongs to. Its security basis (Unsecured, Cash secured, Motor vehicle, or Real estate) decides the collateral fields and the loan agreement used |
| **Rate source** | How the interest rate is set: **Flat rate**, **Risk band**, **Collateral type**, or **Vehicle age**. See [Rate sources](#rate-sources) below |
| **Default rate** | For **Flat rate** products only, the annual rate every loan gets, such as `12.00` |
| **Minimum and maximum amount** | The loan amounts a Credit Officer can request. Leave blank for no limit. The minimum cannot exceed the maximum |
| **Minimum and maximum term** | The repayment period in months, each from 1 to 480. Leave blank for no limit |
| **Description** | An optional internal note, up to 500 characters. Not shown to Credit Officers |

When your credit union allows product-limit exceptions, an amount a set margin above the maximum can be kept with a reason and goes to the approver your policy names. Beyond the margin it is refused. See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes#product-limit-exceptions).

## The product sheet

The rest of the product sheet is optional and sits on the same form.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | **General** (any member) or **Staff**. A label for grouping and filtering. A staff product keeps its normal security basis and pricing |
| **Repayment method** | Salary deduction, Over-the-counter payment, Automatic transfer, or Standing order. Tick all that apply. Shown to staff at capture |
| **Repayment frequency** | Weekly, Fortnightly, or Monthly. For reference; the payment is still worked out monthly |
| **Minimum shares** | The shares a member must hold, as a percentage of the loan. Overrides the loan type's figure for this product only. Leave blank to use the loan type's figure |
| **Compulsory savings** | The monthly compulsory-savings minimum for this product. The member saves the greater of this minimum and a share of the monthly payment set for your credit union. It counts in the affordability check at submission and prints on the loan agreement. Leave blank for your credit union's default |
| **Approvals required** | How many approvers the product calls for. Shown to approvers as guidance; the binding rules come from your approval routing |
| **Applicable fees** | The fees that apply to this product. See [Fees per product](#setting-fees-per-product) |
| **Required supporting documents** | The documents the member must provide. See [Required supporting documents](#required-supporting-documents) |
| **Product specifications** | Free-form label and detail rows for anything else: purpose, target market, special terms, risk notes |

Repayment, approvals-required, and specifications are information for staff. They do not change pricing or what the workflow allows. Compulsory savings does change the member's monthly obligation.

### Setting fees per product

A product uses the fees set for its loan type on the [Fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule) unless you add fee rows to the product:

- **Attach a fee.** Pick a fee from your fee schedule.
- **Change the amount.** Enter a percentage or flat amount for this product only. The fee schedule keeps its own figure.
- **Waive a fee.** Keep it on the sheet for the record at zero.

Once a product has any fee rows, those rows are its whole fee set. New fees are created on the fee schedule, where each also gets its ledger account and tax setting.

On the loan, the Credit Officer sees these fees at capture and can change one for that loan while it is a draft. At funding, the Disbursement officer starts from those figures and can still adjust or waive them. See [Fees, deductions, and tax](/omnia/los/disbursement/fees-and-gct#where-the-starting-figures-come-from).

### Required supporting documents

List the documents the product needs, such as a completed application form, photo ID, a job letter, recent payslips, or proof of address. Mark each **Required** or **Recommended**. A one-click button adds a standard set you can then edit.

The list appears on each loan as a checklist so the Credit Officer knows what to collect. The documents themselves are uploaded in the member and income document cards on the loan.

## Finding a product

Search by code or name, filter by loan type or rate source, and switch between **All**, **Active**, and **Archived**. Each tab shows a count. Each row shows how many applications have used the product, so dormant products stand out. The list loads 20 at a time; click **Load more** for the rest.

## Creating a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make sure a loan type exists">
    If none are active, add one under **Administration → Loan types** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New product">
    On **Administration → Products**, click **New product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a code and name">
    The code must be unique in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the loan type">
    The product takes its security basis from the loan type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the rate source">
    For **Flat rate**, enter the rate in **Default rate**. For the others, the rate comes from your rate table when the loan is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set amount and term limits">
    For example, a term of `12` to `60` months for a one-to-five-year product. Leave a limit blank to skip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the product sheet">
    Audience, repayment, shares, compulsory savings, fees, and documents, as needed. All of it can change later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create product">
    The product appears in the new-application list straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing a product

Click the product's row, or **Edit** on the row, change what you need, and click **Save changes**.

Every field can change, including the loan type and rate source. Change the loan type deliberately: it changes the product's security basis. Loans read the product live, so a new name, code, or limit shows on loans already using it. A loan not yet approved is priced at approval with the edited settings. The amount and term on each application stay as captured, and a loan's rate is fixed only once it is approved. Every save is recorded in the audit trail.

## Archiving and restoring a product

Archive a product you no longer offer. It disappears from the new-application list but stays on every loan that used it.

- **To archive:** find the product and click **Archive**. It moves to the **Archived** tab.
- **To restore:** open the **Archived** tab, find the product, and click **Restore**. It returns to the new-application list.

Both are recorded in the audit trail.

<Accordion title="What happens to loans that used an archived product?">
  Nothing. Archiving only stops new applications. A product that loans use can never be deleted, and it stays visible on those loans. Loans show the product's current details, not a copy from when they were made. A loan still in progress is priced at approval against the product as it is then.
</Accordion>

## Rate sources

| Rate source | Best for | How the rate is set |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat rate** | Simple fixed-rate products | The rate on the product applies to every loan |
| **Risk band** | Unsecured and mortgage loans | From your rate table by the borrower's risk band, R1 to R5. Real estate products use a separate band table |
| **Collateral type** | Cash secured and other collateral-priced loans | From your rate table by collateral: Vehicle, Shares, Cash hypothecation, Funds held externally, or Property. A loan pledging several cash accounts is priced at its highest cash tier |
| **Vehicle age** | Motor vehicle loans | From your rate table by the vehicle's age in years |

See [Rate tables](/omnia/los/admin/rate-tables).
