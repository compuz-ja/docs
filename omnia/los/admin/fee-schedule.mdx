The fee schedule holds the standard fees your credit union charges on each loan type: processing, legal, valuation, stamp duty, and the rest.

These are the starting figures. A product can set its own fees, a Credit Officer can change a fee on one loan while it is a draft, and a Disbursement officer can still adjust or waive a fee at funding. The schedule is where the standard numbers live.

**Required permission:** **Manage fees**, held by the Credit Manager and the Administrator.

Open **Administration → Fee Schedule**.

## What you can change

Each fee is one row. Edit it in place and save that row.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | The name staff see, for example "Processing fee". |
| **Rate** | The percentage for a percentage fee, for example `2` for 2%. |
| **Amount** | The fixed amount for a flat fee, for example a registration fee. |
| **Charge tax on this fee** | The checkbox names your sales tax and rate, for example "Charge 15% GCT on this fee". Tick it and the tax is added to the fee. Leave it off for a tax-exempt fee such as an insurance premium. |
| **Treatment** | How the fee affects what the member receives: **Deducted from proceeds**, **Paid separately**, or **Added to loan**. |
| **Active** | Untick to stop using a fee without deleting it. An inactive fee no longer appears on the disbursement screen. |

Each row also shows the fee's code and the loan types it applies to, so you can tell the unsecured processing fee from the mortgage one.

<Note>
  A fee worked out by a formula, such as stamp duty, has no rate to edit. Its row says how it is computed instead. You can still change its label, tax checkbox, treatment, and active state.
</Note>

## Changing the processing fee

The processing fee is the one you are most likely to change. Find the processing row for the loan type, type the new percentage into **Rate**, and click **Save**.

The new figure applies to the next loan funded. Loans already disbursed keep the fee they were charged.

## What happens at funding

The disbursement screen is where one loan's fees are settled. Each fee starts from the amount agreed at capture, or else the product's own fee, or else this schedule. The Disbursement officer can still adjust or waive it for that loan. See [Fees, deductions, and tax](/omnia/los/disbursement/fees-and-gct) for how fees take the loan amount down to what the member receives.

Every save is recorded in the audit trail with the old and new values.

<Warning>
  A change here applies to every future loan of that type that has no product or loan-level figure of its own. Check the figure before you save.
</Warning>
