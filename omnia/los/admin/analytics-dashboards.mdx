The analytics page gives managers a live view of loan production, cycle time, how long loans sit with each officer, credit quality, disbursement health, and members.

Every panel reads one **date range** at the top of the page, 12 months by default. The numbers are current as of the moment the page loads. A badge names the currency every amount is shown in.

**Required permission:** **View reports**, held by the Credit Manager, Branch Manager, and Administrator. Without it you are sent back to your dashboard.

Open **Dashboards → Analytics**. The page is titled **Loan production**.

<Note>
  Analytics show your workspace as it is now. For a fixed record of what happened, for an auditor or your regulator, use the [audit trail](/omnia/los/compliance/audit-trail) and [compliance export](/omnia/los/compliance/compliance-export).
</Note>

## Branch scoping

- **Credit Managers** and **Administrators** see every branch.
- **Branch Managers** see only their own branch, in every chart, total, drill-down, and export.

You set no filter for this. A Branch Manager cannot see another branch's figures with any date range.

## The six panels

The page stacks six panels: Production, Cycle time, Stage timing by officer, Credit quality & pricing, Operations & compliance, and Members. Each has its own drill-downs and its own **Export CSV**.

### Production

Five tiles:

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Applications** | Applications started in the range, with their total requested value |
| **Disbursed** | Total value disbursed in the range, with the loan count |
| **Approval rate** | Approved share of decided applications |
| **Pull-through** | Share of applications started in the range that were disbursed |
| **Avg loan size** | Average requested amount |

Below the tiles:

- **Production volume** over the range, switchable between **Applications** and **Disbursed**, grouped by day, week, or month to suit the range.
- An **application funnel**: Created, Submitted, Approved, Disbursed, Filed. Click a stage to list the loans in it.
- A **loan mix** bar by loan type. Click a segment to list those loans.

### Cycle time

Shows where applications slow down.

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Time to decision** (average and 95th percentile) | Days from submission to an approve or decline decision |
| **Time to cash** (average and 95th percentile) | Days from submission to disbursement |
| **End to end** (average) | Days from starting the application to filing |
| **Re-work rate** | Share of applications sent back at least once, for more information or for a securities issue |

A bar chart shows the average days spent in each stage. Only stages a loan has already left are counted.

### Stage timing by officer

Cycle time says how long a stage takes. This panel says who held the loan, and for how long: two days with the Credit Officer, then three hours with the Adjudicator, and who they were.

A loan counts against a stage from the moment it arrives until someone moves it on, and the person who moved it is the one who held it. The stage a loan is in now counts against whoever it is assigned to. The loans included are those started in the date range.

**Time in each stage, by officer** lists each person once, with the stages they worked underneath: loans handled, average time, and 95th percentile. Search by name, filter by stage or role, and sort by most total time, most loans, or slowest stage. Click a stage row to see the loans. Rows with fewer than three loans are dimmed so one slow loan does not look like a pattern.

**Per-loan timelines** draw each loan as one bar, split into colored segments by stage, with the officer and the time on each. Search by loan ID or member, filter by stage, and sort by slowest, fastest, or newest. Click a segment to see that loan's full breakdown: every stage, who held it, when it arrived and left, and how long it took.

Times are rounded to what a manager reads, such as **13d** or **3h**, with quick steps in minutes and seconds. The export writes one row per stage per loan.

### Credit quality & pricing

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Weighted-avg rate** | Average interest rate weighted by loan amount, using the overridden rate where one was set |
| **Override rate** | Share of loans with a manual rate override |
| **Approval rate** | Approved share of decided applications |
| **Decisions** | Decisions recorded in the range |

Below them, a **risk-band distribution** from R1 (lowest risk) to R5 (highest) and a **decision outcomes** breakdown: Approved, Declined, Info requested.

<Tip>
  Read the override rate next to the risk bands. Lots of R4 and R5 loans plus a high override rate shows where pricing is drifting from your rate table. Drill in to see the individual decisions.
</Tip>

### Operations & compliance

Covers disbursements made in the range.

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Cleared rate** | Share of disbursements whose payment cleared |
| **Dual-control** | Share of second-approver requests that were approved |
| **AML flagged** | Share of payee screenings that came back Review or Blocked |
| **Fee revenue** | Fees charged on disbursements in the range |
| **GCT collected** | Sales tax collected on taxable fees in the range |

A **settlement status** bar splits every disbursement into Scheduled, Sent, Cleared, Returned, Failed, and Reversed. Click a slice to see the loans.

Loans by stage are on the Credit Manager dashboard, not here.

### Members

| Tile | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **New members** | Members added in the range |
| **Total members** | All member records that are not deleted |
| **KYC verified** | Share of members whose identity check is Verified |
| **Pending KYC** | Members whose check is In progress or in Manual review |

Below them, a **KYC funnel** (Unverified, In progress, Manual review, Verified, Failed) and a member growth chart.

Only **New members** and the growth chart follow the date range. The other figures count every current member. Drill-downs here open the member, not a loan.

## The Dashboards hub

**Dashboards** lists every dashboard and marks each one open or locked for your role, with a hint on which role unlocks it.

| Dashboard | What it shows | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **My Work** (in the sidebar) | Your own work in hand, completions, recent activity, and cycle time | Everyone |
| **Overview** | Action queues, recent activity, and trends, scoped to the branch for Branch Managers | Everyone |
| **Loan production** | The six analytics panels | **View reports** |
| **Credit Manager** | The whole pipeline by stage, throughput, and loans with no owner | **View reports** |
| **Adjudication** (the Credit Approvals dashboard) | Pending decisions, weekly approvals and declines, time to decision | **Approve loans** or **View reports** |
| **Securities** | Loans awaiting collateral checks, open requests, how fast requests are resolved | **Review securities** or **View reports** |
| **Disbursement** | Approved loans ready to fund, weekly totals, approval-to-funding time | **Disburse loans** or **View reports** |
| **Reports** | Your credit union's periodic credit reports | **View reports** |
| **Audit log** | The searchable audit trail | **View reports** |

### My Work

**My Work** is each person's home page: what is mine now, and what have I done lately. Every figure is your own; nobody sees anyone else's.

Pick a week, month, or quarter (week by default). Four tiles sit at the top: loans waiting on you and how long the oldest has waited (**Needs your action** for a Credit Officer, **On your plate** for the queue roles), the **pipeline value** of what you hold, your **average cycle time**, and how many loans you **touched**. A **Completed** block follows your role: submissions for a Credit Officer, approvals and declines for an Adjudicator, resolutions for Securities, disbursements for Disbursement, plus a **Returned to you** count. Tiles link to the loans behind them, and **Export CSV** downloads your work in hand and completions.

### Credit Manager dashboard

A pipeline view for the whole workspace. Choose **week**, **month**, or **quarter** for three tiles: Approved, Declined, and Disbursed in the period. **Active loans** counts everything not yet finished. **Pipeline by state** lists each active stage with a count and bar; click a row to see those loans. When active loans have no owner, an **unassigned backlog** callout appears so you can route them. The page has its own CSV export.

### Team dashboards

**Credit Approvals**, **Securities**, and **Disbursement** are each the home page of the team that works that queue. Each has tiles, a daily activity sparkline, and a team CSV export.

- **Credit Approvals**: Pending decision, Approved this week, Declined this week, Avg time to decision.
- **Securities**: Pending reviews, Outstanding requests, Resolved this week, Reviews completed, Auto-skipped (unsecured loans that pass straight through securities).
- **Disbursement**: Awaiting disbursement, Disbursed this week, Total disbursed, Avg approval to fund. **Awaiting disbursement** matches the Disbursement queue's **Pending** tab, so a loan still in Securities is not counted.

Each has an **Audit trail** button that opens the audit trail filtered to that team's main action: decisions, resolved security requests, or disbursements. The Credit Manager dashboard's button opens it unfiltered.

## Drilling into the numbers

Click a funnel stage, mix segment, chart bar, or settlement slice to open a list of the loans or members behind it. The list is searchable and shows up to 50 rows. For everything, use the export.

## Exporting

Each panel's **Export CSV** downloads the records behind it, with columns to suit: Production gives loan ID, status, type, product, branch, member number and name, requested amount, term, and the started and submitted times; Operations gives settlement, approval, and screening status with gross, fees, tax, and net to the member; Members gives member number, name, status, identity check status, and date added.

Team exports share one column set: Loan ID, Status, Loan type, Product code, Branch code, Member CIF, Member name, Requested amount, Term (months), Approval level, Submitted at, and Updated at. Each covers only the stages that team works:

- **Adjudication**: Credit approval, Info requested, Approved, Declined.
- **Securities**: Securities, Securities issue.
- **Disbursement**: Disbursement, Disbursed.

Exports respect branch scope, and every export is recorded in the audit trail. Each download holds up to 5,000 rows. If you need more, narrow the date range and export in parts.

<Accordion title="How often does analytics data refresh?">
  Every time you open the page or change the date range. There is no schedule; the figures are current as of the moment the page loaded.
</Accordion>
