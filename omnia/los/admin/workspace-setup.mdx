Workspace setup is where an Administrator sets your credit union's branding and checks the things fixed when the workspace was created: its web address and its branches.

Each credit union has its own private workspace. Nobody outside your credit union can see into it.

**Required permission:** **Manage workspace**, held by the Administrator.

## Your workspace address

Your workspace has a short name that appears in every web address your team uses, for example `/my-credit-union/loans`. It is set when your workspace is created and **cannot be changed afterwards**, because every bookmark, email link, and document link points at it. If it needs to change, raise it with your LOS support team before you go live.

## Updating branding

Open **Administration → Settings → Branding** to set your credit union's look and the names on emails and documents. Changes apply at once to new emails, new loan agreements, and newly prepared documents.

<Note>
  Branding is not retroactive. Documents already generated keep the branding they were made with. Re-prepare an unsigned document to pick up the change.
</Note>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Primary color" icon="palette">
    A hex color such as `#003366`, used across the workspace, on emails, on loan agreements, and on the sign-in pages. Starts as `#6366F1`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product name" icon="type">
    The name shown after the platform name in the app header and browser tabs. Up to 40 characters. Leave it blank to show **LoS**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Short name" icon="text-cursor-input">
    The name printed in the footer of generated documents such as the Loan Disbursement Sheet. Up to 20 characters: letters, digits, spaces, and basic punctuation. Leave it blank to use your workspace name.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email from-name" icon="mail">
    The name members and staff see in the **From** field of emails, for example `My Credit Union`. 1 to 80 characters. Until you set it, emails come from "Loans Team".
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agreement letterhead text" icon="file-text">
    A short text header printed at the top of every loan agreement. Up to 2,000 characters.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every branding change is recorded in the audit trail with the old and new values.

Two names are not on this page. Your **workspace name**, shown in the app and at the start of email subject lines, is set when the workspace is created. Your credit union's **legal name**, which names the credit union as the lender in the loan agreement and heads the Loan Disbursement Sheet, is set by your LOS support team; until it is set, the workspace name is used.

### Logo

Upload your logo as PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 2 MB. The crop keeps your image's shape, so a square mark or a wide banner both work. The logo appears in the workspace header, on sign-in pages, on loan agreements, at the top of the Loan Disbursement Sheet, and in emails. The disbursement sheet prints PNG and JPEG logos only; with a WebP logo it prints a text heading instead. Uploading a new logo replaces the old one.

### Letterhead image

You can also upload a letterhead **image**, PNG, JPEG, or WebP up to 2 MB, printed as a banner across the top of loan agreements. Without one, agreements use the letterhead text. Click **Remove** to clear it.

### Browser tab icon

Upload the small icon shown in browser tabs and bookmarks: PNG, JPEG, WebP, or SVG, up to 512 KB. Use a square image with a transparent background. SVG files skip cropping. Without one, the platform's default icon is used.

<Note>
  Every upload is checked before it is stored: the file must really be the type it claims to be, and it is scanned for viruses. A file that fails either check is rejected.
</Note>

## Branches

Each branch has a short **branch code**, set when the branch is added, for example `EST` for an Eastview branch. The code starts every loan ID created at that branch:

```
EST-AUTO-00001    (motor vehicle loan, Eastview branch, first loan)
MAIN-UNS-00047    (unsecured loan, Main branch)
```

<Warning>
  Branch codes are permanent. They are part of every loan ID created at the branch and cannot be changed later.
</Warning>

You cannot add a branch in the app. When your credit union opens a new branch, ask your LOS support team to add it.

## Your first administrator

Your first Administrator account is created with your workspace, using the email address your credit union supplied. Sign in with it, then invite your other administrators and staff from **Administration → Add New Users**.

<Accordion title="Which roles can I invite?">
  Every workspace has seven built-in roles:

  | Role | What they do |
  |---|---|
  | **Administrator** | Branding, settings, products, rates, fees, and invitations. Shown on screen as **Application Administrator** |
  | **Credit Manager** | Products, rates, and fees; all-branch analytics; oversight of the whole pipeline |
  | **Branch Manager** | Branch analytics; assigns and oversees loans in their branch |
  | **Credit Officer** | Captures and manages loan applications |
  | **Adjudicator** | Approves, declines, or asks for more information |
  | **Securities** | Verifies collateral and resolves security requests |
  | **Disbursement** | Releases funds on approved loans |

  Your credit union may also have roles of its own. See [Roles and permissions](/omnia/los/access/roles-and-permissions).
</Accordion>

## Other workspace settings

Branding and branches are the visible setup. The settings that govern lending, such as the tax rate on fees, disbursement controls, approval rules, and required fields, are on [Workspace configuration](/omnia/los/admin/workspace-configuration).
