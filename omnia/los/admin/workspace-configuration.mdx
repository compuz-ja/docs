Workspace configuration is the set of credit-union-wide settings that govern tax on fees, how long audit records are kept, disbursement controls, who may approve what, which fields are mandatory, and how email is sent.

Most of these come from your credit union's written policies, change rarely, and carry regulatory weight, so your LOS support team sets them for you. Send the policy, and they load it.

| Setting | Who changes it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Branding: logo, color, product name, short name, email from-name, letterhead, tab icon | Administrator | **Administration → Settings → Branding**. See [Workspace setup](/omnia/los/admin/workspace-setup#updating-branding). |
| Team channel hand-off posts | Administrator | **Administration → Settings → Teams Posts** |
| Legal name | LOS support team | From your credit union's registration |
| Tax on fees, audit retention, disbursement controls | LOS support team | From your policy |
| Approval rules, required fields, and other lending policies | LOS support team | From your policy |
| Email delivery | LOS support team | From your choice of sender |

Changing branding and team posts needs **Manage workspace**, held by the Administrator.

## Tax, retention, and disbursement controls

These apply to every loan and every branch.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tax rate on fees** | The sales tax added to fees marked taxable (for example GCT at 15%). Fees not marked taxable carry none. A change applies to fees calculated afterwards, never to disbursements already made. |
| **Audit retention** | How many years the tamper-evidence records for your audit trail are kept, at least the minimum your regulator sets. It can be lengthened but never shortened. |
| **Dual-control threshold** | Disbursements at or above this net amount need a second approver before release. Off when not set. |
| **Payee screening required** | When on, every payee must screen clear before a disbursement can be released. |
| **Automatic release** | When on, a small scheduled disbursement that clears every check releases without a manual click. |
| **Automatic release ceiling** | The largest net amount that can release automatically. With no ceiling set, nothing releases automatically, even when automatic release is on. |

A scheduled disbursement releases automatically only when all of these hold: automatic release is on, the amount is at or under the ceiling, no second approval is pending, payee screening has passed where required, and the officer is within their own limit. Anything else waits for a person.

Each disbursement officer's own limit is set per person. How the threshold, screening, limits, and automatic release work together at funding is on [Disbursement controls](/omnia/los/disbursement/controls).

## Lending policies

Each policy below is off until your LOS support team loads it from your written policy. Until then, the simpler default applies.

| Policy | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Approval authority** | How much each approving position may approve, by type of security. An approver over their limit cannot claim or decide the loan. |
| **Approval routing** | Whether a loan is decided by one approver within their limit, by your credit union's committee with a quorum, or recorded as a committee decision with a meeting date and proof. Also which borrowers need an approved waiver first. |
| **Approver is not the capturer** | The person who captured a loan cannot approve it. |
| **Approver is not the rate overrider** | The person who changed a loan's rate cannot approve it. |
| **Product-limit exceptions** | How far over a product's maximum a loan may go with a reason, and who must approve it. Beyond that margin the amount is refused. |
| **Staff borrowing limits** | A cap on staff loans of a given kind. Above it, an approved waiver is needed first. |
| **Required fields** | Which member, loan, guarantor, and security fields must be filled before a member is saved or a loan is submitted. |
| **Member classifications** | The staff and board categories that make a loan confidential and decide its approval route. |
| **Securities check after disbursement** | Sends a disbursed loan back to Securities for a final check before it is filed. |

How each policy shows up on a loan is covered with the step it governs: [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes), [Creating members](/omnia/los/members/creating-members), [Creating an application](/omnia/los/loans/creating-an-application), and [Confidential and staff loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).

### The securities check after disbursement

Some credit unions confirm collateral once more after funds go out, for example that vehicle insurance is active or a lien is registered. Most do not. When the check is off, a disbursed loan moves straight on to filing. When it is on, the loan returns to the Securities team first.

<Note>
  Leave the post-disbursement check off unless your credit union has a written policy that requires it.
</Note>

## Email delivery

Notification and signing emails go out through one sender, chosen with your LOS support team:

- **The platform's mail service**, the default.
- **Your own mail server.**
- **Your credit union's Microsoft 365 mailbox.** Mail goes out as that mailbox, with your **Email from-name** as the display name, and no mailbox password is stored.

If one address bounces, only that email fails. Other emails still go out.
