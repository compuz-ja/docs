While an application is a **Draft**, the Credit Officer sets the fees that will apply to it and the creditor life cover it carries. The **Fees & charges** card and the **Creditor-life insurance** section both feed the disbursement screen, so the Disbursement officer starts from the officer's choices instead of re-keying them.

Both are editable only while the loan is a draft, and need the **Edit loans** permission, which the Credit Officer and the Administrator hold. After submission they are read-only.

## The Fees & charges card

The card lists every fee on your credit union's [fee schedule](/omnia/los/admin/fee-schedule) that applies to this loan type, estimated on the requested amount. Each row shows the fee, how it is worked out (a percentage of the loan, a flat amount, an amount per thousand, or a stamp duty formula), its **Amount**, its tax, and its **Treatment**. Tax (sales tax on fees, for example GCT) is charged at your credit union's rate on taxable fees. The footer shows the total fees plus tax and **Net to member (after deductions)**.

### Edit a fee's amount for this loan

The schedule is the default. When the fee agreed with the member is different, change it on this loan only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit amount on the fee">
    An editor opens under the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a percentage or a fixed amount">
    Enter a percentage of the loan (0% to 100%, two decimal places) or a fixed amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason (optional)">
    Up to 200 characters, for example "rate agreed with the member".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save">
    The row is marked **Edited** and shows the schedule default beside the new value. Tax, totals and net to member recalculate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Change amount** edits it again. **Reset to schedule** removes the edit. The schedule itself is untouched.

Any fee on the schedule can be edited, including a per-thousand or stamp duty fee; the edit replaces it with a percentage or a fixed amount. A fee that is not on the schedule for this loan type cannot be edited. Add it as a custom line instead. Every edit is recorded in the audit trail with the schedule value, the old value and the new one.

### Choose how each fee is collected

| Treatment | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Deducted from the loan** | Taken from the loan proceeds, so the member receives less cash. |
| **Charged to the member's account** | Collected from the member's account. The proceeds are unchanged. |
| **Added to the loan** | Added to the principal, so the member repays it over the term. |

Only **Deducted from the loan** reduces the net to member.

### Add fees that are not on the schedule

Under **Fees & charges you're adding**, add any other charge, like a share deposit, a standing order or an insurance premium. Each line takes a name, an amount, how it is collected, where it goes (**CU income**, **Insurer**, **Attorney**, **Government**, **Tax authority**, **Other lender** or **Other**) and whether tax applies. Lines save automatically.

### Top-ups and consolidations

At the top of the card, **Transaction type** is **New loan**, **Top-up of an existing loan** or **Consolidation of existing debt**. For a top-up or consolidation, enter the **Existing balance being paid off**. The processing fee is then charged on the new money only: the requested amount minus that balance.

### What the Disbursement officer sees

The disbursement screen starts from your edited amounts, treatments and custom lines. The Disbursement officer finalises fees at funding. See [Fees, deductions, and tax](/omnia/los/disbursement/fees-and-gct).

## Creditor life insurance

Creditor life cover settles the outstanding balance if the borrower dies. It is on by default for Unsecured, Motor vehicle and Real estate loans. It never applies to a cash-secured loan, because the pledged savings already settle the balance: the section reads **Not applicable to cash-secured loans**.

### The settings

| Setting | Options | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **This loan carries creditor-life cover** | On or off | On by default. Untick for no cover. |
| **Lives covered** | Single, Double or Triple | Required when cover is on. Picks the rate from the rate card. |
| **Premium payment** | **Annually (annual premium)** or **In full (single premium)** | Required before submission when cover is on. |
| **Sum insured** | An amount | Defaults to the requested amount. |
| **Manual annual premium** or **Manual single premium** | An amount, optional | Replaces the calculated premium. Blank uses the rate card. |

Until you choose **Premium payment**, an amber line says it is required, and submission is refused with "Choose how the creditor life premium will be paid (annually or in full) in the Creditor-life insurance section before submitting."

### How the premium is priced

Your credit union's creditor life rate card sets a monthly rate per 1,000 of sum insured for single, double and triple lives, plus a flat policy fee. The section prices both payment options from it and shows them side by side.

```latex
\begin{aligned}
\text{monthly basic} &= \frac{\text{sum insured}}{1000} \times \text{rate for the lives covered} \\
\text{annual premium} &= \text{monthly basic} \times 12 + \text{policy fee} \\
\text{single premium} &= \text{monthly basic} \times \text{term in months} + \text{policy fee}
\end{aligned}
```

Each premium is rounded once to the cent, so it matches a hand calculation from the same card.

| Payment | Collected | In the monthly payment | In the TDSR |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annually** | The first year's annual premium, once, at disbursement | No | Annual premium ÷ 12 |
| **In full** | The single premium, once, at disbursement | No | No |

The note under the premium box says this in words.

The one-off charge appears on the Fees & charges card as **Creditor life insurance (annual premium, first year)** or **Creditor life insurance (single premium, paid in full)**. It defaults to **Deducted from the loan**, paid to the insurer, with no tax. You can change its treatment but not its amount there: change the amount with the manual premium in the Creditor-life insurance section.

<Note>
  Illustrative figures only. With a rate of 0.50 per 1,000 per month and a 1,000 policy fee, a sum insured of 1,500,000 over 60 months gives a monthly basic of 750, an annual premium of 10,000 and a single premium of 46,000. Paid annually, 833.33 a month counts in the TDSR.
</Note>

### Without a rate card

If your credit union has no rate card loaded, the section says **Estimate only** and prices from a placeholder table by the applicant's age, with a **Premium basis** select (**Level (full sum, whole term)** or **Declining balance (reduces as repaid)**). Ask your LOS support team to load your rate card before you go live.

### Older loans with no payment choice

A loan saved before **Premium payment** existed spreads the whole-term premium across the monthly payment. The note says **Not chosen yet**. Choosing a payment option switches it to the current behaviour.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the processing fee on one loan without changing the schedule?">
    Yes. Click **Edit amount**, enter the percentage or fixed amount, and save. The schedule stays the default for every other loan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not edit the creditor life fee line?">
    Its amount is the premium. Set a different premium with the manual premium field in the Creditor-life insurance section, and the fee line follows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is creditor life charged every month?">
    No. Paid annually, the first year is collected at disbursement. Paid in full, the whole premium is collected at disbursement. Neither is added to the monthly instalment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no creditor life on a cash-secured loan?">
    The pledged savings settle the balance on death, so cover would be redundant. It is never applied or charged on a cash-secured loan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change fees after the loan is submitted?">
    Not at capture. Fees become read-only on submission. The Disbursement officer finalises them at funding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my fee edits if I change the loan type?">
    A change to a different kind of loan creates a new draft and does not carry fee treatments or custom fee lines. See [Change the loan type or product](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
