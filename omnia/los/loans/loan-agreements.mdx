The loan agreement is the PDF contract between your credit union and the borrower. It is generated automatically once a loan is approved, built from the exact approved figures, and must be on file before any funds can be released.

## The four agreement types

The agreement matches the loan type, with the clauses and security section that kind of loan needs.

| Loan type | What the agreement covers |
|---|---|
| **Cash-secured** | A loan secured against the member's savings, including the pledge of the secured deposit. |
| **Unsecured** | A personal loan with no security section. |
| **Auto** | The charge over the vehicle, the registered lien, and the insurance requirement. |
| **Mortgage** | The mortgage over the property, custody of the title, and the property insurance requirement. |

Each agreement prints the approved principal, term, rate, and approval date, the member's name and address, and the full monthly payment. The **Loan agreement** card shows the template and version it was built from, when it was generated, and its size.

## What the agreement contains

**The full monthly payment, itemised.** The agreement splits the monthly payment into principal and interest, the compulsory savings paid alongside the loan, and the creditor-life premium where it is part of the instalment, then shows the total. The total matches the affordability figure the Credit Officer saw at capture.

- **Compulsory savings** uses the product's own minimum when the product sets one, the same figure used in the affordability check. See [Loan products](/omnia/los/admin/loan-products).
- **Creditor-life** is priced from your credit union's creditor-life rate card for the number of lives covered, when your rates include one, and from the standard age table otherwise. It is never charged on a cash-secured loan.
- **Premium paid separately.** When the member pays the creditor-life premium **annually** or **in full**, it is not in the monthly instalment. The agreement prints it as its own line: **Creditor Life Insurance (annual premium, payable yearly)** or **Creditor Life Insurance (single premium, paid in full)**, with the amount.

**The security, stated plainly.** On a secured loan, the agreement names the type of security (Cash Secured, Motor Vehicle, or Real Property) and what is pledged before the legal clause that grants it.

**When the first payment is due.** A cash-secured agreement carries an Effective Date, and the first payment falls one month after it. The other types are not disbursed the day the agreement is drawn up, so they say the first payment is due thirty days after disbursement.

**Standard clauses.** A consent to report to and check with credit bureaus, and a change-in-circumstances clause asking the member to tell the credit union promptly of any material change during the loan.

**The signature page.** The member, any guarantors, the Credit Officer (the line is printed as "Loan Officer"), and the Branch Manager sign, each with a date. The Adjudicator does not sign. Each page carries the member's initials, and the page closes with the member's and each guarantor's acknowledgement that they have read and understood the agreement.

<Note>
  The name the agreement uses for your credit union as the legal party is set to your registered name. It can differ from the display name on the letterhead.
</Note>

## When the agreement is generated

As soon as a loan is approved, the agreement is generated in the background and attached to the loan. It is ready by the time the loan reaches the Disbursement team.

<Warning>
  A loan cannot be disbursed without an agreement. Scheduling and release are refused with "The signed loan agreement has not been generated yet." The agreement is one of three items on the **Pre-execution checklist**, with a **Verified** identity check and no open security requests. The check runs when you click, so a stale page cannot get around it. Releasing funds also needs the signed [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet).
</Warning>

## Regenerating an agreement

Regenerate an agreement to reissue it, for example after a correction. The new agreement replaces the old one, which stays on file and in the audit trail. Downloads always serve the newest version.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the loan">
    Open the loan from the Adjudication or Disbursement queue. The **Loan agreement** card shows whether an agreement exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regenerate">
    Click **Regenerate**, or **Generate agreement** if none exists yet. The agreement is rebuilt from the loan's latest approval decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    When it finishes, the page refreshes and the card shows the new agreement. If generation fails, the page shows the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two failures come up most:

- **The loan is not approved.** An agreement is only built against an approval decision.
- **No collateral record.** An auto or mortgage agreement needs the collateral captured. The message reads "The agreement could not be generated because the loan has no collateral record on file. Open the loan and capture the collateral details, then retry."

Staff with the **View reports** permission (Administrator, Credit Manager, and Branch Manager) can regenerate. So can the Disbursement team, with **Disburse loans**, when a missing agreement is blocking their checklist.

## Downloading the agreement

Everyone working the loan can read its agreement:

| Who | Through |
|---|---|
| Administrator, Credit Manager, Branch Manager | **View reports** |
| Credit Officer | Their access to the loans they capture |
| Adjudicator | **Approve loans** |
| Securities team | **Review securities** |
| Disbursement team | **Disburse loans** |

On the **Loan agreement** card, click **View** to open it in a new tab, or the download button to save it. The file name includes the agreement's version. If no agreement exists yet, you are told it has not been generated rather than handed an empty file.

Agreements are kept for seven years in storage that cannot be altered or deleted. Every generation, failure, and download is recorded.
