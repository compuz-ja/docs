A loan application is captured on one page, section by section, and saved as you go. You start it from **New Application**, fill each section, and click **Submit application** when the page says **Ready to submit**. Submission sends it to the **Credit Approvals** queue.

## Before you start

- **The member exists and is Active.** You cannot draft a loan for a suspended or deceased member. Search before creating a new member; see [Creating members](/omnia/los/members/creating-members).
- **Identity status allows drafting.** You can draft for any member except one who is **Unverified** or **Failed**. To submit, the member must be **Verified**.
- **You have the permissions.** **Create loans**, **Edit loans** and **Submit loans**, which the Credit Officer and the Administrator hold.

## Start the draft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New Application">
    In the sidebar, open **Loans**, then **New Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Search by name or CIF. The member's branch and identity status carry through to the application. A **Member context** panel shows the CIF, date of birth, branch, contact details and their last loan.

    If the member is not verified and you have **Submit identity checks**, a **KYC verification** card appears here so you can run the check without leaving the page. If the member cannot be drafted for, an **Application blocked** panel says why.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the loan type and product">
    **Loan type** is your credit union's own list, and the product list filters to match. Each loan type maps to one of four kinds: Unsecured, Cash secured, Motor vehicle or Real estate. See [Loan types](/omnia/los/loans/loan-types).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a quick estimate (optional)">
    Enter an amount and term (and a vehicle year for a motor vehicle loan) for an indicative payment at the current rates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create draft application">
    The draft is created with its Loan ID, for example `MAIN-UNS-00001`, and opens on the application page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capture the sections

Most sections save automatically a moment after you stop typing. The footer shows **Saving…**, **Saved** or **Not saved yet**. Collateral sections (vehicle, property, each cash account) have their own save button. Sections are editable only while the application is a **Draft**.

The step bar at the top tracks progress, and each section folds to a one-line summary when you are not working in it.

### Loan parameters

**Purpose**, **Requested amount** and **Term (months)**, all required. Amount and term must sit inside the product's limits. A value outside them is refused with the limit, for example "Above the maximum for this product. The maximum is 5,000,000."

If your credit union allows [product-limit exceptions](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes#product-limit-exceptions), an amount a little above the maximum opens an **Exception reason** field instead of a refusal.

The section also shows:

- **Estimated monthly**, **Effective rate** (with the risk band it assumes), **Rate source**, and a plain sentence explaining the rate. The figures are indicative, calculated the same way the approver's will be.
- **Share requirements**: **Mandatory shares**, the membership requirement every member holds, and **Minimum shares**, the loan-linked shares your credit union sets per loan type, or **Not required**.
- **Rate override**, for staff with **Override rates** (Credit Manager and Administrator). Set a rate and a reason of 3 to 280 characters, and the section reads **Rate override active** with who set it. Officers without the permission see a note to ask their Credit Manager.

### Income & expenses

Income lines with their counted share, monthly expenses with an after-loan amount for any debt this loan pays off, this loan's monthly payment, and the totals: **Total income**, **Total expenses**, **Money left over** and **TDSR**. At least one income line is required. See [Affordability and TDSR](/omnia/los/loans/affordability-and-tdsr).

### Net worth

Assets (real estate, motor vehicle, furniture and household, savings and investments, life insurance cash value, other) and liabilities (other loans, credit cards, lines of credit, contingent and other). The approver reads it alongside the TDSR.

### Guarantors

Optional for every loan type. Click **Add Guarantor** and capture their personal, contact, employment and income details and their relationship to the applicant. Each guarantor has a **Documents** block with **ID**, **Income** and **Employment** uploads (10 MB per file, virus-scanned). The documents follow the loan through every later stage.

### Collateral

Shown for secured loans only.

- **Cash secured**: pledge one to five accounts. Each is **Cash / Shares**, **Fixed / Time Deposit**, **Savings Hypothecation** or **Funds held at another institution**, with its account number and amount. The card totals them and shows the most you can borrow: the share of the pledged cash your credit union allows, which is lower when any of it is held at another institution.
- **Motor vehicle**: make, model, year, VIN or chassis number, engine number, colour, registration, insurer and policy number, valuation, forced sale value, valuator, valuation date and source. Click **Save vehicle details**.
- **Real estate**: property type, address, parish or region, title reference (volume and folio or deed number), registered mortgagees, insurer and peril insurance, valuation, forced sale value, valuator and valuation date. Click **Save property details**.

### Creditor-life insurance and fees

Creditor life is on by default except on cash-secured loans. Choose **Lives covered** and **Premium payment** (**Annually** or **In full**). The payment choice is required before submission. The **Fees & charges** card lists your credit union's fees for this loan. You can edit an amount, choose how each fee is collected, and add others. See [Fees and creditor life at capture](/omnia/los/loans/fees-and-insurance-at-capture).

### Escrow shares

Shown only when the product requires escrow shares: months of this loan's payment the member holds in shares as security. You can override the months (0 to 6) for this loan, or enter the member's savings percentage where the product varies the months by savings level.

### Credit rating & risk registry

The external credit rating from a credit report (**Very low risk** to **Very high risk**), an optional credit score and report source, and the **Risk Registry check** (**Clear**, **Adverse listing found** or **Not checked**). The rating feeds the risk band at submission. Not captured counts as average risk.

### Officer recommendation

Your note to the approver and the approval level your branch policy recommends, from the levels your credit union sets. Approvers see both. It is advice: the approval route decides who approves.

## Submit

When nothing is outstanding, the footer reads **Ready to submit**. Otherwise it reads **Almost there** and lists every item, for example "2 items outstanding before this can be submitted: at least one income line, identity verification for Jane Demo (KYC in progress)." Identity verification is listed last because you complete it on the member's record.

Click **Submit application**. A message confirms the loan is in the approval queue and names who was notified.

### Why a submission is refused

Everything is checked again when you submit, so a stale page cannot slip past. Each refusal names its cause.

| Cause | Message |
|---|---|
| Identity not verified | "Identity verification for FIRST LAST is waiting on manual review. It must be completed on the member's record before this application can be submitted." (or *not started*, *in progress*, *failed*) |
| Member not active | "This member is not active. Reactivate the member before submitting." |
| Creditor life payment not chosen | "Choose how the creditor life premium will be paid (annually or in full) in the Creditor-life insurance section before submitting." |
| No income line | "Statement of affairs is empty. Capture at least one income line before submitting." |
| Required fields missing | "Complete these fields before submitting." followed by the fields, grouped by the loan, each guarantor and each security record |
| Collateral not captured | "Cash-secured loans require at least one captured cash security record." (likewise for a vehicle or property) |
| Over the cash-secured limit | The message gives the percentage that applies and the most you can borrow against the pledged cash. Lower the amount or pledge more cash. |

### The required-fields policy

Your credit union can require more than the minimum before submission. Ask your system administrator to change the list. Fields that can be required:

- **the loan**: purpose, amount, term, an income line, an expense line, credit rating, credit score, report source, Risk Registry status, the officer recommendation;
- **each guarantor**: name, relationship, date of birth, tax ID, driver's licence, phone, email, address, credit union account number, employment, industry sector, salary frequency, existing commitments, other guarantees held;
- **each vehicle**: make, model, year, VIN, engine number, colour, registration, insurer, policy number, forced sale value, valuator, valuation amount, date and source;
- **each property**: address, parish or region, title reference, registered mortgagees, insurer, policy number, peril insurance, forced sale value, valuator, valuation amount, date and source;
- **each cash security**: pledged amount, source account number, source of funds, hypothecation note.

A field can also be conditional, for example required only when another field is filled. The same rules cover [member records](/omnia/los/members/creating-members#required-fields).

## After submission

Every section locks. The risk band appears in the **Risk rating** section. If the approver requests more information, the application comes back to **Draft** with their conditions, and you edit and resubmit.

## Abandon a draft

**Abandon draft** asks "Abandon this draft?" and moves the application to **Draft abandoned**. It stays on file and cannot be revived. To change the product or kind of loan instead, use [Change loan type / product](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to click Save?">
    Not for most sections. They save automatically and show **Saved**. Vehicle, property and cash accounts have their own save buttons.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Submit greyed out?">
    Something is outstanding. The footer lists every item. If the only item is identity verification, finish KYC on the member's record and come back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I checked the member's documents. Why does it still say identity verification?">
    Checking documents is not completing verification. Record the verification on the member's KYC page so their status becomes **Verified**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I picked the wrong product. Do I have to start again?">
    No. Use **Change loan type / product** on the draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my amount refused?">
    It is outside the product's limits. Lower it, or record an exception reason if your credit union allows product-limit exceptions and the amount is within the allowed margin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the rate?">
    Only with **Override rates**, which the Credit Manager and Administrator hold. Ask them to set an override on the draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I put a bonus?">
    On a **Performance-Based Pay** income line. It counts toward affordability at the share your credit union sets.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
