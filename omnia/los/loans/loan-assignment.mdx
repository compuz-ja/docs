Every loan in an active stage can have an owner: the one person responsible for its current step. While a loan is unassigned it sits in a shared team queue and notifications go to everyone who can work that stage. Once someone owns it, notifications go to the owner alone. A loan gets an owner when a staff member claims it, or when a supervisor assigns it.

## Claim or assign

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Claim: take an unassigned loan for yourself">
    Staff who work a stage pull an unassigned loan in that stage onto themselves. You need the **Claim loans** permission and must be able to work the loan's current stage. If two people claim the same loan at the same moment, only one wins. The other is told it is already owned.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Assign: direct a loan to a specific person">
    A supervisor with **Assign loans** can assign or reassign any loan to an eligible person, replacing any existing owner. You can only pick someone who can actually work the loan's current stage. The new owner is notified by email.

    Two narrower assign rights also exist. A Credit Officer with **Submit loans** can route their own draft to a specific approver, and that owner carries into Credit Approvals. And a stage worker can hand a loan to a colleague in the same stage, for example one Adjudicator to another.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Who can do what

| Permission | Roles | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| **Claim loans** | Adjudicator, Securities, Disbursement, Administrator | Claim an unassigned loan in your stage. |
| **Assign loans** | Credit Manager, Branch Manager, Administrator | Assign or reassign any loan to an eligible person, and release a loan back to the queue. |

Stage workers claim their own work. Supervisors direct work to others.

## Which stages can have an owner

| Loan stage | Owner role |
|---|---|
| **Submitted**, **Credit approval**, **Info requested** | Adjudicator (or another approver) |
| **Securities** | Securities |
| **Disbursement** | Disbursement |

**Approved**, **Disbursed** and the final stages have no new owner. A **Draft** can carry a pre-assignment: the Credit Officer routes it to the approver who will review it once it is submitted.

## Approval authority at claim and assignment

At **Submitted** and **Credit approval**, owning a loan means approving it. When your credit union uses an [approval schedule](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes) and the loan is on the single-approver route, authority is checked before ownership changes:

- **Claim** is refused when your role cannot approve this loan on its own. The message names the loan and who can approve it, for example: "Adjudicator cannot approve Personal Loan (unsecured) loans at this amount under the approval schedule. It can be approved by: Credit Manager. Assign it to one of them, or send it back."
- The **Assign to** picker lists only people whose role can approve this loan on its own.

The check matches the approval itself, so a claim never succeeds for someone who would be refused at the last click. Loans on a committee route skip it: committee membership decides who acts. Without an approval schedule, anyone with **Approve loans** can claim.

Confidential loans add one rule: you can assign only a loan you can already see. See [Staff and confidential loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).

## The Assigned to card

The **Assigned to** card is on the loan page in the Credit Approvals, Securities and Disbursement queues, and on the Credit Officer's own loan page, where the draft pre-assignment happens.

- An unassigned loan you can work shows **Claim**.
- With **Assign loans**, you get a picker of every eligible person. In the stage queues it reads **Assign to** or **Reassign to**. On a draft it reads **Route to an approver** or **Change approver**, with the hint "Not routed yet. Pick the approver who'll review this once it's submitted, or leave it for the credit approvals team to claim." If nobody eligible exists, the card reads **No eligible staff to assign for this stage**.
- An owned loan shows **Release** to its owner and to anyone with **Assign loans**.

An owned loan shows an **Owned** badge, with **(you)** next to the name when the owner is you.

## Filter the queue by ownership

The Credit Approvals, Securities and Disbursement queues each have three tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every loan in the queue. The default. |
| **My queue** | Loans you own. |
| **Unassigned** | Loans waiting to be claimed or assigned. |

## How a loan loses its owner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Release">
    The owner, or anyone with **Assign loans**, clicks **Release**. The loan goes back to its team queue for anyone eligible to claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stage handoff">
    The owner clears automatically at two points: when the loan moves from **Approved** to **Securities**, and from **Securities** to **Disbursement**. The receiving team then claims it. The approver stays the owner from **Submitted** through **Approved**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reassignment">
    A supervisor assigns the loan to someone else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every claim, assignment and release is recorded in the audit trail with who did it and when.

<Note>
  Assignment never moves a loan through the workflow. It only changes who owns the current step.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why was my claim refused when the loan is unassigned?">
    Your role cannot approve this loan on its own under your approval schedule, for its security type and amount. The message names the roles that can. Ask a supervisor to assign it to one of them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a colleague missing from the Assign to list?">
    Their role cannot work the loan's current stage, their account is suspended or revoked, or, at an approval stage, their role cannot approve this loan on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does assigning a loan move it forward?">
    No. Assignment only changes who owns the current step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the owner when a loan is approved?">
    The approver stays the owner through **Approved**. Ownership clears when the loan moves into **Securities**, and again at **Disbursement**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I route my draft to a specific approver?">
    Yes, if you have **Submit loans**. Use **Route to an approver** on the Assigned to card of your draft.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
