A confidential loan is visible only to the people who must handle it. It is the usual treatment for loans to your own staff and officials. A colleague in the same branch, who would otherwise see every loan there, does not see it at all: no redacted row, no placeholder. The loan is simply not in their lists.

## Classifications decide it

A member can carry a **classification** from your credit union's list, for example Staff or Manager. Each classification is marked confidential or not. An unclassified member is an ordinary member, and a classification that was never marked either way is treated as confidential.

Which classifications exist and which are confidential is your credit union's policy. Ask your system administrator to change the list.

## Set a member's classification

Open the member's profile. The **Identity and access** card holds two settings:

- **Classification**: pick from the list. A confidential classification shows "confidential" beside its name. **Not classified** is an ordinary member.
- **Linked login**: the staff member's own login, when the member is also a user. Click **Link a login**, choose the login and click **Link**. **Unlink** removes it.

The card needs the **Manage member identity links** permission, separate from **Edit members**, so a clerk who may correct an address cannot decide whose loans are confidential. The Administrator holds it. A login is never matched automatically by name or email: you choose it. Every change is recorded in the audit trail.

## Confidentiality is fixed at capture

When an application is created, it records whether it is confidential and the borrower's classification at that moment. A later change to the member affects only future applications. A promotion never hides or reveals a loan already in progress.

## Who can see a confidential loan

| Who | Sees the loan |
|---|---|
| Staff with **View confidential loans** | Always. These are your designated handlers of staff loans. |
| The borrower, through their linked login | Always. |
| The person the loan is assigned to | While it is assigned to them. |
| The Credit Officer who captured it | Only while it is a **Draft** or **Info requested**. |
| Everyone else | Never. |

The capturing officer finishes what they started and fixes what comes back. After submission the loan leaves their loans list, My Work and every queue they see.

The rule applies everywhere loans are listed: the loans list, the queues, My Work, reports, analytics, the audit viewer and notifications. Branch access does not override it.

No built-in role has **View confidential loans** by default. Your credit union grants it to the roles that handle staff lending.

## How staff loans are approved

Your [approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes) can use the borrower's classification, or their own staff role, to change the route:

- send the loan to your credit union's committee instead of a single approver;
- name a substitute recorder when the borrower would normally record the committee's decision, so nobody records the decision on their own loan;
- require an **approved waiver** before the loan can be approved.

The approval route card gives the reason, for example "The borrower's staff category sends this loan to this route."

## Staff vehicle loan limits

Your credit union can cap a motor-vehicle loan by the borrower's classification. A classification with no cap is limited only by the product's maximum.

Above the cap the loan is not refused, but it cannot be approved until a waiver is approved. The approval route card shows **Above the staff motor-vehicle limit** with the limit, and approval is refused with "Approval blocked: the amount is above the CLASSIFICATION motor-vehicle limit of AMOUNT."

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I captured a staff loan and now I cannot find it. Is it lost?">
    No. Once it left **Draft**, it left your view, as designed. It is with the approvers. If it comes back for more information, you see it again while it is **Info requested** or **Draft**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a board member's loan confidential?">
    If their classification is marked confidential, yes. Your credit union decides which classifications exist.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We promoted someone. Do their existing loans move?">
    No. Each loan keeps the classification it was captured under. Their next application uses the new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a staff member approve their own loan?">
    Your approval routes are the control: route staff loans to a committee, name a substitute recorder, and require a waiver for staff classifications. If your credit union turns on the capturer rule, the officer who keyed a loan also cannot approve it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Branch Manager see a confidential loan in their branch?">
    Only with **View confidential loans**, or when the loan is assigned to them. Branch access alone is not enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can change a member's classification?">
    Staff with **Manage member identity links**. By default, the Administrator.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
