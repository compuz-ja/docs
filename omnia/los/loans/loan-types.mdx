Every loan is one of four kinds: **Unsecured**, **Cash secured**, **Motor vehicle** or **Real estate**. The kind decides which collateral section the Credit Officer completes, how the loan is priced, and whether Securities has collateral to perfect after approval.

Your credit union names its own loan types and products under **Administration > Loan types**, managed by staff with the **Manage loan types** permission. Each named type maps to one of the four kinds, which set the behaviour described here.

Every kind shares the same core sections (loan parameters, income and expenses, net worth, credit rating, recommendation; see [Creating an application](/omnia/los/loans/creating-an-application)). Guarantors are optional for every kind. The loan type is one of the factors in the risk band, alongside employment, TDSR, loan-to-value and credit history: secured loans generally score as lower risk than unsecured ones, but the band depends on all the factors together.

## Unsecured

A personal or consumer loan with no collateral. Eligibility rests on income, affordability, credit history and the strength of any guarantors.

- **Collateral section:** none.
- **After approval:** passes through Securities with no security requests, then moves to Disbursement once the legal documents your credit union requires are signed.
- **Creditor life:** on by default.
- **Typical uses:** personal expenses, home improvement, education, medical costs, debt consolidation.

## Cash secured

Backed by cash the member pledges: shares, deposits or savings at the credit union, or funds held at another institution.

- **Collateral section:** **Cash Collateral**, up to five pledged accounts. Each is **Cash / Shares**, **Fixed / Time Deposit**, **Savings Hypothecation** or **Funds held at another institution**, with its account number, amount held, and an optional source of funds and hypothecation note. The card totals every account and shows the most the member can borrow.
- **Advance limit:** the loan can be at most a share of the **combined** pledged cash that your credit union sets, lower when any account is held at another institution. The limit applies to the total, not to each account, and it is enforced: over it, the card shows a red warning and submission is refused with the maximum and the fix ("Lower the amount or pledge more cash").
- **Pricing:** your rates can differ by who holds the cash: shares, other credit union savings or deposits, and funds held at another institution. When a loan pledges a mix, the weakest security prices the whole loan, in this order: funds elsewhere, then savings or deposits, then shares. Capture order makes no difference.
- **After approval:** goes through Securities so the pledged accounts are placed on hold before disbursement. The funds stay held for the life of the loan.
- **Creditor life:** never applies. The pledged savings settle the balance on death.
- **Typical uses:** a lower rate for members with cash they are not using.

<Note>
  Illustrative example only: if shares price at 8%, credit union deposits at 9% and external funds at 10%, a loan secured by shares plus a fixed deposit prices at 9%.
</Note>

## Motor vehicle

The financed vehicle is the collateral.

- **Collateral section:** **Vehicle Details**: make, model, year, VIN or chassis number, engine number, colour, registration, insurer and policy number, valuation, forced sale value, valuator, valuation date and source.
- **Loan-to-value:** the loan amount divided by the vehicle valuation.
- **Pricing:** vehicle age (this year minus the vehicle year) can select the rate, so older vehicles may price differently. A next-model-year vehicle prices as new. A year further ahead is refused as a typing error.
- **Staff borrowers:** your credit union can cap a staff member's vehicle loan by their classification. Above the cap, approval needs a waiver. See [Staff and confidential loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).
- **After approval:** Securities requests confirmation of title and insurance. The list of documents is set per request.
- **Typical uses:** new and used vehicle purchases.

## Real estate

Secured against property.

- **Collateral section:** **Property Details**: property type, address, parish or region, title reference (volume and folio or deed number), registered mortgagees, insurer, policy number and peril insurance, valuation, forced sale value, valuator, valuation date and source.
- **Property type:** Residential House, Residential Townhouse, Residential Apartment, Commercial Building, Land or Other. **Security type:** First Mortgage, Second Mortgage, Strata Title or Other.
- **Loan-to-value:** the loan amount divided by the property valuation.
- **Term:** up to 480 months, within the product's own limits.
- **After approval:** Securities reviews valuation reports, title and mortgage registration before disbursement.
- **Typical uses:** home purchase, equity release, land purchase, construction.

<Note>
  Amount and term limits come from the product, not the kind of loan. An Administrator or Credit Manager sets each product's minimum and maximum on [Loan products](/omnia/los/admin/loan-products), and the application refuses values outside them.
</Note>

## Shares, compulsory savings and creditor life

These apply across loan types:

- **Mandatory shares** is the membership share amount every member holds, set by an Administrator. **Minimum shares** is the loan-linked share amount your credit union sets per loan type, or not required. Both show under **Share requirements** on the application.
- **Compulsory savings** is added to the monthly payment, never less than the minimum set on the product.
- **Creditor life** is priced from your rate card and paid **Annually** or **In full** at disbursement. It never applies to cash-secured loans. See [Fees and creditor life at capture](/omnia/los/loans/fees-and-insurance-at-capture).

The monthly payment used for affordability is the instalment plus compulsory savings plus the monthly creditor life amount. See [Affordability and TDSR](/omnia/los/loans/affordability-and-tdsr).

## Choosing the right kind

| If the member has... | Kind |
|---|---|
| No collateral to offer | **Unsecured** |
| Cash they can pledge for a better rate | **Cash secured** |
| A vehicle to use as security | **Motor vehicle** |
| Property to use as security | **Real estate** |

The type and product can be changed while the application is a **Draft**. See [Change the loan type or product](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type). After submission the type is locked.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much can a member borrow against their shares?">
    Up to the share of the combined pledged cash your credit union sets, which is lower when any of it is held at another institution. The Cash Collateral card shows the exact maximum.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my cash-secured loan price at the higher rate?">
    The weakest security on the loan prices all of it. Shares plus a fixed deposit price at the deposit rate. Anything held at another institution prices at that rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the loan type after creating the draft?">
    Yes, while it is a **Draft**. Use **Change loan type / product**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is there no creditor life on a cash-secured loan?">
    The pledged savings settle the balance on death, so the cover is never applied.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a next-year model be priced?">
    Yes. A next-model-year vehicle prices as new. A year two or more ahead is refused as a typing error.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
