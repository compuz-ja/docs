The Loan Origination System runs your credit union's lending process in one audited workflow. A Credit Officer captures the application, an approver decides it in **Credit Approvals**, Securities clears the collateral, and Disbursement releases the funds. Each department picks up the application exactly when it is their turn.

## How a loan moves through the system

Every application starts as **Draft** and ends in one of four final stages: **Filed** (completed), **Declined**, **Cancelled** or **Draft abandoned**.

1. A Credit Officer captures the application as a **Draft**. Sections save automatically. While it is a draft, the officer can [change the loan type or product](/omnia/los/loans/changing-loan-type).
2. The officer submits. The system checks the member's identity status, your credit union's required fields and the affordability inputs, and lists anything missing. The application becomes **Submitted** and its risk band is assessed.
3. An approver claims it into **Credit approval** and decides. Who may approve depends on your [approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes): one approver within their limit, a committee quorum, or a recorded committee decision.
4. Once **Approved**, it moves to **Securities** for collateral review. Unsecured loans have nothing to perfect and move on once the required documents are signed.
5. **Disbursement** schedules and releases the funds. The loan becomes **Disbursed** and finally **Filed**.

Some steps send an application backward: a request for more information (**Info requested**, then back to **Draft**), a return to **Draft** for a rebuild, a collateral problem (**Securities issue**), and returns from Securities and Disbursement. See [Workflow states](/omnia/los/loans/workflow-states).

No application is ever deleted. Declined, cancelled and abandoned applications stay on file.

## The four kinds of loan

Your credit union defines its own loan types and products. Each maps to one of four kinds, which decide the form sections and collateral rules.

| Kind | Collateral | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Unsecured** | None | Creditor life cover is on by default. |
| **Cash secured** | Pledged savings, deposits or funds held elsewhere | Lends up to a share of the pledged cash that your credit union sets. No creditor life. |
| **Motor vehicle** | The vehicle | Vehicle age can set the rate. |
| **Real estate** | The property | Long terms where the product allows. |

See [Loan types](/omnia/los/loans/loan-types).

## Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Credit Officer** | Creates the draft, captures every section, sets fees and creditor life, submits, and answers information requests. |
| **Adjudicator** | Claims and decides applications in Credit Approvals, within the authority your credit union gives the role. |
| **Credit Manager** | Oversees the pipeline, overrides rates, decides identity reviews and approves disbursements. Approves loans where your credit union grants the role **Approve loans**. |
| **Branch Manager** | Sees and assigns their branch's loans. |
| **Securities** | Reviews and perfects collateral for secured loans. |
| **Disbursement** | Schedules and executes the release of approved funds. |

Approval limits, committee membership and who records committee decisions are set for your credit union, so senior approvers it designates may also approve. See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes).

## Loan ID format

Every application gets a Loan ID when the draft is created: the branch code, the loan type code and a 5-digit sequence number, joined by hyphens. For example, `MAIN-AUTO-00042` is the 42nd motor vehicle loan opened at the branch coded `MAIN`.

| Segment | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Branch code | `MAIN` | The branch where the application was opened |
| Loan type code | `AUTO` | The kind of loan, for example `AUTO`, `UNS`, `CASH` or `MORT` |
| Sequence number | `00042` | A running count per branch and loan type |

Changing a draft to a different kind of loan issues a new Loan ID from the new type's sequence.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a submitted application be edited?">
    No. Sections lock at submission. The approver can request more information or return it to **Draft**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the loan type after creating the draft?">
    Yes, while it is a **Draft**. The same kind keeps the Loan ID. A different kind creates a new draft with a new Loan ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who approves a loan?">
    Whoever your approval routes say: an approver within their own limit, a committee quorum, or a committee decision entered by its recorder. The approval route card on the loan shows which.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a staff member's loan missing from my list?">
    Staff loans can be confidential. Only their designated handlers, the assignee and the borrower see them. See [Staff and confidential loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is anything ever deleted?">
    No. Every application reaches a final stage and stays on file.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
