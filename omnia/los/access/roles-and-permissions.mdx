Every staff member holds exactly one role in a workspace, and that role decides which pages, queues, and actions they see. A role is a named set of permissions. Roles are set per credit union, so the same person can be a **Credit Officer** in one workspace and an **Adjudicator** in another.

<Note>
  Your **Administrator** assigns roles. If you need something your role does not allow, ask them about a role change.
</Note>

## The seven built-in roles

Every workspace starts with seven roles, one per step of the credit process plus oversight and administration.

<Note>
  On screen the Administrator role is labelled **Application Administrator**, and the Adjudicator's queue is labelled **Credit Approvals**. They are the same role and the same queue.
</Note>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit Officer" icon="file-text">
    Captures loan applications. Creates and updates member records, submits identity checks, adds guarantors and their documents, prepares the loan's documents for signing, and submits the finished application for approval. Sees the loans they captured.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adjudicator" icon="gavel">
    Decides loans. Approves, declines, or asks the Credit Officer for more information. Sees every loan in every branch and claims work from the team queue. Can void a signed document that needs correcting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Securities" icon="shield-check">
    Perfects the security on approved loans. Raises and resolves security requests, records collateral, places holds on pledged member funds, and prepares and records the security documents. Claims work from the team queue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Disbursement" icon="banknote">
    Releases the money. Schedules and executes disbursements, screens payees, updates settlement as payments clear, reverses a disbursement when needed, and keeps the saved-payee list. Claims work from the team queue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit Manager" icon="badge-check">
    Runs the lending operation. Sees every loan, assigns work, approves disbursements that need a second approver, sets disbursement controls, overrides a loan's rate when policy allows, reviews escalated identity checks, and maintains products, loan types, rates, fees, and document templates. Sees analytics and the audit trail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branch Manager" icon="building">
    Oversees one branch. Sees every loan in their branch, assigns work within it, and sees analytics and reports for it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Administrator" icon="settings">
    Owns the workspace. Invites staff and changes roles, manages branding and workspace settings, maintains products, loan types, rates, and fees, and can capture loans end to end. Does not approve loans, review securities, or disburse funds: those steps belong to the roles above.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Your credit union can also have roles of its own, for example a role that only manages staff accounts, a front-desk role that can only look up a member's branch and status, or the senior approvers your credit union designates. Your LOS support team sets these up from your written policy.
</Note>

### Permissions no built-in role holds

Two permissions are switched on only when your credit union's policy calls for them:

- **View confidential loans** lets a role see confidential staff and board loans. Your policy names the roles that handle staff lending. See [Confidential and staff loans](/omnia/los/loans/confidential-and-staff-loans).
- **Manage member identity links** links a member record to a staff login and sets the member's classification. It is granted when your credit union adopts member classifications.

## Changing what a role can do

Each role's permissions are visible to an administrator on **Administration → Users & Roles**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Roles & permissions panel">
    On **Users & Roles**, click **Roles & permissions** to expand it. It reads "What each role can do" across every role in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role">
    Click a role along the top. Each permission group shows **Full access**, **Some capabilities**, or **No access** for that role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a group">
    Click a group, such as **Disbursement controls**, to see each permission in it and whether the role holds it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel is read-only. To add or remove a permission on a role, or to create a role of your own, send the change with your credit union's written approval to your LOS support team. The change applies to everyone in that role the next time they load a page.

## Rules that keep duties separate

Some actions need more than the right permission. These rules stop one person from controlling both sides of a decision.

- **You cannot approve a loan you captured**, when your credit union turns this rule on.
- **You cannot approve a loan whose rate you changed**, when your credit union turns this rule on. Approving a loan whose rate someone else changed is fine.
- **On a committee decision**, the person who captured the loan or changed its rate cannot vote to approve it, and nobody votes twice. See [Approval routes](/omnia/los/workflows/approval-routes).
- **Disbursing and approving a disbursement are separate.** The Disbursement officer releases funds. When a disbursement needs a second approver, only a Credit Manager can approve it, and the officer who scheduled it never can. See [Disbursement controls](/omnia/los/disbursement/controls).
- **The person who disburses cannot be named as the checker** on that loan's disbursement sheet.
- **You cannot void a document you recorded as signed.** Voiding is a supervisor's step. See [Correcting documents](/omnia/los/legal-documents/correcting-documents).
- **Rates and approvals sit with different roles.** The Adjudicator decides the loan; the Credit Manager sets its price.
- **The Administrator does not touch money.** It holds no approval, securities, or disbursement permission.

Loan imports carry no such split. Any role that can import loans can commit a batch, with or without a recorded sign-off, because no money moves. See [Loan migration](/omnia/los/migration/overview).

## When an action is unavailable

Some steps need several things to line up. Submitting a loan, for example, needs **Submit loans**, **Move loans between stages**, and a member whose identity check is **Verified**. Moving a loan into the next stage needs **Move loans between stages** plus the permission for that step, such as **Approve loans** or **Review securities**.

If any piece is missing, the button is hidden or the platform refuses the action with a message saying what is still needed. That is why an action can be unavailable even when your role looks right. Every refused action is recorded in the audit trail.
