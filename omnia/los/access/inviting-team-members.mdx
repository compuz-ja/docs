Staff join your workspace by email invitation. Each invitation carries a role, so the person's access is ready the first time they sign in.

You need the **Manage users and invitations** permission. The **Administrator** holds it, and your credit union may give it to a separate staff-administration role. If you do not see **Administration** in the sidebar, ask your Administrator.

## Sending an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add New Users">
    In the left sidebar, open **Administration** and select **Add New Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter one or more email addresses">
    In the **Send invitations** card, type the work email addresses. Separate several with commas, semicolons, spaces, or new lines. Everyone in the card gets the same role. Check the addresses carefully: each link works only for the address it was sent to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the role">
    Pick the role that matches the job, for example **Credit Officer**, **Adjudicator**, or **Branch Manager**. See [Roles & permissions](/omnia/los/access/roles-and-permissions) if you are unsure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send invitations">
    Each invitation appears in the list below as **Pending**, and a message confirms how many were sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your colleague accepts">
    They get an email with an **Accept invitation** link, valid for **72 hours**. A new user goes to sign-up; someone who already has an account goes to sign-in, then straight into the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your colleague lands in the workspace">
    Once a new user has set a password and verified their email, they land on the dashboard with their role already active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Some addresses are skipped, and the page tells you why: the address is not a valid email, it already belongs to an active user in this workspace, or it already has a pending invitation. Someone you previously suspended or revoked can be invited again, and their old record comes back when they accept.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Invitation links work once and expire after 72 hours. If a link expires, find the invitation on **Add New Users** and click **Resend**.
</Warning>

### If the invitee is signed in as someone else

The link only works for the invited address. Someone signed in under a different email who clicks it sees an email-mismatch page and is not added. Ask them to sign out, or open the link in a session for the invited address.

## Inviting many people from a spreadsheet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import dialog">
    On **Add New Users**, click **Import** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a CSV file">
    The file needs a header row and two columns: an email column headed `email`, `email address`, or `e-mail`, and a role column headed `role` or `role name`. Roles match by name, ignoring case; for the Administrator, both `Application Administrator` and `Administrator` work. You can add optional first name, last name, and branch columns (the branch header can be `branch` or `branch code`). The limit is 500 rows or 1 MB, whichever comes first. Every file is virus-scanned, and spreadsheet formulas in cells are neutralised.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the preview">
    Every row is marked ready, invalid, duplicate, already a user, or already invited. Nothing is sent yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the import">
    Confirm to invite the ready rows. Each becomes a pending invitation, the same as one typed by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can commit up to 5 imports per hour. Every preview, rejection, and import is recorded in the import history and the audit trail.
</Note>

## Tracking invitations

**Add New Users** lists every invitation with its status.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    Sent, not yet accepted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accepted" icon="circle-check">
    The person has joined. They now appear on **Administration → Users & Roles**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Expired" icon="calendar-x">
    The 72 hours ran out. Click **Resend** for a new link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Revoked" icon="lock">
    Cancelled before it was accepted. A revoked invitation cannot be revived; send a new one.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Open the **⋯** menu at the end of a pending or expired invitation for **Resend** and **Revoke**. **Resend** issues a new link and kills the old one, so the invitee must use the latest email. **Revoke** kills the link at once.

## Changing a team member's role

Each person holds one role, so a new role replaces the old one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users & Roles">
    In the sidebar, open **Administration** and select **Users & Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Search by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new role">
    Choose the new role from the dropdown in their row. It saves the moment you pick it and applies on their next page load.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suspending a team member's access

Suspend someone who is on leave or under review. Their loans, actions, and audit history stay exactly as they are.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    On **Administration → Users & Roles**, search for them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Suspend">
    Open the **⋯** menu at the end of their row and click **Suspend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Suspend member**. Their next click in the workspace is blocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To bring them back, open the same menu and click **Reactivate**.

## Revoking a team member's access

Revoke access when someone has left for good. Suspension can be undone; revocation cannot. A revoked person can only return through a new invitation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Revoke access">
    On **Administration → Users & Roles**, open the **⋯** menu on the person's row and click **Revoke access**. It sits below a divider, in red.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason and type their name">
    Enter a reason of at least 20 characters, then type the person's name (or email, if they have no name) exactly as shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Revoke access**. They lose access on their next click and cannot sign back in to this workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Their loans, decisions, and documents still point at them, and the audit trail records who revoked them and why. Their access to any other credit union's workspace is unaffected.

Two revocations are refused: your own ("You cannot revoke your own access. Ask another administrator to do it."), and the last account that can revoke access, so the workspace is never left without an administrator.
