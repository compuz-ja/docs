You sign in to the Loan Origination System with your email address and password. Each credit union has its own workspace with its own web address. Bookmark it so you always land in the right place. If you do not know the address, ask your **Administrator**.

## Signing in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your workspace address">
    Go to the address your administrator gave you. If you open the site without a workspace in the address, you land on the sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address and password">
    Type the email address and password for your account. The password is case-sensitive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your verification code, if you use MFA">
    If multi-factor authentication is on for your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. See [MFA](/omnia/los/access/mfa).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start work">
    You land on your workspace dashboard. The top of the left sidebar shows your credit union's logo and name, the product name, and the release version. Quote that version when you report a problem.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you sign in from a device or network that does not match your other active sessions, you get a new-sign-in alert email with the time, the network address, and the device and browser. Your very first sign-in sends no alert. If the sign-in was you, do nothing. If it was not, change your password straight away under **Profile → Authentication**.
</Note>

## Verifying your email

When your **Administrator** invites you, the invitation link takes you to sign-up if you are new, or to sign-in if you already have an account. A new account gets a verification email. You must click its **Verify email** link before your first sign-in.

The link expires 24 hours after it is sent. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

If you try to sign in before verifying, the page says "Please verify your email first." and offers a **Resend verification email** link. The resend button waits 30 seconds between sends and shows "Resend in Ns" while it counts down.

<Note>
  Joining by invitation does not skip verification. You still sign up and verify your email before your first sign-in.
</Note>

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** below the password field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type your account email and click **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    A reset email arrives if the address has an account. The page shows the same message either way, so nobody can use it to find out which addresses have accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Click **Reset password** in the email and enter a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and include a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number, and a symbol. A strength meter checks each rule as you type. The link is valid for **1 hour**. If it has expired, request a new one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching between credit unions

If you work for more than one credit union on the platform, you can switch workspaces without signing out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner. **Switch workspace** appears only when you have active access to more than one workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace">
    Each workspace shows as a card with the credit union's name and your role there. Click the one you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work in the new workspace">
    Your role is set separately in each workspace, so your menus and queues may look different.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The picker lists only workspaces where your access is active. If one you expect is missing, contact that credit union's **Administrator**.
</Note>

## When your access is paused

Access is paused per workspace, not per account. If one credit union suspends your access, you can still use any other workspace where your access is active.

When you open a workspace where your access is paused, you see an **Access paused** page that reads "Your access to this workspace is paused." Contact that credit union's **Administrator** to restore it.

<Warning>
  Never share your sign-in with a colleague. Each person needs their own account. A shared account makes it impossible to tell from the audit trail who approved, submitted, or changed a loan.
</Warning>
