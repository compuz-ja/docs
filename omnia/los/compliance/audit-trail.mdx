The audit trail is a permanent, tamper-evident record of every action taken in your workspace. Nobody can edit or delete an entry. It is the record you rely on for your regulator's questions, internal investigations, and disputes.

## What is recorded

Recording is always on. There is nothing to set up.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Loans" icon="file-text">
    Every step of every loan: submission, approval decisions and their reasons, security requests, document signing, and disbursement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Members and documents" icon="user">
    Every change to a member record, every document uploaded or removed, every identity check, and every time someone reveals a member's identity numbers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sign-in and access" icon="lock">
    Sign-ins, sign-outs, failed sign-ins, and every action refused because the person lacked permission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="settings">
    Every change to rates, fees, products, roles, branding, and other settings, with who made it and the value before.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who did what

Each entry captures the person exactly as they were at that moment:

- **Who**: the staff member, shown with their name and email when you open the entry.
- **Their role at the time**, so a later role change does not rewrite history.
- **Their permissions at the time.**
- **Where from**: the network address and the browser or device.
- **How they signed in**, and whether multi-factor authentication was used for that session.
- **Reference numbers** that tie the entry to related activity, for your support team.

Actions the platform takes on its own, such as scheduled checks, are marked **System**.

## Who can open the audit trail

Staff with **View reports** see the whole workspace's trail. In the built-in roles that is the **Administrator**, **Credit Manager**, and **Branch Manager**. A role your credit union sets up with **View branch loans** but not **View reports** sees only its own branch's entries. The Credit Officer, Adjudicator, Securities, and Disbursement roles cannot open it.

## Searching the audit trail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Audit Log">
    In the sidebar, under **Administration**, click **Audit Log**. The page is titled **Audit trail** and shows the newest entries first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Use the filter bar: **Event**, **Resource**, **Actor**, **Resource ID**, and **Date range**. Tick **Show system & integrity** to include the platform's own activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an entry">
    Click a row to see the person, their role and network address, the decision reason for approval decisions, and the full detail of what changed. **Copy JSON** copies that detail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Everything that happened to one loan or member">
  Pick the **Resource**, for example a loan application or a member, and paste its ID into **Resource ID**. You get every event that touched that record in order: each step, decision, document, and access. Add a **Date range** to cover the life of the application.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Everything one staff member did">
  Type the person's name or email in **Actor**. You get every action they took in the date range. Use this when you suspect someone else used their sign-in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="One kind of action">
  Pick an **Event**, such as approval decisions, disbursements, or refused actions. The list shows every kind of event the platform records, including ones that have not happened in your workspace yet, so you can confirm a negative.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A period of time">
  Set the **Date range** and combine it with the other filters. For example, every approval decision in the last calendar year.
</Accordion>

## Quick export

To take what you are looking at into a spreadsheet, apply your filters and export from the toolbar as **CSV** (opens cleanly in Excel and Sheets) or **JSON** (with the full detail of each entry).

The quick export is for day-to-day work, not for your regulator:

- It holds at most **1,000 rows**. A larger view is cut off at 1,000, and the download says so.
- It is **not signed**, so nobody can prove later that it was not edited.
- It contains only entries you can already see.

For a record your regulator or auditor can check for themselves, generate a [compliance export](/omnia/los/compliance/compliance-export). Every quick export is itself recorded in the audit trail.

## Why the trail can be trusted

- **Entries cannot be changed.** The database itself refuses any edit or deletion. No screen, administrator, or support action can alter an entry.
- **Every entry is sealed to the one before it.** Changing, removing, or reordering any entry breaks the seal from that point on.
- **The platform checks the seals every hour** and raises an alert on any break, saying exactly where it is.
- **Signed checkpoints are kept outside the database.** The latest seal is signed regularly and copied each day to separate locked storage that cannot be altered or deleted until the retention period ends. Even someone with direct access to the database could not rewrite history without the checkpoints showing it.

To hand over a record that can be verified independently, use the [compliance export](/omnia/los/compliance/compliance-export).
