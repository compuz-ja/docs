A compliance export is a signed ZIP file of audit records that your regulator or auditor can check for themselves, offline, without access to your system. It answers questions like _"every loan decision over $5 million last year, with the reason for each"_ in minutes.

## Who can generate one

Staff with **View reports**: in the built-in roles, the **Administrator**, **Credit Manager**, and **Branch Manager**. The bundle only ever contains your own workspace's records.

<Note>
  This is not the [quick export](/omnia/los/compliance/audit-trail#quick-export) on the same page. The quick export is an unsigned file of up to 1,000 rows for your own use. The compliance export is signed, complete for the filters you chose, and kept in locked storage.
</Note>

## Generating a bundle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Audit Log">
    In the sidebar, under **Administration**, click **Audit Log**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter to the records you need">
    Use the filters to set what goes in. For an examination of loan decisions, for example, set **Event** to approval decisions and **Date range** to the year under review. The bundle holds every entry that matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export bundle">
    In the toolbar, click **Export bundle**. The **Compliance export** dialog opens, marked **Beta**. Check the filters.

    To include only large loans, open **Advanced** and enter an amount in **Restrict to loan decisions at or above**, for example `5,000,000`. Only loans whose requested amount is at or above it are included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate bundle">
    It takes a few seconds. The dialog then shows the **Export ID**, the number of records, the start of the **Manifest signature** for reference, and when the link expires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and hand it over">
    Click **Download** to save the ZIP file. Give it to your examiner or auditor. They need no access to your system to check it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The download link expires **1 hour** after the bundle is made. Download it straight away and store it securely. If the link expires, generate a new bundle; the records are not lost.
</Warning>

## What is in the bundle

The ZIP holds four files.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="manifest.json" icon="shield">
    The signed index. It lists the other three files with a fingerprint of each, and carries the platform's digital signature plus the key needed to check it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="audit-entries.csv" icon="table">
    The records as a spreadsheet, one row per entry, in a fixed column order. Good for searching and spot checks in Excel. It leaves out the full detail of each entry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="audit-entries.jsonl" icon="code">
    The complete records, one per line, including the full detail of each event and the reason recorded for each decision.
  </Card>
  <Card title="chain-proof.json" icon="link">
    Proof that the records are one unbroken, unaltered stretch of your audit trail, with nothing removed from the middle.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each row records what happened, to which loan or member, who did it, their role and permissions at the time, the network address they used, how they signed in, the decision reason where there is one, and when.

<Note>
  If nothing matches your filters, you still get a complete, signed bundle with zero records. That is evidence too: it proves to an examiner that the answer to their question is "none".
</Note>

## How an examiner verifies a bundle

Verification needs only the ZIP file and standard tools. It confirms four things:

1. **The files are complete.** The four files are all there and in the expected form.
2. **Nothing was edited.** Each file still matches the fingerprint recorded in the signed index. A single changed character fails this check.
3. **The index is genuine.** The signature on the index checks out against the key inside it, so the index came from the platform and was not altered.
4. **No records are missing or rearranged.** Each record links to the one before it, and the last link matches the proof file.

If any check fails, the bundle was changed after it was made. Your LOS support team can give your examiner the technical verification procedure.

## How long bundles are kept

Every bundle is also stored in locked storage for seven years. The lock is set when the bundle is made and cannot be shortened. Nobody, administrators included, can delete a bundle early.

Your downloaded copy is yours to keep under your own retention policy.

<Note>
  Generating a bundle is itself recorded in the audit trail: who made it, when, which filters they used, and how many records it held. So the hand-over is auditable too.
</Note>
