The Loan Origination System protects member data the way a regulated lender needs. Sensitive identity numbers are encrypted before they are stored. Each credit union's data is kept apart from every other's. Every change, and every refused attempt, is recorded in the [audit trail](/omnia/los/compliance/audit-trail).

## Your workspace is private

Each credit union works in its own **workspace**. Nobody in another credit union can see your data, whatever their role.

The separation is enforced several times over: by the application, by the database itself, and by encryption, because each credit union's data is encrypted with its own key. It is always on and cannot be turned off. There is nothing for you to set.

Screens show these masked. Only staff with **View member personal details** can reveal one, and every reveal is recorded in the audit trail. The Core Banking System account number shows only its last four digits; the Loan Disbursement Sheet, the one document that prints it, shows only the last five. See [Loan Disbursement Sheet](/omnia/los/legal-documents/disbursement-sheet#the-member-account-stays-masked).

The same protection covers guarantors' identity numbers, the payee account number on each disbursement, and the connection details your credit union stores for outside services.

None of these can be exported in bulk through any report.

## Documents

Member documents, such as ID scans, payslips, and PDFs, are kept in secure file storage, apart from the database. To open one, a staff member must:

1. Be signed in, with active access to your workspace.
2. Hold **View member documents**.
3. Be opening a document that belongs to your credit union.

Every upload and removal is recorded in the audit trail with who did it and when.

## Operational logs and the audit trail

The platform keeps two kinds of record, and they treat personal data differently.

| Record | Personal data | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational logs**, for keeping the service running | Removed automatically before anything is written | Your LOS support team |
| **Audit trail**, your legal record | Kept in full | Staff with **View reports**: in the built-in roles, the Administrator, Credit Manager, and Branch Manager |

Your regulator gets the audit trail's detail through a signed [compliance export](/omnia/los/compliance/compliance-export), not through access to your system.

## Access controls

<Accordion title="Sign-in and multi-factor authentication">
  Everyone signs in with an email and password. There is no anonymous access.

  Every user can turn on multi-factor authentication under **Profile → Authentication**, and every administrator and manager should. Each audit trail entry records whether it was used, so you can check that sensitive actions came from properly signed-in staff. See [Set up multi-factor authentication](/omnia/los/access/mfa).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Roles and permissions">
  What each person can see and do comes from their role. Nobody can go beyond it, and every refused attempt is recorded in the audit trail. See [Roles and permissions](/omnia/los/access/roles-and-permissions).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sessions">
  Sign-in sessions are protected against theft by other websites and scripts, and are only ever sent over an encrypted connection. A forged request from another site cannot act on behalf of a signed-in user.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Suspending access">
  An administrator can suspend any user at once. Their next click in the workspace is refused, so you can act immediately on a suspected compromise or a departure. See [Inviting team members](/omnia/los/access/inviting-team-members#suspending-a-team-members-access).
</Accordion>

## Detecting misuse

Because everything is recorded and the audit trail cannot be altered, misuse can be found. You can:

- List everything one staff member did in a given period.
- Find every record viewed or changed during a suspected incident.
- Prove the audit trail is intact by generating a [compliance export](/omnia/los/compliance/compliance-export) and verifying it.

If anyone altered or deleted an audit entry after the fact, the hourly integrity check would flag it, and so would any examiner checking an export.

<Note>
  There is no back door. No database console, bulk download, or data feed skips the role and permission checks. Every read of a loan, document, or audit entry goes through them.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Make sure every Administrator and Credit Manager has turned on multi-factor authentication. Those accounts can see the full audit trail and generate compliance exports. Each person turns it on for themselves; an administrator cannot do it for them, or reset it.
</Tip>
