OMNIA is the banking value leg of the CompuZign portfolio. Core banking and the digital services around it.

## What is in it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Core banking" icon="landmark">
    Delivered in partnership with Profile Software Finuevo. Accounts, deposits, lending and product management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Loan Origination System" icon="file-text" href="/omnia/los/introduction">
    Capture, adjudicate, secure, disburse and file loans in one audited workflow, with KYC and a verifiable audit trail.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Onboarding" icon="user-plus">
    Member onboarding and identity verification.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile and online banking" icon="smartphone">
    Member channels: balances, payments, applications and service requests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Core Banking Migration as a Service" icon="move-right">
    Moving an institution off a legacy core, with the cutover rehearsed against the second site before the production event.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments and cards" icon="credit-card">
    Payment rails and card services.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
