iLoan automatically sends email notifications when a loan changes state. Notifications are scoped to the person responsible for the file — if a loan is assigned to a specific staff member, only that person is notified. If the loan has no owner yet, the entire team for that stage receives the notice so nothing falls through the cracks.

## How routing works

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Assigned loan" icon="user-check">
    Only the assigned staff member receives the notification. Once you claim or are assigned a file, you are the single point of contact for all stage events on that loan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unassigned loan" icon="users">
    The whole team for that stage receives the notification — every Adjudicator, every Securities Reviewer, or every Disbursement Officer as appropriate. This ensures that unowned work is never silently missed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Claiming a loan" icon="hand-pointer">
    Claiming a loan for yourself does **not** trigger an email. Claiming is a self-service action; only explicit assignment by a Credit Manager or Lead sends an "assigned to you" notification.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Notification event table

| Event | Recipient(s) |
|---|---|
| Loan submitted — routes to Adjudication | Assigned adjudicator, or all adjudicators if unassigned |
| Loan assigned to a staff member | The newly assigned staff member |
| Loan approved | Credit Officer who originated the application |
| Loan declined | Credit Officer + Member (if member email notifications are enabled by your Tenant Administrator) |
| Information requested | Credit Officer |
| Loan returned to Draft (full rebuild) | Credit Officer |
| Loan stepped back to a previous stage | Destination team + Credit Officer (FYI) |
| Security request created | Credit Officer |
| Security request documents submitted by officer | Assigned Securities reviewer, or all Securities reviewers if unassigned |
| Security request resolved | Credit Officer |
| Security request rejected | Credit Officer |
| Ready for Disbursement | Assigned Disbursement Officer (or all Disbursement Officers if unassigned) + Credit Officer (FYI) |
| Disbursement scheduled | Assigned Disbursement Officer, or all Disbursement Officers if unassigned |
| Disbursement executed | Credit Officer + Member (if member email notifications are enabled by your Tenant Administrator) |
| Loan filed | Credit Officer |
| Loan cancelled | Assigned owner + Credit Officer (or team fan-out if unassigned) |

## Member notifications

Emails to borrowers are controlled by a setting your Tenant Administrator manages. By default, member notifications are **off**. When enabled, members receive emails for two events only:

- **Loan declined** — an adverse action notice informing them the application was unsuccessful
- **Disbursement executed** — a confirmation that funds have been released

Members never receive internal workflow notifications (such as Information Requested or Ready for Disbursement) — those are staff-facing events.

<Note>
Your credit union's name and branding, as configured by your Tenant Administrator, are included in every email notification sent from iLoan — to staff and to members.
</Note>

## Assignment notifications

When a Credit Manager or Lead uses the Assignment panel to route a loan to a specific person, that person receives a **"A loan needs your attention"** email immediately. This is separate from the event-based notifications in the table above.

Assignment notifications fire for:
- Direct assignment by a manager or lead (`loans.assign` capability)
- Reassignment from one person to another

They do **not** fire when you claim a loan yourself. Self-claiming is logged in the audit trail but does not generate an email.

## Noise reduction — scoped vs. broadcast

iLoan's notification system is designed to reduce inbox noise as loan ownership is established. The comparison below shows the difference between a fully unassigned pipeline and one where loans are actively assigned:

| Event | Unassigned (worst case) | Assigned (scoped) |
|---|---|---|
| Loan routed to Adjudication | Every adjudicator in the tenant | The one assigned adjudicator |
| Security request documents submitted | Every Securities reviewer | The one assigned reviewer |
| Ready for Disbursement / scheduled | Every Disbursement Officer | The one assigned officer |
| Loan filed | Credit Officer + all Credit Managers + all Branch Managers | Credit Officer only |

Assigning loans promptly — either through the Assignment panel or by claiming — keeps your team's inboxes manageable and makes it clear who is accountable for each file.

<Tip>
If you are not receiving notifications you expect, check two things: (1) confirm with your Tenant Administrator that your role is correctly configured and includes the right capabilities; and (2) check whether the loan is assigned to someone else — if it is, that person is the only one receiving stage notifications for that file.
</Tip>
